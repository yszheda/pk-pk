A few weeks ago [we looked](https://www.construction-physics.com/p/information-and-technological-evolution) at a [simulation of technological evolution](https://sites.santafe.edu/~wbarthur/Papers/AP-Complexity.pdf) by economist Brian Arthur, in which he was able to start with simple building blocks (such as a NAND gate) and evolve surprisingly complex circuits (such as a 12-way AND gate or a 4-bit adder) by randomly combining increasingly useful existing components. We analyzed this as a way of simplifying a search problem: by using existing, working components as modules that can be combined, a few at a time, into more complex modules, and then combining *those* into even more complex modules, many unpromising and time-consuming branches of the search tree are screened off, and the simulation can find useful technologies amidst an enormous branching set of possibilities.几周前， [我们研究了](https://www.construction-physics.com/p/information-and-technological-evolution) 经济学家布莱恩·阿瑟所做的一项 [技术进化模拟](https://sites.santafe.edu/~wbarthur/Papers/AP-Complexity.pdf) 。在该模拟中，他从简单的构建模块（如与非门）出发，通过随机组合日益实用的现有组件，进化出了令人惊叹的复杂电路（如12路与门或4位加法器）。我们将此视为一种简化搜索问题的方法：通过将现有的可用组件作为模块，每次少量组合成更复杂的模块，再将 *这些* 模块组合成更复杂的模块，搜索树中许多前景渺茫且耗时的分支被筛选掉，从而使模拟能在庞大的分支可能性中找到实用的技术。

Real human technology is, of course, not generated by randomly combining components together and seeing if they do anything useful; the randomness in these simulations is just a way to see how easy or hard it is to create new technologies under different conditions. But *biological technology* — the huge panoply of lifeforms that exist on earth, from microscopic single-celled organisms to [whales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_whale) the size of a 737 — is also generated by randomness. Evolution builds biological technology bit by bit by harvesting the fruits of genetic variation, often caused by random mutation, preferentially selecting the most fit organisms to propagate their genes into the future. Over billions of years, this process can generate astoundingly complex biological systems.当然，真正的人类技术并非通过随意组合组件并观察其是否有用而产生；这些模拟中的随机性只是为了探究在不同条件下研发新技术的难易程度。但 *生物技术* ——即地球上存在的种类繁多的生命形态，从微小的单细胞生物到体型堪比737客机的 [鲸鱼](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_whale) ——同样由随机性驱动。进化通过积累基因变异的成果逐步构建生物技术，而基因变异往往由随机突变引发，进化会优先选择最适应环境的生物体，使其基因得以延续。历经数十亿年，这一过程能够演化出极其复杂的生物系统。

What’s interesting is that biological evolution uses a very similar trick to Arthur’s circuit simulation. By leveraging modularity at the genetic level, populations of organisms can increase the rate that useful genetic variants spread through the population, effectively increasing their rate of information acquisition. Sexual reproduction, along with other ways of sharing genetic material like horizontal gene transfer, is essentially a mechanism for doing this. We can show this with some simple simulations.有趣的是，生物进化采用的技巧与亚瑟的电路模拟非常相似。通过利用基因层面的模块化特性，生物种群能够提高有益遗传变异在种群中传播的速度，从而有效提升其获取信息的速率。有性生殖以及水平基因转移等其他遗传物质共享方式，本质上都是实现这一目标的机制。我们可以通过一些简单的模拟来验证这一点。

#### Evolution and reproductive strategies进化与繁殖策略

The simplest way for an organism to reproduce is asexual reproduction, where a parent produces a child that’s a genetic copy of itself. Simple single-celled organisms, for instance, reproduce by [cellular fission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fission_\(biology\)), dividing into two or more “children” that each have the same genes as the original parent.生物体最简单的繁殖方式是无性繁殖，即亲代产生一个基因层面的自身复制品作为子代。例如，简单的单细胞生物通过 [细胞分裂](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fission_\(biology\)) 进行繁殖，分裂成两个或多个“子代”，每个子代都与亲代拥有相同的基因。

But children won’t necessarily be identical copies of their parents. Due to genetic mutation, some genes might get randomly altered during the fission process, producing children with slightly different genes. In some cases, these mutations might be useful, giving additional functionality such as [antibiotic resistance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7568785/) and thus better odds of surviving and reproducing. Because of their contribution to the organism’s fitness, over time the useful mutations will become more and more common in the population.但子代不一定会和亲代完全相同。由于基因突变，一些基因在分裂过程中可能会发生随机改变，使得子代的基因存在细微差异。在某些情况下，这些突变可能是有益的，能赋予子代额外的功能，比如 [抗生素抗性](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7568785/) ，从而提高其存活和繁殖的几率。由于这些有益突变对生物体的生存能力有帮助，随着时间的推移，它们在种群中的出现频率会越来越高。

We can demonstrate this with a simple simulation. In our simulation, we start with a population of 100 creatures, each of which has a genome of 200 individual genes. A gene can either be a 1 (the “good” version of the gene) or a 0 (the “bad” version of the gene). The initial population is random, with each creature having roughly a 50-50 mix of good and bad genes. Each iteration of the simulation, each creature produces two children. A child copies the genes of its parent, but due to mutation each gene has a 0.2% chance of being flipped, going from a 1 to a 0 or vice versa. The 100 most fit children (where fitness is just the sum of each gene value, since 1 is the “good” version of the gene in our simplified model) are selected to continue the next generation, and the cycle repeats. This is a simplification compared to how evolution actually functions — for one, it treats genes as contributing to fitness independently, ignoring the fact that the fitness value of one gene often depend on other genes — but it’s enough to show some of the dynamics at work.我们可以通过一个简单的模拟来证明这一点。在我们的模拟中，初始有100个生物，每个生物都拥有由200个独立基因组成的基因组。一个基因可以是1（基因的“优良”版本）或0（基因的“劣质”版本）。初始种群是随机的，每个生物的基因中优良与劣质基因的比例大致各占50%。在模拟的每一轮中，每个生物都会产生两个后代。后代会复制亲代的基因，但由于突变，每个基因有0.2%的概率发生翻转，从1变为0或从0变为1。我们会选择100个最适应的后代（适应度仅为每个基因值的总和，因为在我们的简化模型中1代表基因的“优良”版本）来繁衍下一代，如此循环往复。与实际的进化机制相比，这是一种简化——首先，它将基因视为独立影响适应度的因素，忽略了一个基因的适应度值往往取决于其他基因这一事实——但它足以展示进化过程中的一些动态规律。

When we run this simulation, the proportion of “good” genes in the population steadily rises over time as more-fit offspring outcompete less-fit offspring. Depending on the mutation rate, the population may eventually reach maximum possible fitness of 200, or plateau at some level below it.当我们运行这个模拟程序时，随着适应性更强的后代比适应性较弱的后代更具竞争力，种群中“优良”基因的比例会随着时间稳步上升。根据突变率的不同，种群最终可能达到200的最大可能适应度，也可能在低于该数值的某个水平上趋于稳定。

The problem with this strategy — producing children that are noisy copies of a single parent, and relying purely on random mutation as a source of genetic variation — is that once you’re at above-average fitness, mutations are likely to be bad on average. If a genome has more 1s than 0s, a random change will be more likely to change a 1 to a 0 than a 0 to a 1. Thus for parents of above-average fitness, their children will on average have lower fitness.这种策略的问题在于——培育出的子代只是单一亲本的“噪音复制品”，且完全依赖随机突变作为遗传变异的来源——一旦个体的适应度高于平均水平，突变平均而言很可能是有害的。如果一个基因组中1的数量多于0，随机改变将更有可能把1变成0，而非把0变成1。因此，对于适应度高于平均水平的亲本来说，它们的子代平均而言适应度会更低。

Because mutation is random, there will nonetheless be variation, and some children will end up with higher fitness than their parents. And because selection eliminates the least fit each iteration, the pool of selected children will have higher average fitness than their parents, allowing average fitness to increase over time. But mutation reducing average fitness drags down this process.由于突变是随机发生的，种群中依然会出现变异，部分子代的适应度最终会高于亲代。而选择会在每一轮淘汰适应度最低的个体，因此被选中的子代群体的平均适应度会高于亲代，使得整体的平均适应度随时间提升。但突变会降低平均适应度，从而拖慢这一进程。

You can see this in the graph below, which shows a simulation with slightly different parameters (a genome length of 1000 and a mutation rate of 2%) to more easily see the trends. The top graph shows the distribution of population fitness at generation 50, and the second graph shows the distribution of the population’s children prior to selection. You can see that, thanks to mutation, average fitness has dropped, though due to randomness some proportion of the children have lucked into getting higher fitness. The last graph shows the children after the top half of the distribution has been selected. Average fitness rises, and is now above the initial population, though just barely.你可以从下方的图表中看到这一点，该图表展示了一组参数略有调整的模拟结果（基因组长度为1000，突变率为2%），以便更清晰地观察趋势。第一张图表显示了第50代时种群适应度的分布情况，第二张图表则展示了选择前种群后代的适应度分布。可以看到，受突变影响，平均适应度有所下降，不过由于随机因素，部分后代恰好获得了更高的适应度。最后一张图表展示了选择了适应度分布前50%的后代后的情况，此时平均适应度有所上升，且虽仅微幅提升，但已高于初始种群的水平。

Now let’s look at a simulation of a different reproductive strategy: sexual reproduction, where children get their genes from two parents rather than just one. In this simulation, we still have a population of 100 creatures with genomes of 200 genes, each of which can either be a 0 or a 1. But now children have two parents, and in each iteration members of the population are paired up randomly and each pair produces four children. Children get their genes from both parents with each gene having a 50% chance of coming from a particular parent. The top 100 most fit children are then selected for the next generation, and the iteration continues. In this simulation, there is no mutation, so genetic variation entirely comes from reshuffling the genes of the parents.现在我们来模拟一种不同的繁殖策略：有性生殖，在这种方式下，子代的基因来自双亲而非单亲。在本次模拟中，我们仍有100个生物种群，每个生物的基因组包含200个基因，每个基因只能是0或1。但此时子代拥有两个亲本，在每一次迭代中，种群成员会随机配对，每对亲本产生四个子代。子代从双亲处获取基因，每个基因有50%的概率来自某一特定亲本。随后，适应度最高的100个子代会被选中进入下一代，迭代过程持续进行。本次模拟不存在突变，因此基因变异完全源于亲代基因的重新组合。

Like the previous simulation, the population gradually reaches maximum fitness. But sexual reproduction gets there much faster. With asexual reproduction, after 200 generations the population was around an average fitness of 187. With sexual reproduction, the population average reached the fitness maximum of 200 in just 33 generations.与之前的模拟一样，种群会逐渐达到最大适应度。但有性生殖能以快得多的速度实现这一点。在无性生殖的情况下，经过200代后，种群的平均适应度约为187。而在有性生殖的情况下，种群平均适应度仅在33代内就达到了200的最大适应度。

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!1_dF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6cd3ef70-1934-4d15-ab5d-082ef8a21052_988x394.png)

The key is that sexual reproduction introduces genetic variation without reducing average fitness. Since children are a random combination of their parents’ genes, on average they’ll have the same fitness as their parents (with some randomly having higher fitness, and others randomly having lower fitness). When the most-fit children are selected for the next generation, this is taking the top half of a distribution with a much higher average than the distribution of children in the asexual simulation. Average fitness thus rises much faster.关键在于有性生殖在不降低平均适应度的情况下引入了遗传变异。由于子代是父母基因的随机组合，平均而言，它们的适应度与父母相同（部分子代的适应度随机更高，另一部分则随机更低）。当适应度最高的子代被选入下一代时，这相当于从一个平均值远高于无性生殖模拟中子代分布的分布中选取了前半部分。因此，平均适应度的提升速度要快得多。

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!NNP2!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F349f0d2c-b558-4fc7-84a6-35027377c35d_1080x1080.png)

If you work out the math (or, as I did, simply [read the math that someone else worked out](https://www.inference.org.uk/itprnn/book.pdf)), in an asexual population the rate of fitness increase is 1/(8\*f), where f is the *differential normalized fitness*. (The normalized fitness of a population is the average fraction of good genes in that population; so a population where, on average, a member has 150 good genes in a genome of 200 would have a normalized fitness of 0.75. Differential normalized fitness is the normalized fitness of the population minus 0.5, the normalized fitness of a randomly generated population.) Early on, fitness of a population can increase quickly, but the rate soon drops below an increase of 1 unit of fitness per generation (one gene flipped from a 0 to a 1 on average). As a population gets closer to maximum possible fitness, the rate of fitness increase approaches 0.25 (flipping one gene, on average, from a 0 to a 1 every four generations).如果你计算一下相关数学（或者像我一样，直接 [阅读他人计算好的数学结果](https://www.inference.org.uk/itprnn/book.pdf) ），在无性繁殖的种群中，适合度的提升速率为1/(8\*f)，其中f是 *差异标准化适合度* 。（一个种群的标准化适合度是指该种群中优良基因的平均占比；例如，一个种群的个体平均在200个基因的基因组中拥有150个优良基因，那么其标准化适合度为0.75。差异标准化适合度是指种群的标准化适合度减去0.5，也就是随机生成种群的标准化适合度。）在初期，种群的适合度会快速提升，但很快这一速率就会降至每代1个适合度单位以下（平均每代有1个基因从0突变为1）。随着种群逐渐接近最大可能适合度，适合度的提升速率会趋近于0.25（平均每四代有1个基因从0突变为1）。

With sexual reproduction, on the other hand, the rate of fitness increase turns out to be much higher: it’s proportional to the square root of the length of the genome.而在有性生殖的情况下，适合度的提升速率要高得多：它与基因组长度的平方根成正比。

#### The informational power of genetic recombination基因重组的信息能力

One way to think about why sexual reproduction is so powerful is to look at lineages of descent. Say that one of the members of our asexually reproducing population stumbles across a new, useful mutation. Because genes are passed from one parent to one child, the only way this gene can spread throughout the population (in the absence of some other member of the population also stumbling across it) is if the children of whoever has it outcompete the children of everyone else. In this scenario, the population eventually ends up consisting entirely of descendants of one particular member of the population — as a necessary condition of this spread, every other genetic lineage (along with whatever useful mutations they might have stumbled across) gets wiped out.理解有性生殖为何如此强大的一个角度是审视其遗传谱系。假设我们这个无性繁殖种群中的某个个体偶然出现了一个新的有益突变。由于基因是由亲代传递给子代的，在没有其他个体也偶然出现相同突变的情况下，这一基因想要在种群中传播开来，唯一的办法就是拥有该突变的个体，其后代要比其他所有个体的后代更具生存竞争优势。在这种情况下，种群最终会完全由该特定个体的后代构成——而这一传播的必要条件是，其他所有遗传谱系（以及它们各自偶然出现的有益突变）都会被淘汰。

We can see this in our simulation results. The chart below assigns each member of the initial population, and their children, a unique color. The simulation starts out with 100 different colors (one for each member of the population), but this quickly gets winnowed down to a much smaller number. After a few generations, the population is one uniform color, all descendents of one particular member of the initial population. (This chart is from one particular simulation run, but repeated runs will show the same behavior.) 我们可以从模拟结果中清楚地看到这一点。下方的图表为初始种群中的每个成员及其后代分配了唯一的颜色。模拟开始时有100种不同的颜色（对应种群中的每个成员），但很快就会大幅减少，只剩下极少数。经过几代之后，整个种群会变成单一颜色，即初始种群中某一个特定成员的所有后代。（该图表来自一次特定的模拟运行，但重复运行会呈现出相同的规律。）

In the chart below, the population at generation 48 are all descendants of one particular member of the population who lived in generation 25. At generation 80, they’re all descendants of one particular member alive from generation 48.每当种群繁衍至所有个体均源自单一祖先这一节点时，我们重新进行颜色编码，会发现这一情况会反复出现。在下方的图表中，第48代的种群全部是第25代中某一个体的后代；而到第80代时，它们又全部是第48代中某一存活个体的后代。

In evolutionary biology, this phenomenon is known as “ [clonal interference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clonal_interference) ”: if two different beneficial mutations arise in different members of the population of the same generation they can’t be shared and so they end up competing against each other, and one beneficial mutation ultimately gets wiped out.在进化生物学中，这一现象被称为“ [克隆干扰](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clonal_interference) ”：如果同一代种群的不同个体中出现了两种不同的有益突变，这些突变无法被共享，最终会相互竞争，而其中一种有益突变最终会被淘汰。

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!6p3i!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa9569259-0f5d-4251-bc0a-1f39000bf530_1920x1325.png)

Image of clonal interference via wikipedia. In the bottom image of an asexually reproducing population, beneficial mutations “B” and “A” appear in different lineages, but then “B” is wiped out, only reoccuring later through mutation and subsequently spreading through the population. In the top representation of a sexually reproducing population, B and A independently arise but can quickly be shared, spreading through the population much faster. 维基百科上关于克隆干扰的图示。在无性繁殖种群的下方图示中，有益突变“B”和“A”出现在不同的谱系中，但随后“B”被淘汰，之后仅能通过突变再次出现并在种群中传播。而在有性繁殖种群的上方图示中，B和A虽独立产生，却能迅速相互共享，在种群中的传播速度要快得多。

With a sexually reproducing population, on the other hand, useful mutations can be shared much more easily. In an asexual population, a member has one parent, one grandparent, one great-grandparent, and so on. But in a sexual population, a member has two parents, four grandparents, eight great-grandparents, etc. Beneficial variation from earlier generations can spread much more easily.另一方面，对于有性繁殖的种群，有益突变的共享要容易得多。在无性繁殖的种群中，一个个体有一个亲本、一个祖父母、一个曾祖父母，以此类推。但在有性繁殖的种群中，一个个体有两个亲本、四个祖父母、八个曾祖父母，依此类推。来自前代的有益变异能够更轻松地传播开来。

We can see this in the graph below, which shows the proportion of genes of the original members of a sexually reproducing population represented in the gene pool at any given time. We can see that the proportion stays very high: genes from almost 75% of the original population are found in the population after 34 generations. In the asexual population, this was 1% (and would be even lower in larger populations, since it’s just 1/total starting population).我们可以从下方的图表中看到这一点，该图表展示了有性繁殖种群原始成员的基因在任意给定时间点在基因库中所占的比例。我们能看到这一比例始终保持在很高的水平：34代之后，原始种群中近75%个体的基因仍存在于种群中。而在无性繁殖种群中，这一比例仅为1%（且在种群规模更大的情况下会更低，因为该比例仅为1/初始种群总数）。

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!kSWF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff28c6749-ce8b-4bb3-805f-677f6b6e9dd0_993x791.png)

We previously noted that Brian Arthur’s circuit simulation took advantage of modularity, finding useful subcomponents, locking in their designs, and then using those to build more complex technologies. Once the simulation finds a 3-way AND gate, it can use that to make a 4-way AND gate, which it can use to make a 5-way AND gate. We likewise noted that if you’re trying to build an 8-bit adder by randomly combining NAND gates together, it’s vastly easier if you can add one NAND gate at a time and verify you’re correct than if you have to guess all 68 gates at once.我们之前指出过，布莱恩·阿瑟的电路模拟利用了模块化特性：找到有用的子组件，确定其设计方案，然后借助这些子组件构建更复杂的技术。一旦模拟程序找到一个三输入与门，它就能用该门电路构建四输入与门，进而用四输入与门制作五输入与门。我们同样注意到，若要通过随机组合与非门来构建一个8位加法器，相较于一次性猜测全部68个门电路，每次只添加一个与非门并验证其正确性的方法要容易得多。

You can think of this like someone solving a combination lock. A lock with a five-digit combination, with 100 possible values for each digit, has 100^5 = 10 billion possible combinations. Trying combinations one by one would take forever.你可以把这想象成有人在解密码锁。一个五位数密码锁，每个数位有100种可能的数值，总共有100的5次方，也就是100亿种组合。一个一个去试密码会花上无穷的时间。

Technological modularity is like being a skilled lockpick that can check whether each individual digit attempted on a combination is correct (maybe by listening carefully you can hear a telltale “click” when the dial is in the right spot). Now instead of searching over 10 billion possible combinations, you’re doing five searches over 100 possible values each, or 500 possibilities total. The space of possible options that must be considered is vastly reduced.技术模块化就好比一名技艺精湛的开锁匠，他可以逐一检验密码锁上尝试的每一个数字是否正确（或许只要仔细聆听，就能在拨盘转到正确位置时听到明显的“咔哒”声）。如今，你无需排查超过100亿种可能的组合，而是进行5次排查，每次只需考虑100个可能的数值，总共也就500种可能性。需要考量的潜在选项范围被大幅缩减了。

Sexual reproduction is, as I understand it, doing something similar: by letting genes from two parents be combined to form children, it effectively lets the fitness of each gene be tested independently, turning the search from something like “find the best 200-gene genome” to something closer to 200 parallel “find the best gene at this location” searches. In our combination lock analogy, the modular circuit simulation is sort of like turning the dial until you hear a “click,” which indicates that the given number is correct. Sexual reproduction is more like trying a bunch of different random combinations, getting back a score for “how close this combination is to being solved,” and using that to infer which “dials” are correct. The search space is correspondingly greatly narrowed, and the search proceeds much faster.据我理解，有性生殖的原理与此类似：通过将双亲的基因结合形成子代，它能有效测试每个基因的适合度，将原本类似“寻找最优的200个基因组成的基因组”的搜索，转化为200次并行的“寻找该位置最优基因”的搜索。用我们的密码锁类比来说，模块化电路模拟就像是转动密码拨盘直到听到“咔嗒”声，这代表对应数字正确。而有性生殖更像是尝试大量随机组合，得到一个“该组合接近解锁的程度”的评分，并据此推断哪些“拨盘位置”是正确的。这样一来，搜索空间会大幅缩小，搜索过程也会快得多。

With Arthur’s technological search, we couched this reduction in terms of information theory, calculating the bits of information gleaned per iteration. (As a reminder, a “bit” is just “something that cuts the number of possibilities to consider in half.”) In our 8-bit adder, 68 NAND gate search, finding the working arrangement required narrowing down 2^853 possibilities, or 853 bits. Trying to get all 68 gates right at once got us less than 0.000001 bits per attempt, requiring a long and painful search. But with modularity — going gate-by-gate and knowing whether each gate is in the correct location — we accumulate information much faster, around 0.003 bits per attempt (3,000x faster than nonmodular search).借助阿瑟的技术研究，我们从信息论的角度来解释这种缩减，计算每一次迭代中获取的信息比特数。（提醒一下，“比特”就是“能将需要考虑的可能性数量减半的东西”。）在我们的8位加法器和68个与非门的搜索过程中，找到可行的布局需要从2的853次方种可能性中筛选，也就是853个比特。如果试图一次性让68个门电路都正确，每一次尝试能获取的信息还不到0.000001比特，这就意味着需要进行漫长而艰难的搜索。但借助模块化设计——逐门电路检查并判断每个门电路是否处于正确位置——我们能更快地积累信息，每次尝试大约能获取0.003个比特，速度比非模块化搜索快3000倍。

We can similarly look at biological evolution and the spread of useful genetic variants in terms of information acquisition. For our simulations, the information we have at a given time is a function of how “certain” the population is about the value of each gene. With our starting, randomized population for each gene approximately 50% of the population has a 1 and 50% has a 0, we’re maximally uncertain, and we have 0 bits of information for each gene. When the population has reached maximum fitness (every member having a 1 for every gene) we’re maximally certain, and we have 1 bit of information for each gene. Total information is thus roughly equal to 2 \* (F - G/2), where F is fitness and G is genome length.我们也可以从信息获取的角度，类似地研究生物进化以及有益基因变异的传播。在我们的模拟中，某一时刻我们所拥有的信息，取决于种群对每个基因值的“确定程度”。在初始阶段，每个基因的种群是随机的，约50%的个体携带1，50%携带0，此时我们的不确定性达到最大，每个基因的信息熵为0比特。当种群达到最大适应度时（每个个体的所有基因都为1），我们的确定性达到最大，每个基因的信息熵为1比特。因此，总信息熵大致等于2×(F - G/2)，其中F为适应度，G为基因组长度。

We can see that information is acquired much more quickly in our sexual reproduction simulation than in our asexual simulation.我们可以看到，在有性生殖模拟中，信息的获取速度比在无性生殖模拟中快得多。

There’s an important caveat to all the above analysis: it assumes that genes independently contribute to fitness: that is, that the usefulness of gene 27 isn’t a function of gene 145. When genes are coupled like this, and the usefulness of one genetic variant is a function of what other genetic variants you possess (as they often are in practice), the evolutionary search process gets much more complex to analyze. (Stuart Kauffman has done a lot of work here with his concept of NK landscapes, which you can read about [here](https://www.amazon.com/At-Home-Universe-Self-Organization-Complexity/dp/0195111303?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.XgK_yltK3ZQjayEQRSEnxENIcFtWtOdJmHtTHJzz_9p6UhuEqj_1CB12Nedh9Dm69B4fJsb6sl1WJ4aX8XJ_ANBsoJTPk42X4IJy46IbbQnOztKBGr7G5E6neqSszUsoKpDCV0ASkzchnnVG7Ts6bApaTfi3_yqYuKkykFvBInx5UB0VJ8r-qGspMmaOjWSaOCjEquO8hz8yIDmzRW1MMcchOD1Ikmb2ZonuoQ9T75A.-RHVbctOSZcGIj5cxnOlRdTIkd6g_lAwC1Pm_V7DhMc&dib_tag=se&qid=1780932304&refinements=p_27%3AStuart+Kauffman&s=books&sr=1-1) and [here](https://www.amazon.com/Origins-Order-Self-Organization-Selection-Evolution/dp/0195079515?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.XgK_yltK3ZQjayEQRSEnxENIcFtWtOdJmHtTHJzz_9p6UhuEqj_1CB12Nedh9Dm69B4fJsb6sl1WJ4aX8XJ_ANBsoJTPk42X4IJy46IbbQnOztKBGr7G5E6neqSszUsoKpDCV0ASkzchnnVG7Ts6bApaTfi3_yqYuKkykFvBInx5UB0VJ8r-qGspMmaOjWSaOCjEquO8hz8yIDmzRW1MMcchOD1Ikmb2ZonuoQ9T75A.-RHVbctOSZcGIj5cxnOlRdTIkd6g_lAwC1Pm_V7DhMc&dib_tag=se&qid=1780932304&refinements=p_27%3AStuart+Kauffman&s=books&sr=1-2).) But the simple, additive fitness case is still useful for understanding these evolutionary mechanics.上述所有分析都有一个重要的前提假设：即基因各自独立地对适应性做出贡献，也就是说，27号基因的效用并非145号基因的函数。当基因之间存在这种关联，且某一基因变体的效用取决于你所拥有的其他基因变体（实际情况中往往如此）时，进化搜索过程的分析就会变得复杂得多。（斯图尔特·考夫曼在这一领域围绕其NK景观理论开展了大量研究，相关内容可在 [此处](https://www.amazon.com/At-Home-Universe-Self-Organization-Complexity/dp/0195111303?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.XgK_yltK3ZQjayEQRSEnxENIcFtWtOdJmHtTHJzz_9p6UhuEqj_1CB12Nedh9Dm69B4fJsb6sl1WJ4aX8XJ_ANBsoJTPk42X4IJy46IbbQnOztKBGr7G5E6neqSszUsoKpDCV0ASkzchnnVG7Ts6bApaTfi3_yqYuKkykFvBInx5UB0VJ8r-qGspMmaOjWSaOCjEquO8hz8yIDmzRW1MMcchOD1Ikmb2ZonuoQ9T75A.-RHVbctOSZcGIj5cxnOlRdTIkd6g_lAwC1Pm_V7DhMc&dib_tag=se&qid=1780932304&refinements=p_27%3AStuart+Kauffman&s=books&sr=1-1) 和 [此处](https://www.amazon.com/Origins-Order-Self-Organization-Selection-Evolution/dp/0195079515?dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.XgK_yltK3ZQjayEQRSEnxENIcFtWtOdJmHtTHJzz_9p6UhuEqj_1CB12Nedh9Dm69B4fJsb6sl1WJ4aX8XJ_ANBsoJTPk42X4IJy46IbbQnOztKBGr7G5E6neqSszUsoKpDCV0ASkzchnnVG7Ts6bApaTfi3_yqYuKkykFvBInx5UB0VJ8r-qGspMmaOjWSaOCjEquO8hz8yIDmzRW1MMcchOD1Ikmb2ZonuoQ9T75A.-RHVbctOSZcGIj5cxnOlRdTIkd6g_lAwC1Pm_V7DhMc&dib_tag=se&qid=1780932304&refinements=p_27%3AStuart+Kauffman&s=books&sr=1-2) 查阅。）不过，简单的加性适应性模型对于理解这些进化机制仍具有重要价值。

It’s also important to know that in real life, asexually reproducing organisms like bacteria have ways of sharing genes between them that get them some of the benefits of sexual reproduction. Bacteria widely engage in what’s known as [horizontal gene transfer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizontal_gene_transfer), which is exactly what it sounds like: genes being transferred between existing members of a population. This is apparently how genes for antibiotic resistance mostly spread, and some analyses suggest that [20-80% of bacterial genomes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5993296/) are the result of this sort of gene transfer.同样重要的是要了解，在现实生活中，细菌等无性繁殖生物拥有彼此共享基因的方式，这让它们能获得有性繁殖的部分好处。细菌广泛进行所谓的 [水平基因转移](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizontal_gene_transfer) ，顾名思义，就是基因在种群中现存个体之间转移。这显然是抗生素抗性基因主要的传播方式，一些分析表明， [20%至80%的细菌基因组](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5993296/) 都是这类基因转移的结果。

#### Conclusion 结论

The technological search process benefits greatly from modularity: being able to break a technology down into subcomponents with specific functionality, and determining whether those subcomponents are functioning properly. In information theoretic terms, this greatly narrows the possibilities that must be considered in a search process. It’s interesting to see that biological evolution, which creates and operates in an entirely separate domain, uses a similar sort of trick: using genetic recombination (in the form of sexual reproduction and horizontal gene transfer) to make the search process more modular and gain information more rapidly.技术搜索过程从模块化中获益良多：能够将一项技术拆解为具有特定功能的子组件，并判断这些子组件是否正常运行。从信息论的角度来看，这极大地缩小了搜索过程中必须考虑的可能性范围。有趣的是，在完全不同的领域中产生和运作的生物进化，也运用了类似的策略：通过基因重组（以有性生殖和水平基因转移的形式），使搜索过程更具模块化，从而更快地获取信息。

(*For more about these ideas about evolution and information acquisition, including a much more rigorous mathematical treatment, see chapter 19 in David Mackay’s book “ [Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms](https://www.inference.org.uk/itprnn/book.pdf).*”) （想要了解更多关于进化与信息获取的这些观点，包括更严谨的数学推导，可参见大卫·麦凯所著《 [信息论、推理与学习算法](https://www.inference.org.uk/itprnn/book.pdf) 》一书的第19章。）
