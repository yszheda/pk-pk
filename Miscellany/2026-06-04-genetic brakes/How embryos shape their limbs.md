Canadian scientists have made a significant advance in understanding the mechanisms that enable embryos to properly form their limbs, thanks to new research led by Université de Montréal medical professor Marie Kmita at the Montreal Clinical Research Institute (IRCM). 加拿大科学家在理解胚胎正常形成四肢的机制方面取得了重大进展，这要归功于由蒙特利尔大学医学教授玛丽·克米塔（Marie Kmita）领导、在蒙特利尔临床研究所（IRCM）开展的新研究。

In findings published in the journal PNAS, Kmita and her team highlight the crucial role of certain molecular systems that act as true “genetic brakes,” ensuring that development proceeds correctly.  在《美国国家科学院院刊》（PNAS）上发表的研究结果中，基塔及其团队强调了某些分子系统的关键作用，这些系统如同真正的“遗传刹车”，能确保发育过程正常进行。

At the very beginning of limb formation, certain genes must be activated to initiate the process. But once this first step is completed, those same genes must be rapidly switched off to allow subsequent genetic programs to take over and complete development. 在肢体形成的最开始阶段，必须激活特定基因来启动这一过程。但一旦这第一步完成，这些相同的基因必须迅速关闭，以便后续的基因程序接管并完成发育。

The new research at the IRCM shows that two groups of proteins, known as Polycomb complexes (PRC1 and PRC2), cooperate to silence these genes at the right time.  加拿大麦吉尔大学附属犹太总医院（IRCM）的新研究表明，两组被称为\*\*多梳蛋白复合物\*\*（PRC1和PRC2）的蛋白质会协同作用，在正确的时间使这些基因沉默。

In mice, the researchers demonstrated that altering one of these systems already leads to abnormalities in gene expression. When both systems are disrupted simultaneously, the consequences are major: early genes remain active and normal limb development is severely compromised.在小鼠身上，研究人员证实，改变其中一个系统就已经会导致基因表达异常。当两个系统同时被破坏时，后果十分严重：早期基因持续活跃，肢体的正常发育也受到严重损害。

Marie Kmita, professor at the Faculty of Medicine at Université de Montréal

“This discovery illustrates how development relies on finely tuned and precise orchestration of gene expression," said IRCM president and scientific director Jean-François Côté. “这一发现揭示了发育过程如何依赖于基因表达的精细调控和精准编排，”加拿大魁北克分子生物学研究所（IRCM）所长兼科学主任让-弗朗索瓦·科泰（Jean-François Côté）表示。

"Understanding these mechanisms is essential to better grasp the origin of certain malformations." “理解这些机制，对于更好地弄清某些畸形的成因至关重要。”

Beyond limb formation, these findings reveal a fundamental principle of development: cells must not only activate the right genes, but also remember which ones must remain switched off. This regulatory system helps prevent errors that could lead to congenital abnormalities.  除了肢体形成之外，这些发现还揭示了发育的一个基本原理：细胞不仅必须激活正确的基因，还需要记住哪些基因必须保持关闭状态。这一调控系统有助于避免可能导致先天性畸形的错误。

The next step in Kmita's research will be to identify the signals that guide the recruitment of these Polycomb complexes to the right genes at the right time—a key question for deepening scientists' understanding of embryonic development and its dysregulation.三木田的研究下一步将是识别出那些引导这些多梳蛋白复合物在正确时间结合到目标基因上的信号——这是加深科学家对胚胎发育及其失调机制理解的关键问题。
