I recently got a [Pebble Time 2](https://repebble.com/watch) as it seemed like a fun smartwatch away from Google/Apple/Samsung with a good 4 weeks of battery life. One thing I wanted to do is to create a custom watchface for *my* specific problems.我最近入手了一块 [Pebble Time 2](https://repebble.com/watch) ，因为它作为一款脱离谷歌、苹果和三星体系的智能手表，看起来很有趣，而且续航能达到整整四周。我想做的一件事，就是为 *我* 的个性化需求制作一款专属的表盘。

It took more effort to design the watch than I had anticipated and there’s a deceptively large amount of thought that has gone into some of the features here, so I thought it’d be interesting to write a little about it.设计这块手表所花费的精力比我预想的要多，而且其中一些功能背后看似简单，实则融入了大量的思考，所以我觉得写点相关的内容会很有意思。

## Issues I want the watch to help me with我希望这块手表能帮我解决的问题

It’s difficult to describe what I’ve been going through the last ~6 months. It started with a [normal depression](https://www.jonashietala.se/blog/2023/03/14/battling_burnout/) (yeah, a “normal depression” says a lot already) but it was followed by what felt like a big increase in volume; manageable issues I’ve had forever were suddenly overpowering.过去大约6个月里的经历，我实在难以言喻。起初只是 [普通的抑郁情绪](https://www.jonashietala.se/blog/2023/03/14/battling_burnout/) （没错，“普通的抑郁情绪”已经足以说明问题了），但随后那种压抑感仿佛急剧加重；我一直以来都能应对的小问题，突然之间就让我招架不住了。

I’ve always found it difficult to start chores and boring tasks, yet now it felt like trying to swim through quicksand.我一直觉得开始做家务和枯燥的任务很难，而此刻这种感觉却像是在流沙里游泳。

At the same time I had *huge* problems with hyper focus. I could start working on something and the entire day would just disappear; I would sit in front of the computer for 8 hours without any break, interrupted by having to go get my kids from school and I realize that my bladder is exploding and I hadn’t eaten lunch yet. Then I would spend the rest of the day thinking about it, even having difficulty falling asleep because I’m *still* thinking about it.与此同时，我在过度专注方面遇到了 *极大* 的问题。我可能一开始做某件事，一整天就一晃而过了；我会连续8小时坐在电脑前不休息，只有去学校接孩子时才会中断，这时我才发现自己的膀胱都快憋炸了，还没吃午饭。之后我一整天剩下的时间都会想着这件事，甚至因为 *还在* 琢磨这件事而难以入睡。

Needless to say, my time management has gone out the window, even missing appointments for the first time ever.不用说，我的时间管理已经彻底乱了套，甚至还破天荒地错过了几次预约。

Of course, a watchface won’t solve any of these but I’m desperate and nothing I’m trying is helping. Concretely I’d like the watch to help me with these things:当然，一款表盘并不能解决所有这些问题，但我实在没辙了，我试过的所有方法都没用。具体来说，我希望这款手表能帮我做这些事：

1. Give an overview of today’s calendar. 查看今日日程概览。
2. Getting started with tasks. 开始处理任务。
3. Alerts to break hyper focus. 提醒以打破过度专注。

I was going to name the watchface “ADHD hero” but that was a little on the nose as I don’t have an ADHD diagnosis (yet… The investigation is under way but it’s not a quick process).我本想把这个表盘命名为“多动症英雄”，但这个名字有点太直白了，因为我还没有被诊断出多动症（目前还在排查中……但这不是一个快速的过程）。

### A watchface only for me 一款只属于我的表盘

I like open source but I don’t have any plans of publishing the watchface. The entire point of the watchface is to *remove* friction in my life, not create another source of anxiety. This way I’m free to change it, rewrite it, pollute it with vibes, and I can focus on making it just good enough for me without having to worry about others.我喜欢开源，但我没有发布这款表盘的计划。这款表盘的核心目的是为我 *消除* 生活中的不便，而非制造新的焦虑来源。这样我可以自由修改、重写，给它融入各种个性化风格，也能专注于把它打磨到适合自己的程度，而不必担心他人的看法。

I encourage you to steal anything you like and create your own watchface; it’s straightforward with Pebble.我鼓励你尽情借鉴并打造属于你自己的表盘界面；用 Pebble 来做这件事非常简单。

Design-wise it’s hit-and-miss and Claude seems to lack creativity and taste (but sometimes it comes up with great ideas). You need to guide it properly and I’ve gotten good usage from telling it to generate dozens of variations of a concept and iterating from there. All images in this post are generated in this way at various points of the development.从设计角度来看，效果时好时坏，Claude 似乎缺乏创意和品味（不过有时也能想出绝妙的点子）。你需要对它进行恰当的引导，我发现让它生成一个概念的数十种变体，再在此基础上迭代优化，使用效果就很不错。本文中的所有图片都是通过这种方式，在开发的不同阶段生成的。

## Calendar overview 日历概览

I want to be able to glance at the watch to see my calendar. Watchfaces such as the [Sectograph](https://sectograph.com/) add event information inside the clock (so an event between 1 and 2 would colorize the section of the analog clock) and I wanted something similar.我希望能瞥一眼手表就能查看我的日历。像 [Sectograph](https://sectograph.com/) 这样的表盘会在时钟内部添加事件信息（比如1点到2点之间的事件会给模拟时钟的对应区域上色），而我想要类似的功能。

Here are some prototypes to give you an idea of what I’m talking about:这里有一些原型，让你了解我在说什么：

Single event in the day. 一天中的单个事件。

Four events the next 6 hours. 接下来6小时有四项活动。

Fully loaded day. 日程排满的一天。

This should *hopefully* make it easier for me to be able to plan my day a little better.这应该 *有望* 让我能更轻松地规划好自己的一天。

A notable omission in my design is the lack of a text description of the event. That’s nice but it also clutters the design I’m going for. The important thing for me isn’t what the event is, it’s “is there an event at all”.我的设计中有一个明显的遗漏，就是没有为事件添加文字说明。这样的设计固然简洁，但也会让我想要的效果显得杂乱。对我来说，重要的不是事件具体是什么，而是“是否存在事件”。

### Non-uniformity 非均匀性

My brain is very good at filtering away static and uninteresting information. For example, I’ve tried to have Calendar, [Habitica](https://habitica.com/), and [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com/) widgets on my phone to help me keep track of my events/habits/tasks… But it didn’t take long for my brain to start completely ignoring them despite them taking up 80% of the screen. When I say ignore I mean that literally: I completely stopped noticing them.我的大脑很擅长过滤掉杂乱且无趣的信息。比如，我曾尝试在手机上设置日历、 [Habitica](https://habitica.com/) 和 [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com/) 小组件，来帮我记录日程、习惯和任务……但没过多久，尽管这些组件占据了80%的屏幕，我的大脑还是开始彻底忽略它们。我说的忽略是字面意义上的：我完全不再注意到它们的存在。

So what I wanted to try was to spice it up a little by randomizing the event geometry. See these two examples:所以我想尝试的是通过随机化事件几何形状来稍微增添一些趣味。请看这两个示例：

Non-uniform events. 非均匀事件

To me the non-uniform prototype is more visually interesting and the changing geometry should hopefully prevent me from filtering them out.对我来说，非均匀原型在视觉上更有趣，而且不断变化的几何形状应该能让我不会把它们过滤掉。

### Moving towards 12 o’clock 向12点钟方向移动

Another idea I had was instead of statically fixing events to their times I wanted the events to move towards “now” at 12 o’clock. So an event that renders at 3 o’clock is 3 hours away and an event that overlaps 12 is ongoing.我还有另一个想法，与其将事件固定在对应的时间点静态显示，不如让事件朝着12点的“当前时刻”移动。这样一来，显示在3点位置的事件就代表还有3小时才会发生，而与12点位置重叠的事件则正在进行中。

While this prevents me from easily looking back at my day (past events disappear) it makes it easier to feel the urgency as events draw closer. A bit weird perhaps but in practice it feels great.虽然这让我无法轻易回顾自己的一天（过去的事情会消失），但随着事件临近，我能更容易感受到紧迫感。或许有点奇怪，但实际体验却很棒。

Here’s the same event through its lifecycle: drifting in from 3 o’clock, ongoing as it crosses 12 (with the countdown band draining in the clock), then gone once it’s in the past.以下是同一事件的完整生命周期：从3点方向飘来，在经过12点时持续进行（时钟上的倒计时条逐渐耗尽），一旦成为过去便彻底消失。

Something like this: 大概是这样：

Event is a few hours away. 活动还有几小时开始。

Event is ongoing. 活动正在进行中。

Event is in the past and has disappeared. 活动已成为过去且已消失。

### Completion countdown 倒计时完成

This works pretty well but there’s a problem: how to differentiate between an event that will start in one minute and that has been going on for a while? Because the event gets cut off at 12 there’s no way to tell at a glance.这个方法效果不错，但有一个问题：如何区分将在一分钟后开始的事件和已经持续了一段时间的事件？由于事件在第12位被截断，根本无法一眼分辨。

At first I tried to enlarge the event wedge so it touches the clock if it’s ongoing. That was a bit weird so instead I tried to colorize the clock outline with the event that’s ongoing. Then I realized, why not have the outline count down the remaining time of the event, similar to a [Time Timer](https://www.timetimer.com/)?起初，我尝试放大事件楔形，让它在事件进行时能碰到时钟。但这样做有点奇怪，于是我改为用正在进行的事件来给时钟轮廓上色。接着我想到，为什么不让轮廓来倒数事件的剩余时间呢，就像 [时间计时器](https://www.timetimer.com/) 那样？

The countdown band draining as the event completes. 随着活动结束，倒计时进度条逐渐耗尽。

In practice this works out wonderfully well.实际效果非常好。

## Work timer 工作计时器

I quite like the [completion countdown](#Completion-countdown) for calendar events. This kind of design is often associated with tracking tasks (such as in [Pomodoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique)) so why not add it to the watch too?我很喜欢日历事件的 [完成倒计时](#Completion-countdown) 功能。这种设计通常与任务追踪相关（比如在 [Pomodoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique) 中），那为什么不也把它添加到手表上呢？

For example, I might want to configure a stretch of 60 min work / 15 min break / 60 min work before I should stop working. Pretty standard stuff. As I’m struggling to get started I’ll add a twist: a working block should have a 5-minute warm-up period. 60 min 60分钟

Task 5-min warm-up followed by two 1 hour rounds, with a 15-min break between. 任务：进行5分钟热身，随后进行两轮各1小时的训练，中间休息15分钟。

### Starting/stopping timers on a Pebble watchface在 Pebble 智能手表表盘上启动/停止计时器

This is a post about design, not about implementation, but I still need to address how we can implement a work timer toggle.这是一篇关于设计而非实现的文章，但我仍需说明如何实现一个工作计时器的切换功能。

The issue is that a Pebble watchface is a background process that cannot interact with Pebble’s physical buttons. You can bind the buttons to quick launch Pebble apps but not *watchfaces*. A watchface can detect steps and taps on the watch but it cannot communicate with other apps.问题在于，Pebble 表盘是一个后台进程，无法与 Pebble 的实体按键进行交互。你可以将按键绑定到快速启动 Pebble 应用程序，但无法绑定到 *表盘* 。表盘可以检测手表上的步数和点击操作，但无法与其他应用程序通信。

There’s a few ways to get around this:有几种方法可以解决这个问题：

1. Toggle using taps only. 仅通过点击进行切换。

## Clearing alarms and forcing breaks 清除闹钟并强制休息

I’ve tried alarms, [Pomodoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique), and similar before but they all share the same failure mode: I simply ignore the alarm and continue with what I was doing.我之前试过闹钟、 [番茄钟](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique) 以及类似的方法，但它们都有同一个问题：我会直接忽略闹钟，继续做手头的事。

> A Pomodoro can’t be interrupted; it marks 25 minutes of pure work. 番茄钟无法被打断；它代表着25分钟的专注工作。

I don’t know, it could be 2 minutes just as likely as 200 minutes.我也不清楚，可能是2分钟，也可能是200分钟，概率都一样。

So I figured I’ll try to **force** myself to take breaks by making it annoying to clear (a bit like the [Hand Grenade Alarm clock](https://www.techshout.com/hand-grenade-alarm-clock-promises-to-wake-you-up/) but in wrist form). The watchface can vibrate, play sound, react to steps, and detect taps and this is the flow I came up with:所以我想，我可以通过让清除这个提醒变得麻烦一点的方式，来强迫\</b>自己休息（有点像 [手雷闹钟](https://www.techshout.com/hand-grenade-alarm-clock-promises-to-wake-you-up/) ，不过是做成手表的形式）。这个表盘可以震动、播放声音、对步数做出反应，还能检测点击，我想到的流程是这样的：

1. An alarm fires when I should stop working 当我该停止工作时，闹钟就会响起
2. I first need to walk +20 steps before I can clear it 我得先走满20步才能解除它
3. When that’s done I can dismiss it by tapping on the watch a bunch of times 完成这一步后，我就可以在手表上连续点击几下来解除它

The alarm transforms the screen and vibrates and plays a tune until it’s dismissed (repeats every 30 seconds).闹钟会改变屏幕显示，同时持续震动并播放音乐，直到你将其关闭（每30秒重复一次）。

Visually the required steps are drawn at the border (no events are shown during an alarm) and the alarm is drawn as a comic-style blast (tapping it shrinks the blast). Like this:视觉上，所需步骤绘制在边框上（警报触发期间不显示任何事件），警报以漫画风格的爆炸效果呈现（点击该效果会使爆炸缩小）。如下所示：

### How well does it actually work? 它实际效果到底怎么样？

Most of the time I forget to start the work timer and other times I’m able to add enough steps by waving my hand around like a crazy person, and then continue working. Could be tuned better I suppose. 我想这还能调得更好。

Manual alarm 手动闹钟
