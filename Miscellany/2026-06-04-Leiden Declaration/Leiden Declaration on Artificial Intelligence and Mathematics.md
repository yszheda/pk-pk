### Preamble 序言

Technological developments have repeatedly transformed the practice of mathematics. Recent artificial intelligence technologies, including symbolic and neural methods for the generation and formalization of mathematics, may already have initiated a significant chapter in this long history. Among researchers, artificial intelligence has produced a wide range of reactions: enthusiasm for its potential to yield new discoveries; intimidation by the pace of developments; indifference to these rapid changes; and concern for the implications, both for mathematics and in wider society. 技术发展一次次重塑了数学的研究实践。近期的人工智能技术，包括用于数学内容生成与形式化的符号方法和神经网络方法，或许已在这一漫长的历史中开启了重要的篇章。在研究人员群体中，人工智能引发了多样的态度：有人对其有望带来新发现的潜力满怀热情，有人因技术发展的速度感到畏惧，有人对这些快速变革漠不关心，也有人担忧其对数学领域乃至更广泛的社会所产生的影响。

Mathematicians have a choice about whether and how to adopt artificial intelligence in the conduct of their research. They also have a responsibility to ensure the continued flourishing of the discipline. This Declaration calls upon mathematicians to exercise this responsibility, and provides recommendations for individuals, institutions, government, and industry. 数学家们需要决定是否以及如何在研究工作中采用人工智能，同时也有责任确保该学科持续繁荣发展。本宣言呼吁数学家履行这一责任，并为个人、机构、政府和行业提供了相关建议。

Although we adopt the perspective of mathematical research, much of what we write applies equally to other aspects of mathematics. This includes work in the broader mathematical sciences, education, mentoring, publishing, funding, science policy, and use of mathematics in the wider world. 尽管我们是从数学研究的视角展开论述，但我们所撰写的大部分内容同样适用于数学领域的其他方面。这包括更广泛的数学科学领域的研究工作、数学教育、导师指导、学术出版、科研经费资助、科学政策制定，以及数学在更广阔现实世界中的应用。

The Declaration is conceived in solidarity with other research endeavors and creative professions facing similar challenges, both within and beyond academia. It complements other calls for action such as the [Uppsala Code of Ethics for Scientists](https://phsj.org/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/Uppsala-Code-of-Ethics-for-Scientists.pdf), the [San Francisco Declaration on Research Assessment](https://sfdora.org/read/), the [UNESCO Recommendation on Open Science](https://www.unesco-floods.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/379949eng.pdf), and the [UK Universal Ethical Code for Scientists](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/universal-ethical-code-for-scientists). The [International Mathematical Union Committee on Publishing](https://www.mathunion.org/cop), the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](https://www.siam.org/media/b03hwuwe/siam-report-ai-task-force.pdf), and the [American Mathematical Society](https://www.ams.org/about-us/ai-summary) have also produced related material. 本宣言的制定秉持与学术界内外面临类似挑战的其他研究工作及创意职业团结一致的理念。它是对《 [乌普萨拉科学家道德准则](https://phsj.org/wp-content/uploads/2007/10/Uppsala-Code-of-Ethics-for-Scientists.pdf) 》、《 [旧金山研究评估宣言](https://sfdora.org/read/) 》、《 [联合国教科文组织开放科学建议书](https://www.unesco-floods.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/379949eng.pdf) 》以及《 [英国科学家通用道德准则](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/universal-ethical-code-for-scientists) 》等其他行动呼吁的补充。 [国际数学联盟出版委员会](https://www.mathunion.org/cop) 、 [工业与应用数学学会](https://www.siam.org/media/b03hwuwe/siam-report-ai-task-force.pdf) 以及 [美国数学学会](https://www.ams.org/about-us/ai-summary) 也制定了相关文件。

#### About our values 关于我们的价值观

We base our recommendations on what we take to be characteristic values of mathematical research that we have a joint interest in preserving. Among these are the following: 我们的建议基于我们认为具有数学研究特征且我们共同有兴趣予以保留的价值。其中包括以下几点：

1. There are many reasons to pursue mathematical research, ranging from intellectual curiosity to a desire to solve practical and societal problems. Underlying much of mathematics is the activity of proof. Mathematical proofs are regarded as conferring the highest degree of certainty to their conclusions, as well as imparting understanding of why their conclusions are true. These characteristics of proof support the scientific integrity of mathematics.开展数学研究的原因有很多，既可以是出于求知欲，也可以是为了解决实际问题和社会问题。证明活动是数学领域的核心基础。数学证明被认为能为其结论提供最高程度的确定性，同时还能让人理解结论成立的缘由。证明的这些特性保障了数学的科学严谨性。
2. Results are attributable to specific authors who take credit for their discovery and assume responsibility for their correctness. These principles ground the merit-based standards to which we aspire in mathematical research.研究成果归属于具体的作者，他们为自己的发现获得荣誉，并对成果的正确性承担责任。这些原则构成了我们在数学研究中所追求的基于价值的标准。
3. Mathematical arguments are regarded as transparent and subject to independent verification. They may be extremely long or difficult, but in principle no proprietary knowledge or equipment should be required to understand them.数学论证被认为是透明且可独立验证的。它们可能极为冗长或复杂，但原则上理解它们无需任何专有知识或设备。
4. Mathematicians share a concern for proper evaluation of mathematical work relative to shared standards of depth, difficulty, and significance.数学家们都关注如何依据深度、难度和重要性的共同标准，对数学研究成果进行恰当的评价。
5. Mathematics produces not only a body of results, but also understanding, clarity, and judgment among the communities of mathematicians who have shaped them, often in the context of their own autonomously guided research. This expert knowledge is essential, both to effectively use mathematics, and to continue to articulate new and significant research questions. A key source of strength of the discipline has long been the autonomous shaping of the direction of research and the methods used to pursue it.数学不仅催生了一系列研究成果，还在那些塑造这些成果的数学家群体中培养出了理解力、清晰的思维与判断力，而这些数学家往往是在自主开展研究的过程中实现这一点的。这类专业知识至关重要，既能让人们有效运用数学，也能持续提出新的重要研究课题。长期以来，这一学科的核心优势之一，就是自主确定研究方向并研发相应的研究方法。

These characteristics of mathematics as a subject matter are also compatible with understanding mathematics as a human practice, and its place in the world. As mathematicians, and also as inhabitants of a shared world, we have a duty to care for other people and our environment. 数学作为一门学科的这些特征，也与将数学理解为一种人类实践及其在世界中的定位相契合。作为数学家，同时也作为共享世界的居民，我们有责任关爱他人并守护我们的环境。

#### Potential threats 潜在威胁

Recent developments in artificial intelligence threaten each of these values, often in ways that disproportionately affect students and early-career mathematicians, and hence the long term future of the discipline. 人工智能的最新发展正威胁着每一项这类价值，其影响方式往往对学生和职业生涯早期的数学家不成比例，进而危及该学科的长期未来。

1. Current automated techniques can produce plausible but unreliable (or even incorrect) arguments which are difficult to distinguish from correct mathematical proofs. This applies not only to informal arguments, but also to formalizations, where the difficulty lies in the translation between computer-encoded and human presentations of concepts. These fast-moving developments put our present system of review under increasing pressure, jeopardizing our ability to implement traditional standards for the correctness, transparency, and independent verifiability of proof. 当前的自动化技术能够生成看似合理但不可靠（甚至是错误）的论证，这些论证难以与正确的数学证明区分开来。这不仅适用于非形式化论证，也适用于形式化表述，其中的难点在于概念在计算机编码呈现与人类呈现之间的转换。这些快速发展的趋势给我们现行的评审体系带来了越来越大的压力，危及了我们落实证明在正确性、透明度和独立可验证性方面传统标准的能力。
2. Technologies that draw extensively on the published mathematical commons undermine the traditional system of attribution. Models trained on published works frequently return outputs that do not properly cite the human works they synthesize. Many current models are also built on data obtained by systematically exploiting licenses and access arrangements that were not made with artificial intelligence in mind, or indeed by simply violating copyright protections.广泛依托公开数学公共资源的技术正在破坏传统的归属体系。基于已发表作品训练的模型，其输出结果往往无法正确标注其所整合的人类作品的出处。当前许多模型的构建还依赖于两类数据：一类是通过系统性利用原本并非为人工智能设计的许可协议和使用安排获取的数据，另一类是直接侵犯版权保护所得的数据。
3. Technologies which affect the way in which mathematics is practiced may disturb the current system of incentives. The use of artificial intelligence — and thus also the sort of problems which it can address — may become incentivized for its own sake, disrupting our mechanisms for hiring, funding, and recognition. This disadvantages researchers who do not have access to the technologies or decision-making related to them, or who are unwilling to use technologies controlled by organizations whose values they do not share.影响数学研究实践方式的技术可能会扰乱现有的激励体系。人工智能的使用——以及它所能解决的各类问题——可能会因其自身价值而受到激励，这会破坏我们在招聘、资金资助和学术认可方面的机制。这对那些无法接触相关技术、无法参与相关决策，或是不愿使用由价值观不符的组织掌控的技术的研究人员构成了不利影响。
4. Proper evaluation is endangered if results are communicated through informal channels such as press releases or blog posts, often without any research paper or other disclosure of information necessary for scientific evaluation. This practice seeks publicity for new results on market timelines before the accepted processes of community evaluation in mathematics can take place. In many cases this leads to simplifications in reporting, such as overemphasizing the significance of automated tools and undervaluing the prior human contributions which have made those tools possible. Such oversimplification risks influencing public opinion in a way that not only damages perceptions of mathematics, but also misleadingly uses specific mathematical tasks as metrics for the general reasoning capacities of commercial products.如果研究结果通过新闻稿或博客文章等非正式渠道传播，且通常未附带任何研究论文或科学评估所需的其他信息披露，那么恰当的评估将难以实现。这种做法试图在数学领域公认的社区评估流程完成之前，根据市场节奏为新成果博取关注。在许多情况下，这会导致报告内容过于简化，比如过度夸大自动化工具的重要性，同时低估了促成这些工具问世的前期人类研究贡献。这种过度简化的做法不仅可能损害公众对数学学科的认知，还会误导性地将特定数学任务作为衡量商业产品整体推理能力的标准，从而对公众舆论产生影响。
5. These developments put the autonomy of mathematics under threat. The increasing involvement of technology companies in mathematical research raises the risk that research questions may come to be prioritized because of their amenability to automated mathematics, rather than expert judgment of their deeper significance. Indeed, broader understanding of the field may be permanently lost in the process of automation. With university budgets under pressure, this reshaping also changes professional incentives in a manner which encourages the collaboration of researchers with technology companies on asymmetric terms. If left unchecked, these trends go beyond threatening researchers’ autonomy, affecting the scope and depth of mathematical research itself.这些发展使数学的自主性面临威胁。科技公司越来越多地参与数学研究，增加了一种风险：研究问题的优先级可能会因其适合自动化数学研究，而非基于专家对其深层意义的判断而被确定。事实上，在自动化过程中，人们对该领域更广泛的理解可能会永久丧失。随着大学预算面临压力，这种重塑也改变了职业激励机制，促使研究人员以不对等的条件与科技公司开展合作。若任其发展，这些趋势不仅会威胁研究人员的自主性，还会影响数学研究本身的范围与深度。

All of these challenges arise at a moment when the consequences of large-scale investment in artificial intelligence are being widely discussed in regard to warfare, mass surveillance, political disruption, and environmental damage. These raise grave ethical concerns. By failing to act, we run the risk of becoming complicit in the support of technologies which threaten much more than the practice of mathematics. 所有这些挑战出现的当下，大规模投资人工智能所带来的后果正被广泛讨论，涉及战争、大规模监控、政治破坏以及环境破坏等多个方面。这些问题引发了严重的伦理担忧。如果我们不采取行动，就有可能成为支持那些威胁远不止数学实践的技术的同谋。

We thus feel that there is an urgent need for a considered response from the mathematical community. The following constitute brief descriptions of actionable recommendations. We encourage professional organizations to endorse this Declaration, and to add provisions according to their own values, priorities, and governance. 因此，我们认为数学界迫切需要做出深思熟虑的回应。以下是可采取行动的建议的简要说明。我们鼓励专业组织签署本《宣言》，并根据自身的价值观、优先事项和治理原则补充相关条款。

### Recommendations for individual mathematicians给数学家个人的建议

#### Disclose tool use 披露工具使用情况

Transparently disclose the use of automated tools, including large language models, machine learning systems, proof assistants, and other mathematical software. Include a “Tool and computational resource disclosure” section in your papers; many journals, publishers, and professional organizations have already developed guidelines for this, and though the precise form of such a section will necessarily evolve, we encourage authors to live up to the spirit reflected in the [UNESCO Recommendation on Open Science](https://www.unesco-floods.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/379949eng.pdf) and the [FAIR principles](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618). When acting as a reviewer, abide by publisher guidelines. If the use of artificial intelligence is allowed, be transparent about how you used it, and take responsibility for any significant recommendations you make. 透明披露自动化工具的使用情况，包括大语言模型、机器学习系统、证明助手及其他数学软件。请在论文中增设“工具与计算资源披露”章节；众多期刊、出版社及专业组织已为此制定了相关指导准则，尽管此类章节的具体形式必然会不断演变，但我们鼓励作者秉持 [《联合国教科文组织开放科学建议书》](https://www.unesco-floods.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/379949eng.pdf) 和 [FAIR 原则](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201618) 所体现的精神。担任审稿人时，请遵守出版社的指导准则。若允许使用人工智能技术，应透明说明其使用方式，并对所提出的任何重要建议承担责任。

#### Support the needs of reviewing 支持评审工作的需求

The use of artificial intelligence in preparing papers can introduce material that makes reviewing more demanding. Make it easier for your peers to review your work by disclosing tool use, giving precise and complete references to previous results, and providing formal proofs where feasible and appropriate. 在撰写论文时使用人工智能可能会引入一些内容，使得论文评审工作难度加大。你应主动披露所使用的工具，对以往的研究成果给出精准且完整的参考文献，并在可行且恰当的情况下提供正式的证明，以此让同行更轻松地评审你的研究成果。

#### Adhere to principles of open science遵循开放科学原则

The international open science movement aims to make scientific research transparent and accessible to all. As mathematical research becomes more reliant on data and software, adhere to principles of open science. See also the [UNESCO Recommendation on Open Science](https://www.unesco-floods.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/379949eng.pdf). 国际开放科学运动旨在让科学研究对所有人透明且可获取。随着数学研究对数据和软件的依赖度不断提高，需遵循开放科学原则。另见 [联合国教科文组织开放科学建议书](https://www.unesco-floods.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/379949eng.pdf) 。

#### Retain the responsibility for correctness对正确性承担责任

When automated techniques are employed in published mathematical research, the responsibility for the correctness and adequacy of the arguments and results, as well as for the completeness and accuracy of citations to relevant prior work, remains exclusively with the human authors. 当已发表的数学研究中采用自动化技术时，论证与结果的正确性和充分性，以及相关先前研究文献引用的完整性和准确性，其责任仍完全由人类作者承担。

#### Affirm the humanity of authorship 确认作者身份的人文属性

Credit and responsibility continue to belong to humans within the mathematical community and should not be given to automated systems. Artificial intelligence may obscure, but does not replace, the collective human labor behind a result. 信誉与责任仍属于数学界的人类，不应归属于自动化系统。人工智能或许会让成果背后的人类集体劳动变得模糊，但无法取而代之。

#### Put effort into proper attribution 用心做好合理归因

The known limitations of automated tools in properly attributing ideas create a corresponding obligation for proactive effort to find and credit the sources that made a new result possible. Where a satisfactory attribution is not possible, state this explicitly in the publication. 自动化工具在准确归因观点方面存在已知局限性，这相应地要求人们主动努力去寻找并认可促成新成果的来源。若无法做出令人满意的归因，则需在出版物中明确说明这一点。

#### Participate in public discourse 参与公共讨论

Mathematicians have a responsibility to support serious science journalism and to engage in public discourse to explain and contextualize artificial intelligence-assisted methods and results. This is particularly important for work within our own subfields, where specialized knowledge is required to assess claims about the depth, difficulty, and significance of results. Moreover, we encourage mathematicians to seek opportunities to cooperate with and support other researchers and creative professionals facing similar challenges. 数学家有责任支持严肃的科学新闻报道，并参与公共讨论，以解释人工智能辅助的方法和成果并为其梳理背景。对于我们自身所属子领域的研究而言，这一点尤为重要，因为评估成果的深度、难度和重要性需要专业知识。此外，我们鼓励数学家寻找机会与面临类似挑战的其他研究人员和创意专业人士合作并提供支持。

#### Stay informed about the emerging technologies了解新兴技术的最新动态

As appropriate to your interests and research, stay informed about the capability of computer-aided mathematical tools. Such understanding is important for informing how our discipline adapts to new technologies and for participating in governance and public discourse. 根据你的兴趣和研究方向，及时了解计算机辅助数学工具的性能。这种认知对于明确我们这一学科如何适应新技术，以及参与治理和公共讨论至关重要。

#### Welcome new contributors 欢迎新贡献者

The growing intersection of artificial intelligence and mathematics continues to attract researchers from other disciplines. We welcome this broadening of our community and the range of skills and perspectives these contributors bring. We encourage the mathematical community to actively engage with the broader community, to make our standards and practices explicit and accessible, and to create pathways for meaningful participation. In turn, we ask those entering our field to approach it with respect for our values, while also helping us to adapt and develop them. 人工智能与数学的交叉领域不断发展，持续吸引着其他学科的研究人员。我们欢迎学界的这一拓展，也欢迎这些参与者带来的多元技能与视角。我们鼓励数学界主动与更广泛的群体开展交流，明确并普及我们的标准与实践方式，同时为有意义的参与搭建渠道。反过来，我们也希望进入本领域的人士能够尊重我们的价值理念，同时助力我们对这些理念进行调整与发展。

#### Consider carefully which tools to use仔细考虑选择使用哪些工具

Some automated tools and their developers will align with the provisions of this Declaration, while others will not. Consider this when deciding which tools to use, or whether to use them at all. Also consider whether non-proprietary, energy-efficient, or small-scale systems suffice for your task. If not, consider how preservation of the values articulated in this Declaration may be worth a delay in obtaining results. 部分自动化工具及其开发者会遵守本宣言的条款，而另一些则不会。在决定使用哪些工具或是否使用工具时，请考虑到这一点。同时也请思考，非专有、节能或小型系统是否足以完成你的任务。如果不足以完成，不妨权衡一下，为践行本宣言所阐明的各项价值，延迟获取结果是否值得。

#### Evaluate the ethical consequences of your work, and take action accordingly评估你工作的伦理后果，并据此采取相应行动

Mathematics has led to technology which greatly improves everyday life for many people, yet it also has applications in the development of technology for use in warfare, oppression, mass surveillance, and the undermining of democracy. Evaluate the ethical consequences of your research to the best of your abilities, and if necessary withdraw from harmful work. Only enter into external partnerships which respect the values articulated in this Declaration. 数学催生了技术，极大地改善了许多人的日常生活，但它也被应用于研发用于战争、压迫、大规模监控以及破坏民主的技术。请尽你所能评估你的研究带来的伦理后果，如有必要，退出有害的工作。仅与尊重本宣言所阐明价值观的外部伙伴开展合作。

### Recommendations for mathematical organizations and not-for-profit research funders给数学组织和非营利研究资助者的建议

#### Build expertise and plan strategically培养专业能力并制定战略规划

Professional organizations should keep abreast of technical developments and be proactive in making informed recommendations to members and to the broader community. They should work together to guide the development of policy within academic publishing, funding bodies, and government. They should also actively prepare to become involved if major mathematical results are claimed using unconventional means. 专业组织应紧跟技术发展动态，主动为会员及更广泛的群体提供经过审慎考量的建议。它们应携手合作，指导学术出版、资助机构及政府层面的政策制定。若有研究人员通过非常规手段宣称取得重大数学成果，专业组织也应积极做好准备，参与相关事务的处理。

#### Take the lead on policies for publishing and reviewing牵头制定出版与评审相关政策

Professional organizations within mathematics should take a leadership role in developing guidelines in regard to the use of automated techniques in publication and in reviewing. These would include, for example, tool and computational resource disclosure, attribution, rules pertaining to authorship, and codes of conduct consistent with the values of mathematics. These would supplement and support guidelines already being developed by publishers and journals. 数学领域的专业组织应在制定关于在发表和审稿中使用自动化技术的指导方针方面发挥主导作用。这些指导方针例如应包括工具和计算资源披露、署名归属、与作者身份相关的规则，以及符合数学学科价值观的行为准则。这些指导方针将对出版商和期刊已在制定的相关指南起到补充和支持作用。

#### Maintain standards of rigor 保持严谨标准

When establishing policies, demand that results obtained by automated techniques be held to standards that address the risks raised by those techniques. These might include requiring human descriptions of central arguments obtained by automated tools, insisting on formal verification when appropriate, cross-checking theoretical and computational results, or external pre-submission review. 制定政策时，要求通过自动化技术获取的结果需符合相应标准，以应对这些技术带来的风险。这些措施可能包括：要求对自动化工具得出的核心论点进行人工阐释，在适当时坚持形式化验证，对理论和计算结果进行交叉核验，或开展提交前的外部审核。

#### Protect the rights of authors 保护作者权益

Automated mathematics presents new challenges to the rights of authors, and societies should be proactive in the development of sample licensing agreements to protect these rights. In particular, material should not be used as training data without consent, and publishing agreements should allow authors to opt-out of the use of their work in this way. 自动化数学给作者的权利带来了新挑战，学界应积极制定样本许可协议以保护这些权利。具体而言，相关材料未经同意不得用作训练数据，出版协议也应允许作者选择退出其作品以这种方式被使用。

#### Insist on appropriate publication outlets坚持合适的出版渠道

Demand that mathematical results continue to be published in peer-reviewed venues such as journals, proceedings, and books. Informal mechanisms such as press releases or blog posts can provide a valuable supporting role, but they cannot replace peer-review or community scrutiny. 要求数学成果继续在期刊、会议论文集、书籍等同行评审渠道发表。新闻稿、博客文章等非正式机制可发挥重要的辅助作用，但无法替代同行评审或学界审查。

#### Support public research laboratories 支持公共研究实验室

Support the formation of university-based, national, or international research laboratories devoted to studying automated mathematics which are administratively and financially independent from industry. Support the use of less resource-intensive technologies accessible to individual researchers. 支持成立以高校为依托、面向全国或全球的自动化数学研究实验室，这类实验室在管理和资金上独立于企业。同时支持采用单个研究人员可获取的、资源消耗更低的技术。

#### Provide frameworks for collaboration 建立协作框架

Mathematicians and academic organizations collaborating with industry often face asymmetries in their bargaining positions, as well as in access to professional support such as legal resources, or advice on intellectual property. Support researchers in such collaborations by providing access to legal representation, and by facilitating the development of codes of professional practice. 数学家及与产业合作的学术机构，在谈判地位以及获取法律资源、知识产权咨询等专业支持方面，往往面临不对等的情况。应为这类合作中的研究人员提供法律代理渠道，并推动专业行为准则的制定。

#### Align funding with values 让资金与价值观相契合

Alignment with the values of this declaration should be taken into account in the evaluation and funding of projects which involve collaboration between academics and industrial partners. 在评估和资助涉及学术界与产业伙伴合作的项目时，应考虑其与本宣言价值观的契合度。

### Recommendations for policymakers in government and elsewhere为政府及其他机构的政策制定者提供建议

#### Protect the rights of authors 保护作者权利

Strengthen legal protections for authors, in line with this declaration. 根据本宣言加强对作者的法律保护。

#### Don’t believe the hype 别轻信炒作

There is currently a strong commercial incentive on the part of the technology industry to overstate the capabilities of their products. Consult with experts, including mathematicians, in forming policy decisions rather than relying on press releases or popular reporting of mathematical results. 目前科技行业存在强烈的商业动机，夸大其产品的性能。在制定政策决策时，应咨询包括数学家在内的专家，而非依赖新闻稿或对数学成果的大众报道。

#### Regulate the artificial intelligence industry规范人工智能行业发展

Recent developments continue to highlight the strong public interest in regulating the technology industry, for example in regard to involvement in military and mass surveillance programs, development of technologies which promote misinformation and undermine democracy, and environmental costs. We stand with others in calling for significantly increased public oversight. 近期的动态持续凸显出公众对科技行业监管的强烈兴趣，例如在参与军事和大规模监控项目、研发助长虚假信息并破坏民主的技术以及环境成本等方面。我们与其他人士一道，呼吁大幅加强公众监督。

#### Invest in public computational infrastructure投资公共计算基础设施

Current events illustrate the need for public alternatives to proprietary technologies, from basic services for online collaboration, to computer clusters for mathematical modeling and machine learning applications. We support the funding of public infrastructure at university, national, and international levels. 当下的事件表明，我们需要替代专有技术的公共替代方案，从在线协作的基础服务，到用于数学建模和机器学习应用的计算机集群皆如此。我们支持在大学、国家及国际层面为公共基础设施提供资金。

### Recommendations for commercial artificial intelligence商业人工智能领域的建议

While the mathematical community has recognized standing in academic and public policymaking, it has no comparable role in the corporate decision-making that is playing an increasing role in our discipline. Nonetheless, recent developments have drawn mathematical work into industrial artificial intelligence efforts in multiple ways. One is through the use of mathematics to advertise the capabilities of commercial artificial intelligence systems in public communications and public relations campaigns. Another is that artificial intelligence developers have increasingly used mathematical publications and formal mathematical libraries as sources of training data — not only for specialized models for mathematics, but for more general-purpose artificial intelligence. 尽管数学界已在学术研究和公共政策制定领域确立了自身的地位，但在对本学科影响日益加深的企业决策过程中，却没有发挥出与之相当的作用。不过，近期的发展动态已从多个方面将数学研究纳入工业人工智能的研发工作中。一方面，数学被用于在公共宣传和公关活动中彰显商业人工智能系统的性能；另一方面，人工智能开发者也越来越多地将数学研究成果和正式的数学知识库作为训练数据的来源——不仅用于构建数学领域的专用模型，也用于开发通用性更强的人工智能系统。

What currently makes mathematics attractive for general-purpose artificial intelligence development is that the correctness of formalized proofs can be checked automatically, without the need for human oversight. This makes it possible to generate and check vast numbers of problems, both human-authored and computer-generated, to produce an effectively unlimited source of feedback for training artificial intelligence models. The rationale for this strategy often rests on a further assumption: that capabilities developed through mathematical theorem proving will extend to broader general reasoning. Some of the resulting general-purpose models are being commercialized for applications that raise grave ethical concerns, including those named earlier: warfare, oppression, mass surveillance, and the undermining of democracy. 目前让数学对通用人工智能开发具有吸引力的一点是，形式化证明的正确性可以自动验证，无需人工监督。这使得生成和检查大量问题（无论是人工创作还是计算机生成）成为可能，从而为训练人工智能模型提供几乎无限的反馈来源。这一策略的理论依据往往基于一个进一步的假设：通过数学定理证明所培养的能力，将延伸到更广泛的通用推理领域。由此产生的部分通用模型已被商业化应用于一些引发严重伦理担忧的场景，包括前文提及的战争、压迫、大规模监控以及破坏民主等方面。

We recognize that industry has offered lucrative jobs, monetary rewards, computing resources, and intellectually stimulating opportunities that some mathematicians have found attractive. This has taken place in an era of underfunding of higher education and precarious academic employment. We also recognize that many mathematicians did not expect their work to become entangled with social and ethical implications of such magnitude, nor to be incorporated into systems used for purposes they may find deeply troubling. 我们认识到，行业提供了丰厚的工作岗位、物质报酬、计算资源以及能激发智力的机会，这些对一些数学家而言颇具吸引力。这一情况发生在高等教育资金不足、学术就业不稳定的时代。我们也认识到，许多数学家并未预料到自己的研究会与如此重大的社会和伦理影响纠缠在一起，也未料到自己的研究成果会被纳入到他们可能深感不安的各类用途系统中。

We call on collaborations between mathematicians and industry to abide, at minimum, by the standards we expect of our colleagues and that are described throughout this Declaration. Such collaborations must respect the freedom of conscience of employees or contributors to speak openly about corporate policies and priorities. 我们呼吁数学家与业界的合作至少遵守我们对同行所期望的、并在本宣言通篇所述的标准。此类合作必须尊重员工或贡献者公开谈论企业政策与优先事项的良心自由。
