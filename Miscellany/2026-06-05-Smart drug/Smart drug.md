## Smart drug that strips cancer cells of ‘invisibility cloak’ can shrink tumours by 30%, trial shows试验显示，这种能剥离癌细胞“隐形外衣”的智能药物可使肿瘤缩小30%

Experimental tablet produces encouraging results in patients with world’s most common forms of disease 实验性片剂在全球最常见疾病患者身上取得令人鼓舞的效果

[‘I was getting ready to say goodbye’: patient’s hope after smart drug success “我正准备说再见”：智能药物取得成功后患者重燃希望](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/jun/01/cancer-patient-hope-after-smart-drug-trial-success)

A smart drug that stops cancer cells “hiding” from treatment can shrink tumours by at least 30% in six of the world’s most common forms of the disease, early trial results show.早期试验结果显示，一种能阻止癌细胞“躲避免疫治疗”的智能药物，在全球六种最常见的癌症类型中，可使肿瘤缩小至少30%。

While immunotherapy treatments have improved survival rates for many patients, their effectiveness can stall or fail when tumour cells hide and then spread.虽然免疫疗法治疗提高了许多患者的生存率，但当肿瘤细胞隐匿并随后扩散时，其疗效可能会停滞或失效。

Researchers in Oxford have developed a drug designed to stop cancer cells concealing themselves from the immune system, allowing immunotherapy treatments to identify and destroy them.牛津大学的研究人员研发出一种药物，该药物旨在阻止癌细胞躲避免疫系统，从而让免疫疗法能够识别并摧毁它们。

In a trial spanning the UK, France, Spain and Australia, 83 patients with cervical, bladder, liver, bowel, lung or head and neck cancers were given the experimental drug, GRWD5769, alongside the immunotherapy treatment cemiplimab.在一项覆盖英国、法国、西班牙和澳大利亚的试验中，83名患有宫颈癌、膀胱癌、肝癌、肠癌、肺癌或头颈癌的患者接受了实验性药物GRWD5769联合免疫疗法赛米利单抗的治疗。

Researchers, led by the Christie NHS foundation trust in Manchester, England, found that tumours shrank in 26 patients. Of those, 15 experienced tumour reductions of at least 30%.由英国曼彻斯特克里斯蒂国民保健信托基金会领导的研究人员发现，有26名患者的肿瘤出现缩小。其中15名患者的肿瘤缩小幅度至少达到了30%。

All participants had previously failed to respond to treatment, and most had no options left when they joined the study. Crucially, immunotherapy had not worked or had stopped working.所有参与者此前均对治疗无反应，加入研究时大多数人已无其他治疗选择。关键的是，免疫疗法要么未曾奏效，要么已经失效。

The smart drug was able to remove “invisibility cloaks” from tumour cells, exposing them to the parts of the immune system that attack infections and diseases. This allowed the cemiplimab immunotherapy to pinpoint and destroy the cancer.这种智能药物能够为肿瘤细胞去除“隐形斗篷”，使其暴露在负责攻击感染和疾病的免疫系统部分之下。这让西米普利单抗免疫疗法得以精准定位并摧毁癌细胞。

The findings were presented at the American Society of Clinical Oncology’s annual meeting in Chicago, the [world’s largest cancer conference](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2025/jun/06/key-takeaways-from-worlds-largest-cancer-conference-in-chicago).研究结果在芝加哥举行的美国临床肿瘤学会年度会议上公布，这是 [全球最大的癌症会议](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2025/jun/06/key-takeaways-from-worlds-largest-cancer-conference-in-chicago) 。

GRWD5769 was shown to shrink tumours in all six cancer types included in the trial. The drug halted progression of the disease for at least six months in 18% of cervical cancer patients, 32% of liver cancer patients, 36% of bladder cancer patients, 38% of those with neck and head cancer, and more than half of bowel (51%) and lung (55%) cancer patients.研究显示GRWD5769能使试验涵盖的全部六种癌症的肿瘤缩小。在宫颈癌患者中，18%的人、肝癌患者中32%的人、膀胱癌患者中36%的人、头颈癌患者中38%的人，以及半数以上的肠癌（51%）和肺癌（55%）患者，该药物使疾病进展至少停滞了六个月。

Results from the phase 1 trial were presented at the conference by its principal investigator, Prof Fiona Thistlethwaite, a consultant medical oncologist and medical director of the Christie clinical research facility.该一期试验的结果由其主要研究者菲奥娜·西斯尔思韦特教授在会议上公布，他是一名医学肿瘤学顾问，同时也是克里斯蒂临床研究机构的医疗总监。

Speaking to the Guardian in Chicago, Thistlethwaite said: “For a drug that is given as a tablet, this is very impressive. It’s early days, and we need further studies, but this is a new drug with a new mechanism that clearly helps immunotherapy perform more effectively.” 西斯尔思韦特在芝加哥接受《卫报》采访时表示：“对于一种以片剂形式给药的药物来说，这一效果非常令人印象深刻。现在还处于早期阶段，我们需要开展进一步研究，但这是一种具有全新作用机制的新药，显然能让免疫治疗的效果得到显著提升。”

The tablets, which can be taken at home, were developed by Oxford-based Greywolf Therapeutics and were tolerated well by patients. The trial remains ongoing, with a larger study planned.这种可在家服用的片剂由总部位于牛津的格雷沃尔夫疗法公司研发，患者对其耐受性良好。该试验仍在进行中，公司计划开展一项规模更大的研究。

Immunotherapy enlists T-cells – immune system cells that attack infections and diseases – to hunt and destroy cancer. Although it has revolutionised cancer care, it fails in about two-thirds of patients. This is because immunotherapy struggles when tumours hide from the immune system.免疫疗法利用T细胞——即攻击感染和疾病的免疫系统细胞——来搜寻并摧毁癌细胞。尽管它彻底革新了癌症治疗，但约三分之二的患者对其治疗无效。这是因为当肿瘤躲避免疫系统时，免疫疗法便难以发挥作用。

Tumours can evade the immune system by manipulating an enzyme called ERAP1 (endoplasmic reticulum aminopeptidase 1). By altering this enzyme, cancer cells can hide from a patient’s T-cells.肿瘤可通过操纵一种名为ERAP1（内质网氨肽酶1）的酶来逃避免疫系统。癌细胞改变这种酶后，就能躲过患者T细胞的识别。

GRWD5769 solves this problem by inhibiting ERAP1. This, in effect, removes cancer’s invisibility cloak and makes tumour cells visible to T-cells that could not previously find them.GRWD5769 通过抑制 ERAP1 解决了这一问题。这实际上揭开了癌细胞的隐形外衣，让原本无法识别它们的 T 细胞能够发现肿瘤细胞。

“Immunotherapy has been a gamechanger in the way we treat cancer, but the number of people that can benefit is still relatively low,” Thistlethwaite said. “What excites me about this trial is the combination of what we’re seeing – strong signals of efficacy across six tumour types that have shown great resistance to immunotherapy, with very few side-effects. That’s unusual at such an early stage, when we’re usually just looking at how safe it is.“免疫疗法已经彻底改变了我们治疗癌症的方式，但能从中获益的人群数量仍然相对较少，”西斯尔思韦特说。“这项试验让我感到振奋的是，我们观察到了令人欣喜的联合效果——在六种对免疫疗法已产生极强耐药性的肿瘤类型中，都显示出了显著的疗效信号，且副作用极少。在试验的如此早期阶段，这是很不寻常的，我们通常此时只关注其安全性。

“There’s a lot more work to be done before it reaches the clinic, but for a brand new drug to show that kind of profile so early – and in so many different types of hard-to-treat cancers – it gives me genuine optimism.” “在这种药物应用于临床之前，还有大量工作要做，但一种全新的药物能在如此早期——且针对如此多种不同类型的难治性癌症——展现出这样的效果，这让我由衷地感到乐观。”

The trial’s UK principal investigator, Prof Stefan Symeonides, a consultant medical oncologist at the [Edinburgh](https://www.theguardian.com/uk/edinburgh) Cancer Centre and professor of experimental cancer medicine at the Institute of Genetics and Cancer, University of Edinburgh, described the early results as “exciting”. “It is fantastic to have been able to bring this promising new immunotherapy approach through to clinical trials and to see our patients benefiting,” he said.该试验的英国首席研究员斯特凡·西米奥尼德斯教授是爱丁堡癌症中心的医学肿瘤学顾问，同时也是爱丁堡大学遗传学与癌症研究所实验癌症医学教授，他将早期结果描述为“令人振奋”。他表示：“能够将这种极具前景的新型免疫疗法推进至临床试验，并看到患者从中获益，这真是太棒了。”

Cancer Research UK’s research information lead, Dr Samuel Godfrey, who was not involved with the trial, said: “Immunotherapy has transformed treatment for some cancers but it doesn’t yet work for everyone. This trial seems to show how this new drug could make immunotherapy more effective, including in some cases where immunotherapy had previously failed.英国癌症研究中心的研究信息负责人塞缪尔·戈弗雷博士并未参与该试验，他表示：“免疫疗法已经改变了部分癌症的治疗方式，但目前并非对所有人都有效。这项试验似乎展示了这种新药如何能让免疫疗法更有效，包括在部分免疫疗法此前失效的病例中。

“It is unusual to see such outcomes in patients whose cancers have already stopped responding to treatment, particularly across several hard‑to‑treat cancer types, so these results are encouraging. However, this is still an early‑stage study, and larger trials will be needed to determine whether this approach can deliver lasting benefits for patients.” “在癌症治疗已无效的患者身上看到这样的治疗效果，尤其是在多种难治癌症类型的患者中，实属罕见，因此这些结果令人鼓舞。不过，这仍属于早期研究，还需要开展更大规模的临床试验，才能确定该疗法能否为患者带来持久益处。”
