## Women were never meant to give birth on their backs女人从来不该仰躺生孩子

Pregnant woman standing in front of brightly backlit window (Credit: Getty Images) 孕妇站在明亮背光窗前（图片来源：Getty Images）

For thousands of years, across the world, women tended to give birth in an upright position – whether kneeling [as per Cleopatra](https://sites.nd.edu/biomechanics-in-the-wild/2024/11/06/biomechanical-and-historical-insights-into-birthing-position/), using [birthing stools](https://www.meisterdrucke.us/fine-art-prints/German-School/669644/Birthing-Chair-Used-in-the-16th-Century.html?gQT=1) and [chairs](https://www.historyextra.com/period/roman/childbirth-ancient-rome-empire-mothers-midwives-how-babies-born/), or [squatting](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK601353/). In fact, squatting can enlarge the pelvic diameter [by at least 2.5cm](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9154243/) (1in), while working with gravity makes it far easier to give birth.数千年来，世界各地的女性倾向于以直立姿势分娩——无论是像 [克娄巴特拉那样](https://sites.nd.edu/biomechanics-in-the-wild/2024/11/06/biomechanical-and-historical-insights-into-birthing-position/) 跪着，使用 [产凳](https://www.meisterdrucke.us/fine-art-prints/German-School/669644/Birthing-Chair-Used-in-the-16th-Century.html?gQT=1) 和 [椅子](https://www.historyextra.com/period/roman/childbirth-ancient-rome-empire-mothers-midwives-how-babies-born/) [，还是蹲](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK601353/) 着。事实上，深蹲可以使骨盆直径 [至少增加2.5厘米](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9154243/) （1英寸），而借助重力工作则使分娩更容易。

So why do so many women today give birth on their backs?那么，为什么如今这么多女性仰躺分娩呢？

"There is a generalised ignorance amongst professions and pregnant women about the physiology of birth," says Janet Balaskas, founder of the [Active Birth Centre](https://activebirthcentre.com/) in the UK, and author of a number of books detailing how mothers can take control of their birth experience. In 1982, Balaskas published an ["active birth manifesto](https://activebirthcentre.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/03/AB-Manifesto-2.pdf) " that became the central tenet of her organisation.英国 [主动分娩中心](https://activebirthcentre.com/) 创始人、多本介绍母亲如何掌控分娩体验的书籍作者珍妮特·巴拉斯卡斯说：“各职业和孕妇普遍对分娩生理学无知。”1982年，巴拉斯卡斯发表了 [一份“积极分娩宣言](https://activebirthcentre.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/03/AB-Manifesto-2.pdf) ”，成为她组织的核心信条。

"Throughout the world, and for thousands of years, women have spontaneously laboured and given birth in some form of upright or crouching positions," the manifesto reads. "Whatever the race or culture… the same upright positions predominate." 宣言中写道：“在全世界，几千年来，女性一直以某种直立或蹲姿自发分娩。”“无论种族或文化......同样的直立姿势占主导地位。”

Most women in post-industrial countries are confined to hospital in recumbent positions, Balaskas says. "This practice is illogical, making birth needlessly complicated and expensive, turning a natural process into a medical event and the labouring woman into a passive patient," she argues. "No other species adopts such a disadvantageous position at such a crucial time." 巴拉斯卡斯说，后工业国家的大多数女性被限制在医院，处于卧姿状态。她认为：“这种做法不合逻辑，使分娩变得不必要地复杂且昂贵，将自然过程变成医疗事件，将分娩的妇女变成被动患者。”“没有其他物种在如此关键的时刻采取如此不利的地位。”

Other experts agree. In fact, giving birth lying down is a "relatively modern phenomenon", Hannah Dahlen, professor of midwifery at Australia's Western Sydney University, wrote in a [2013 op-ed for The Conversation.](https://theconversation.com/stand-and-deliver-upright-births-best-for-mum-and-bub-13095)其他专家也同意这一点。事实上，澳大利亚西悉尼大学助产学教授汉娜·达伦在 [2013年为《对话》](https://theconversation.com/stand-and-deliver-upright-births-best-for-mum-and-bub-13095) 杂志撰写的评论文章中写道，躺着分娩是一种“相对现代的现象”。

It's only in the [past 300 to 400 years](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/pdf/10.2105/AJPH.77.5.636) that women have been largely giving birth on their backs. They can thank a [French man named François Mauriceau](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0301211516310181). He claimed that the reclining position would be both more comfortable for the pregnant woman and more convenient for the male physician attending to her (there was already a movement emerging to [dispense of midwives](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/pdf/10.2105/AJPH.77.5.636) and instead have male surgeons present at births).直到 [过去300到400年](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/pdf/10.2105/AJPH.77.5.636) ，女性才大多仰卧分娩。他们要感谢一个 [叫弗朗索瓦·莫里索的法国人](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0301211516310181) 。他声称，躺卧姿势不仅让孕妇更舒适，也让男性医生更方便（当时已有一种运动开始取消 [助产](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/pdf/10.2105/AJPH.77.5.636) 士，转而由男性外科医生在分娩时在场）。

Mauriceau viewed pregnancy as an illness. In his 1668 book [The diseases of women with child and in child-bed](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/e/eebo2/A88969.0001.001?view=toc), Mauriceau advised: "The best and surest is to be delivered in their bed, to shun the inconvenience and trouble of being carried thither afterwards." 莫里索将怀孕视为一种疾病。在他1668年出版的《 [产妇及产床妇女的疾病](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/e/eebo2/A88969.0001.001?view=toc) 》一书中，莫里索建议：“最好的、最确定的就是在床上分娩，以避免事后被抬到那里带来的不便和麻烦。”

However, some scholars argue that the change in birthing position may actually be due to another Frenchman who lived the same time as Mauriceau – King Louis XIV.然而，一些学者认为，分娩姿势的变化实际上可能是因为另一位与毛里索同时代的法国人——路易十四国王。

"Since Louis XIV reportedly enjoyed watching women giving birth, he became frustrated by the obscured view of birth when it occurred on a birthing stool, and promoted the new reclining position," wrote Lauren Dundes, a professor of sociology at McDaniel College in Maryland, US, in her 1987 paper on [the evolution of birthing positions.](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/pdf/10.2105/AJPH.77.5.636)“由于路易十四据说喜欢观看妇女分娩，他对分娩在分娩凳上时视线模糊感到沮丧，并推广了新的躺卧姿势，”美国马里兰州麦克丹尼尔学院社会学教授劳伦·邓德斯在她1987年关于 [分娩姿势演变](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/pdf/10.2105/AJPH.77.5.636) 的论文中写道。

![Getty Images Research in 2011 showed women in birth centres were far more likely to adopt upright positions when delivering their baby (Credit: Getty Images)](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/images/ic/1920xn/p0nb4c4k.jpg.webp)

Research in 2011 showed women in birth centres were far more likely to adopt upright positions when delivering their baby (Credit: Getty Images) 2011年的研究显示，分娩中心的女性在分娩时更有可能采用直立姿势（图片来源：Getty Images）

"The influence of the king's policy is unknown, although the behaviour of royalty must have affected the populace to some degree," she added. "Louis XIV's purported demand for change did coincide with the changing of the position and may well have been a contributing influence." “国王政策的影响尚不清楚，尽管王室的行为必然在一定程度上影响了民众，”她补充道。“路易十四所谓的变革诉求确实与立场的转变同时发生，可能对此产生了影响。”

Regardless of how giving women birth on their backs came about, the trend stuck, much to the [detriment of their birthing experience.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10202683/#:~:text=When%20a%20woman%20is%20in,placental%20blood%20flow%20%5B23%5D.) "Birth has become institutionalised with options such as home birth – which is more conducive for many women wanting a physiological or 'natural' birth – declining," says Balaskas.无论女性仰卧分娩是如何形成的，这一趋势一直延续，严重 [损害了她们的分娩体验。](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10202683/#:~:text=When%20a%20woman%20is%20in,placental%20blood%20flow%20%5B23%5D.) “分娩已经制度化，家庭分娩等选择——对许多希望生理性或'自然'分娩的女性来说更有利于——正在减少，”巴拉斯卡斯说。

The main reason women have given birth in upright positions for so many thousands of years is simple: gravity. A baby has to travel downwards through the birthing canal, and [gravity is beneficial to the process](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10351960/#cit0010). It has been shown that left to their own devices, [women will instinctively lean forward](https://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/springer/ijc/2011/00000001/00000004/art00004) during labour – not backwards – adopting positions such as squatting, leaning forward on their hands and knees, or leaning against a low piece of furniture.女性几千年来一直以直立分娩的主要原因很简单：重力。婴儿必须通过产道向下移动，重 [力对此过程有益](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10351960/#cit0010) 。研究表明，女性在分娩时 [会本能地向前倾](https://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/springer/ijc/2011/00000001/00000004/art00004) ，而不是向后倾斜——采取蹲姿、手膝前倾或靠在低矮家具上。

A 2013 [review of 25 studies](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD003934.pub4) involving more than 5,200 women noted that other important outcomes for women who gave birth upright and mobile rather than lying down in bed included "a reduction in the risk of Caesarean birth, less use of epidural as a method of pain relief, and less chance of their babies being admitted to the neonatal unit". The review did note that more studies were needed for women in high risk groups – some studies have shown an [increase in blood loss](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17217358/) in upright birth positions.2013年一项 [涵盖25](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD003934.pub4) 项研究、涉及5200多名女性的综述指出，对于直立且可活动分娩而非卧床分娩的女性，其他重要结果包括“剖腹产风险降低、硬膜外麻醉作为止痛方法的使用减少，以及婴儿被送入新生儿病房的可能性降低”。综述指出，对于高风险群体的女性，需要更多研究——一些研究显示直立分娩时失 [血量增加](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17217358/) 。

Labouring upright and giving birth upright have advantages for both the mother and baby – Hannah Dahlen 直立分娩和直立分娩对母婴双方都有好处——汉娜·达伦

Upright birthing positions have also been found [to decrease the amount of time a woman is in labour.](https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD002006.pub4/full)研究还发现，直立分娩 [姿势能缩短女性分娩的时间。](https://www.cochranelibrary.com/cdsr/doi/10.1002/14651858.CD002006.pub4/full)

"Labouring upright and giving birth upright have advantages for both the mother and baby," wrote Dahlen in 2013. She listed a number of benefits, including more efficient contractions, less maternal pain, fewer forceps, vacuum births and episiotomies, as well as better oxygenation of the baby in the mother's uterus, as the aorta is not compressed by the uterus.达伦在2013年写道：“直立分娩和直立分娩对母婴双方都有好处。”她列举了许多好处，包括更高效的宫缩、减轻母体疼痛、减少产钳数量、真空分娩和会阴切开术，以及由于主动脉不受子宫压迫，胎儿在子宫内的氧气供应更好。

In 2011, Dahlen and her colleagues [conducted a study](https://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/springer/ijc/2011/00000001/00000004/art00004) on women in labour to understand whether the birth setting impacted the position women adopted while giving birth. They looked at two settings – birth centres, where supportive equipment such as balls, birth stools and bean bags were available, and delivery wards, where a medical hospital bed was the only option. 2011年，达伦和同事 [们开展了](https://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/springer/ijc/2011/00000001/00000004/art00004) 一项关于分娩妇女的研究，以了解分娩环境是否影响了女性分娩时的姿势。他们考察了两个环境——分娩中心，提供球、产凳和豆袋椅等辅助设备;以及分娩病房，医疗病床是唯一选择。

They found the women in birth centres were far more likely to adopt upright positions during the first and second stage of labour compared to a delivery ward setting – 82% of women did so in the birth centres compared to 25% in delivery wards.他们发现，分娩中心的女性在分娩第一和第二阶段更有可能采取直立姿势，而产房环境下则为25%。

![Getty Images The trend for giving birth on backs stuck from the 1600s, but many women find it uncomfortable (Credit: Getty Images)](https://ichef.bbci.co.uk/images/ic/1920xn/p0nb4c5l.jpg.webp)

The trend for giving birth on backs stuck from the 1600s, but many women find it uncomfortable (Credit: Getty Images) 背后分娩的趋势自17世纪起延续，但许多女性感到不适（图片来源：Getty Images）

There is now awareness in Western countries of the concept of active birth, says Balaskas – an approach that promotes the mother's ability to move freely and instinctively during labour, and adopt upright positions, rather than lying on her back tethered to machines and monitors. However caesarean rates [continue to rise](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10196217/) "alarmingly", she says. "In the UK active birth has influenced change in maternity services such as the option of midwife-led birth centres," she adds, noting these are usually within hospitals and custom-designed to give women freedom of movement and access to a birth pool. "This did not exist 50 years ago." Balaskas说，西方国家现在已经开始意识到主动分娩的概念——这是一种促进母亲在分娩过程中自由本能地活动的能力，并能保持直立姿势，而不是仰躺在机器和监视器上。然而，剖腹产率仍在“令人担忧” [地上升](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10196217/) ，她说。她补充道：“在英国，主动分娩影响了产科服务的变化，比如助产士主导的分娩中心的选择，”她补充道，这些中心通常设在医院内，旨在为女性提供自由行动和分娩池的使用权而设计。“50年前还不存在这样的东西。”

[Guidelines](https://www.bedfordshirehospitals.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/FOI-1889-Attachment-2.pdf) from the UK's National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (Nice) [advise that](https://www.bedfordshirehospitals.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/FOI-1889-Attachment-2.pdf) women in labour "should be discouraged from lying supine or semi-supine in the second stage of labour and should be encouraged to adopt any other position that they find most comfortable". 英国国家健康与护理卓越研究所（Nice）的 [指南](https://www.bedfordshirehospitals.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/FOI-1889-Attachment-2.pdf) [建议](https://www.bedfordshirehospitals.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/FOI-1889-Attachment-2.pdf) ，分娩中的女性“应避免在第二产程中仰卧或半仰卧，并鼓励采用她们觉得最舒适的其他姿势”。

As ever, knowledge is power, and the more women are informed about their birthing choices, the more comfortable they will be in choosing what feels right for them.一如既往，知识就是力量，女性越是了解自己的分娩选择，就越能安心选择最适合自己的方式。

"Public education about birth options will always be useful," says Eileen Hutton, a midwife turned academic who runs the midwifery education programme at Canada's McMaster University and [author of several papers on birthing](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/eclinm/article/PIIS2589-5370\(20\)30063-8/fulltext). "A look at the portrayal of birth in popular literature, television and film will quickly demonstrate how the birthing process is misrepresented. Providing a counterbalance could only be helpful." “关于分娩选项的公众教育永远是有用的，”助产士转学者、加拿大麦克马斯特大学助产教育项目负责人、并 [发表多篇分娩论文](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/eclinm/article/PIIS2589-5370\(20\)30063-8/fulltext) 的艾琳·哈顿说。“看看流行文学、电视和电影中分娩的描绘，很快就会发现分娩过程被误解了。提供一个平衡点，肯定会有帮助。”
