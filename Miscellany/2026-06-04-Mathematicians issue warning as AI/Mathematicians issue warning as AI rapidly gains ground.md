As artificial intelligence (AI) muscles its way into field after field, researchers have wrestled with what it means for the future of their disciplines. Few communities have felt that pressure more acutely than mathematicians, who have haplessly watched AI get frighteningly smart, frighteningly fast.随着人工智能（AI）强势涌入一个又一个领域，研究人员一直在纠结这对各自学科的未来意味着什么。没有哪个群体比数学家更真切地感受到这种压力——他们只能无奈地看着人工智能以惊人的速度变得异常智能。

Today, 16 math specialists have turned that unease into a public cry for help—and call to action. Part warning, part manifesto, the 11-page [Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics](https://leidendeclaration.ai/) cautions that unchecked automation threatens not only how math is practiced, but what the discipline stands for. It also lays out principles for using AI in ways that support, rather than erode, the field.如今，16位数学领域的专家将这种不安转化为公开的求助呼声与行动号召。这份长达11页的《莱顿人工智能与数学宣言》兼具警示意义与纲领性，它警告称，不受约束的自动化不仅会影响数学研究的开展方式，还会动摇这一学科的核心价值。同时，宣言也提出了一系列原则，指导人们以支持而非削弱该领域的方式运用人工智能。

For years, AI researchers have used math as a [proving ground for their models](https://www.science.org/content/article/how-will-we-know-if-ai-smart-enough-do-science). By their nature, math problems are easily gradable and offer an effectively unlimited training pool, and building AI models that can prove theorems is widely seen as a steppingstone toward higher performing reasoning systems. But AI is no longer confined to just straightforward tricks. Last month, [OpenAI reported one of its newest general-purpose reasoning models had independently solved a famous, 80-year-old conjecture](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/) —only the latest feat in a series of breakthroughs pushing AI to the frontiers of the subject.多年来，人工智能研究人员一直将数学作为其模型的 [试验场](https://www.science.org/content/article/how-will-we-know-if-ai-smart-enough-do-science) 。从本质上讲，数学问题评分标准明确，还能提供几乎无限的训练库，而打造能够证明定理的人工智能模型，也被广泛视为迈向更高性能推理系统的垫脚石。但人工智能早已不再局限于完成简单的任务。上个月， [OpenAI 表示其最新的通用推理模型已独立解决了一个著名的、已有80年历史的猜想](https://openai.com/index/model-disproves-discrete-geometry-conjecture/) ——这只是推动人工智能迈向该学科前沿的一系列突破中，最新的一项成就。

However, the declaration argues math is more than a machine for producing correct answers. The discipline, its authors believe, is a deeply human endeavor built on creativity, understanding, collaboration, and the pursuit of knowledge for its own sake. Those values often clash with the incentives driving AI development. “The tech industry proceeds in accordance with commercial logic, which is antithetical to the values of mathematics,” declaration co-author Michael Harris of Columbia University [told *The New York Times*](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/02/science/ai-mathematics-leiden-declaration.html).然而，这份声明认为数学远不止是得出正确答案的工具。声明的作者们认为，这门学科是一项根植于创造力、理解、合作以及为知识本身而追求知识的极具人文色彩的事业。这些价值观往往与推动人工智能发展的激励机制相冲突。哥伦比亚大学的迈克尔·哈里斯是该声明的联合作者之一，他对《纽约时报》表示：“科技行业遵循商业逻辑，这与数学的价值观背道而驰。”

The authors warn the consequences are already becoming visible. AI-generated papers could [overwhelm peer-review systems with low-quality work](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/930522/ai-research-papers-slop-peer-review-problem), make it difficult to assign proper credit for discoveries, and disadvantage researchers who choose not to rely on AI tools. The authors also raise ethical concerns about mathematicians’ work being used to train AI systems for military and surveillance purposes.作者们警告称，相关后果已逐渐显现。人工智能生成的论文可能会 [以低质量成果给同行评审系统造成巨大压力](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/930522/ai-research-papers-slop-peer-review-problem) ，难以对研究发现给予合理的认可，同时还会让那些选择不依赖人工智能工具的研究人员处于不利地位。作者们还对数学家的研究成果被用于训练用于军事和监控目的的人工智能系统这一问题提出了伦理方面的担忧。

The declaration, which is endorsed by the International Mathematical Union, the discipline’s leading global body, is now open for [signatures from individuals and organizations worldwide](https://leidendeclaration.ai/signatories). It will also be discussed at next month’s International Congress of Mathematicians in Philadelphia, which comes as governments are also beginning to take a greater interest in the advancement of AI systems. Today, for example, President Donald Trump signed [an executive order aimed at giving federal officials advance access to the most capable AI models before their public release](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/06/02/trump-signs-order-designed-give-government-early-look-powerful-ai-models/), in part so agencies can prepare for any security threats AI might enable.这份由国际数学界的全球领先机构——国际数学联盟背书的声明，目前已开放 [全球个人与组织的签名](https://leidendeclaration.ai/signatories) 。下月在费城举行的国际数学家大会也将对其展开讨论，与此同时，各国政府也开始对人工智能系统的发展给予更多关注。例如，今日唐纳德·特朗普总统签署了 [一项行政令，旨在让联邦官员在最先进的人工智能模型公开发布前提前获取访问权限](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/06/02/trump-signs-order-designed-give-government-early-look-powerful-ai-models/) ，部分原因是为了让各机构为人工智能可能带来的各类安全威胁做好准备。
