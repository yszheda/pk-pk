## The Правец (Pravetz): Bulgaria’s Apple II, Give or Take an Iron CurtainПравец（普拉韦茨）：差一道铁幕之隔的保加利亚版Apple II

I grew up using [Правец](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravetz_computers) (Pravetz) computers — forgive the Cyrillic, but we Bulgarians invented the alphabet, even though half the Slavic world claims the credit, and besides, it makes any noun look like classified military hardware. Every Bulgarian of a certain age used one. The Правец 82 was the machine in my school, with its yellow plastic case, black keyboard, red RESET key, and the unmistakable aura of a computer that had been reverse-engineered from a capitalist original by engineers who had never seen Cupertino and didn’t need to.我是伴着 [普拉韦茨](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravetz_computers) （Pravetz）电脑长大的——原谅我用了西里尔字母，但我们保加利亚人发明了字母表，尽管半个斯拉夫世界都声称这是他们的功劳。而且，用西里尔字母拼写任何名词，都像是在标注军事机密。每个年龄段的保加利亚人都用过这种电脑。学校里用的是普拉韦茨82型电脑，它有着黄色的塑料外壳、黑色的键盘、红色的复位键，还有一种 unmistakable 的气质——这是工程师们从未见过库比蒂诺的原版电脑，却仅凭逆向工程造出的资本主义制式设备，而他们也根本没必要见过。

My first encounter with a personal computer was typing in a BASIC program that drew [Lissajous figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lissajous_curve) in hi-res graphics. I was in the fourth grade. It was 1987. But I am digressing.我第一次接触个人电脑，是在高分辨率图形模式下输入一段能绘制 [利萨如图形](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lissajous_curve) 的BASIC程序。那时我上四年级，时间是1987年。不过我有点跑题了。

The story of the Bulgarian computer is worth telling properly. In 1979, engineer [Ivan Marangozov](https://3seaseurope.com/pravetz-ivan-marangozov-inventor-bulgaria-computer/) at the [Institute of Technical Cybernetics and Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravetz_computers) (ИТКР) in Sofia built the IMKO-1 — the first Bulgarian personal computer. It was a clone of the [Apple II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_II), and “clone” is doing some diplomatic heavy lifting here: the ROM was identical, the schematics were identical, the [6502](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOS_Technology_6502) CPU ran at the same 1 MHz. The differences were a metal case that could double as ballast, a linear power supply heavy enough to qualify as exercise equipment, and the replacement of lowercase Latin with uppercase Cyrillic — because behind the Iron Curtain, you didn’t do lowercase. The keyboard used 7 bits for character codes, so Cyrillic overlapped with Latin lowercase. A limitation, but also an engineering decision that had a certain brutal elegance: you get one alphabet at a time, comrade, and you will type in capitals.保加利亚计算机的故事值得好好讲述。1979年，索菲亚的 [伊万·马拉戈佐夫](https://3seaseurope.com/pravetz-ivan-marangozov-inventor-bulgaria-computer/) 工程师在 [技术控制论与机器人研究所](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravetz_computers) （ИТКР）研制出了IMKO-1——保加利亚第一台个人计算机。它是 [Apple II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_II) 的仿制品，而这里的“仿制品”实则是个委婉说法：其只读存储器（ROM）完全一致，电路图完全相同， [6502](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOS_Technology_6502) 中央处理器（CPU）也以相同的1兆赫兹频率运行。不同之处仅在于：一个可兼作压载物的金属外壳、一款重到足以当作健身器材的线性电源，以及将小写拉丁字母替换为大写西里尔字母——因为在铁幕背后，人们从不使用小写字母。键盘的字符编码采用7位，因此西里尔字母与拉丁小写字母产生了重叠。这是一种局限，但也是一项颇具冷峻美感的工程决策：同志，你一次只能使用一种字母表，而且必须用大写字母进行输入。

The rumours of how this was accomplished are better than the facts. The Bulgarian intelligence services allegedly sent operatives to procure Apple IIs from the West — less James Bond, more cartoon characters getting drunk on capitalist Coca-Cola while trying to buy a computer with a heavy accent and a suitcase full of [leva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_lev) (the Bulgarian currency, not then known for its convertibility). More interesting is what happened next: an institute in Sofia was reportedly tasked with decapping the ICs, lifting the netlists under a microscope, and reproducing them with socialist lithography — the equipment for which was probably lifted from the Dutch. The reasonable question is why arguably clever people went through all of this when designing an ALU from scratch is not that difficult, but we will leave this armchair philosophy question to the LLMs. They have the confidence for it, if not the answer.关于这一过程的传闻远比事实精彩。据称，保加利亚情报部门曾派遣特工从西方采购苹果II型电脑——这场景少了些007式的惊险，更像是卡通角色一边喝着资本主义的可口可乐，一边操着浓重的口音、提着装满列弗（保加利亚货币，当时并不以可兑换性著称）的行李箱，试图购买电脑。更有意思的是后续发生的事：据称索菲亚的一家研究所负责对集成电路进行开封，在显微镜下提取网表，再用社会主义的光刻技术进行复刻——而这套设备很可能是从荷兰偷来的。一个合理的问题是，既然从零设计算术逻辑单元并不那么困难，为何那些堪称聪明的人要大费周章？不过我们还是把这个纯理论的哲学问题留给大语言模型吧。即便没有答案，它们也有足够的底气来探讨这个问题。

Today, people do this sort of thing voluntarily and for fun. The [6502.org](http://6502.org/) community hosts [dozens of homebrew computer projects](http://6502.org/homebuilt) — enthusiasts building 6502-based machines from scratch on breadboards, complete with VGA output and SD card storage, using parts that are still in production. The [MOnSter 6502](https://monster6502.com/) takes it further: a fully functional 6502 processor built from 3,218 discrete transistors on a circuit board the size of a dinner plate, with LEDs showing the state of every register. What the Bulgarian state did with an institute and a five-year plan, hobbyists now do in garages on weekends.如今，人们做这类事是出于自愿，只为取乐。 [6502.org](http://6502.org/) 社区汇聚了 [数十个自制电脑项目](http://6502.org/homebuilt) ——爱好者们在面包板上从零开始搭建基于6502芯片的电脑，配备VGA输出和SD卡存储，所用元件仍在量产。 [MOnSter 6502](https://monster6502.com/) 项目更是登峰造极：它在一块餐盘大小的电路板上，用3218个分立晶体管打造出一台功能完整的6502处理器，每个寄存器的状态都通过发光二极管显示。当年保加利亚通过一个研究所和五年规划实现的事，如今爱好者们在自家车库里利用周末就能完成。

[Marangozov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravetz_computers) was, depending on your perspective, either rightfully accused of cloning the Apple II or laudably credited with delivering computing to an entire country that couldn’t buy one. The truth is both, and neither is shameful. Apple II schematics were published. Steve Wozniak *intended* the design to be understandable. What Marangozov and his team did was take a published design, source the components (Bulgarian and Soviet clones of American chips — clones all the way down), adapt the character set, and manufacture hundreds of thousands of units that shipped to every school and scientific institute in the [Eastern Bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Bloc). By the mid-1980s, Bulgaria was producing 40 percent of the personal computers used in [COMECON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comecon) countries. Not bad for a country whose communist leader [Todor Zhivkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todor_Zhivkov) — a peasant’s son turned printer’s apprentice who rose through the party ranks on the strength of Soviet patronage and the convenient absence of anyone more threatening — happened to have been born in the village that gave the computers their name. Правец was a hamlet of no consequence until Zhivkov turned it into a town by decree in the 1960s; by the 1980s it was assembling the flagship technology of the Eastern Bloc. One does not need a diagnostic engine to detect the fault in this particular circuit of patronage.从不同角度来看， [马拉戈佐夫](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pravetz_computers) 要么被合理指责抄袭了Apple II，要么因将计算机带给整个买不起Apple II的国家而备受赞誉。事实是两者皆有，且两者都不可耻。Apple II的电路图被公之于众。史蒂夫·沃兹尼亚克 *本意* 是让该设计易于理解。马拉戈佐夫和他的团队所做的，就是采用这份公开的设计图纸，采购元器件（保加利亚和苏联仿制的美国芯片——从里到外全是仿制品），调整字符集，然后生产出数十万台设备，发往 [东欧集团](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Bloc) 的每一所学校和科研机构。到20世纪80年代中期，保加利亚生产的个人电脑占 [经互会](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comecon) 国家所用个人电脑的40%。对于一个其共产主义领导人 [托多尔·日夫科夫](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todor_Zhivkov) ——一个农民之子出身的印刷工学徒，凭借苏联的庇护和恰好没有更具威胁的对手这一优势在党内步步高升——的国家而言，这已经相当不错了。而巧的是，日夫科夫就出生在给这些电脑命名的那个村庄。普拉韦茨村原本是个无足轻重的小村落，直到日夫科夫在20世纪60年代通过政令将其升格为城镇；到20世纪80年代，这里开始组装东欧集团的旗舰级技术产品。无需专业的诊断引擎，也能看出这一权力庇护链条中存在的明显漏洞。

The later models improved substantially. The Правец 8M integrated a [Z80](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zilog_Z80) alongside the 6502, letting it run [CP/M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CP/M). The 8A was a proper [Apple IIe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_IIe) clone with expandable memory. The military version had an integrated terminal design, because of course it did. There was even a Правец 8D, which broke ranks entirely — it was a clone of the British [Oric Atmos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oric_Atmos), presumably because even Bulgarian engineers sometimes want variety.后续的型号有了大幅改进。Правец 8M 在 6502 处理器的基础上集成了 [Z80](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zilog_Z80) 处理器，使其能够运行 [CP/M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CP/M) 操作系统。8A 是一款正宗的 [Apple IIe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_IIe) 兼容机，配备可扩展内存。军用版本采用了集成式终端设计，这也是意料之中的事。甚至还有一款 Правец 8D，完全另辟蹊径——它是英国 [Oric Atmos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oric_Atmos) 的兼容机，想必就连保加利亚的工程师有时也想换换口味。

The point is not that Bulgaria copied Apple. The point is that reverse engineering — understanding a design well enough to reproduce and adapt it — was how an entire generation of engineers learned computing. We didn’t have access to Stanford or MIT. We had schematics, soldering irons, and a cheerful disregard for intellectual property law that was, in fairness, philosophically consistent with the economic system. The Правец was my first computer. Everything I know about hardware starts there: with a 6502, 48 kilobytes of RAM, and a cassette recorder that worked when it felt like it.问题的关键不在于保加利亚复刻了苹果公司。而在于逆向工程——深入理解一项设计并实现其复刻与改进——是整整一代工程师学习计算机技术的方式。我们无缘进入斯坦福大学或麻省理工学院。我们拥有的只是电路图、电烙铁，以及一种对知识产权法毫不在意的洒脱态度；平心而论，这种态度在当时的经济体系下，从理念上看也是自洽的。这台保加利亚产电脑是我的第一台计算机。我所有的硬件知识都始于它：一台搭载6502处理器、拥有48千字节内存，还有一台时灵时不灵的盒式磁带录音机的电脑。

## Hayes and the ISCAS Circuits: What Were They For?Hayes 与 ISCAS 电路：它们是用来做什么的？

Now here is a story about reverse engineering that is less well-known outside the EDA community but is, in its own way, just as delightful.现在有一个关于反向工程的故事，在 EDA 社区之外鲜为人知，但从某种意义上说，它同样令人着迷。

The [ISCAS-85 benchmarks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISCAS) are the standard test circuits for digital design research. If you work on test generation, fault diagnosis, synthesis, or timing analysis, you have used them. They were released by [Franc Brglez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISCAS) and Hisashi Fujiwara in 1985 as a “neutral netlist of 10 combinational benchmark circuits” — gate-level descriptions of real circuits, stripped of context, for the research community to use as common benchmarks.[ISCAS-85 基准测试集](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISCAS) 是数字设计研究的标准测试电路。如果你从事测试生成、故障诊断、综合或时序分析相关工作，你一定使用过它们。该基准测试集由 [弗朗克·布格尔茨](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISCAS) 和藤原尚于1985年发布，名为“10个组合基准电路的中性网表”——这些是真实电路的门级描述，去除了上下文信息，供研究社区用作通用基准测试。

There was just one problem. Nobody told the research community what the circuits *did*.问题只有一个。没人告诉研究界这些电路 *究竟是做什么的* 。

For fourteen years, thousands of researchers ran experiments on c432, c499, c880, c1355, c2670, c3540, c5315, c6288, and c7552. They generated test patterns for them. They diagnosed faults in them. They timed them, synthesized them, mapped them to FPGAs. They published papers about them. And nobody — *nobody* — knew what these circuits were actually supposed to compute.十四年来，数千名研究人员对 c432、c499、c880、c1355、c2670、c3540、c5315、c6288 和 c7552 开展了实验。他们为这些电路生成测试向量，诊断其中的故障，对其进行时序分析、综合处理，将它们映射到现场可编程门阵列（FPGA）上，还发表了相关论文。但没有人—— *没有人* ——知道这些电路实际的计算功能是什么。

Then in 1999, [Mark Hansen, Hakan Yalcin, and John P. Hayes](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/785838/) at the University of Michigan did what should have been done from the start. They reverse-engineered the lot. Their paper, “ [Unveiling the ISCAS-85 Benchmarks: A Case Study in Reverse Engineering](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1109/54.785838),” published in IEEE Design & Test, is a masterpiece of detective work. They took each gate-level netlist, partitioned it into standard RTL blocks, identified the function of each block, and reconstructed the high-level architecture. The methodology was elegantly practical: Hayes assigned each circuit to a PhD student. Cheap labour, and almost certainly cheaper per insight than an LLM.1999年，密歇根大学的 [马克·汉森、哈坎·耶尔钦和约翰·P·海斯](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/785838/) 做了一件本该从一开始就该做的事。他们对整套内容进行了逆向工程。他们发表在《IEEE 设计与测试》上的论文《 [揭秘ISCAS-85基准测试：逆向工程案例研究](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1109/54.785838) 》是侦探式研究的杰作。他们获取每个门级网表，将其划分为标准寄存器传输级（RTL）模块，确定每个模块的功能，然后重构出高层架构。该方法精妙且实用：海斯将每个电路分配给一名博士研究生。这是廉价的人力成本，而且每获得一个研究见解的成本几乎肯定比使用大型语言模型（LLM）更低。

The results were revelatory. c432 turned out to be a [27-channel interrupt controller](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c432.html). c880 was an [8-bit ALU](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c880.html). c6288 was a [16×16 multiplier](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c6288.html). c7552 was a [32-bit adder/comparator](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c7552/c7552.html). c499 and c1355 were both [32-bit single-error-correcting circuits](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c499.html) — the same function, different implementations. These weren’t abstract mathematical constructs. They were real designs, ripped from real hardware, and for a decade and a half the research community had been studying them the way archaeologists study pottery shards: knowing the shape but not the purpose.结果具有启发性。c432 是一款 [27通道中断控制器](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c432.html) 。c880 是一个 [8位算术逻辑单元](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c880.html) 。c6288 是一个 [16×16乘法器](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c6288.html) 。c7552 是一个 [32位加法器/比较器](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c7552/c7552.html) 。c499 和 c1355 均为 [32位单纠错电路](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/doc/trunk/c499.html) ——功能相同，实现方式不同。这些并非抽象的数学结构，而是源自真实硬件的实际设计。十五年来，研究界一直像考古学家研究陶片一样研究它们：只知其形，不知其用。

Hayes’s contribution is profound and under-appreciated. By recovering the behavioral specifications, he gave the community something it had never had: the ability to test at the functional level, to verify synthesis results against intended behaviour, to use hierarchical structure for more efficient test generation. The [high-level models](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/) are still available, complete with annotated schematics and structural Verilog, and they remain useful tools nearly three decades later.海耶斯的贡献意义深远却未得到足够重视。通过还原行为规范，他为该领域带来了前所未有的东西：具备在功能层面进行测试的能力，能够对照预期行为验证综合结果，还能利用层级结构更高效地生成测试用例。 [高级模型](https://tumbleweed.nu/r/iscas.restore/) 至今仍可获取，附带带注释的原理图和结构化 Verilog 代码，近三十年来一直是实用的工具。

## Why This Matters 为何重要

There is a thread connecting the Правец, the ISCAS reversal, and the work I’ve been doing with [LyDiA](https://gitlab.llama.gs/constraints/lydia) and [qbf-designer](https://gitlab.llama.gs/logic/qbf-designer).有一条线索将 Правец、中科院逆转以及我一直在做的与 [LyDiA](https://gitlab.llama.gs/constraints/lydia) 和 [qbf-designer](https://gitlab.llama.gs/logic/qbf-designer) 相关的工作串联起来。

Reverse engineering is synthesis in reverse. When Hayes looked at c6288 and deduced it was a multiplier, he was doing — by hand, with extraordinary patience — what a diagnostic engine does automatically: given a circuit and its behaviour, determine its function. When Marangozov looked at the Apple II and built the IMKO-1, he was doing technology transfer through structural analysis. And when [Johan de Kleer](https://dekleer.org/) described synthesis as “diagnosing a circuit into existence,” he was observing that the mathematical machinery is the same in both directions. Start with a broken specification (nothing works, every gate is “faulty”), and ask: what collection of “repairs” (gate placements) would make the circuit compute the desired function?逆向工程是反向的综合推理。当海耶斯观察 c6288 并推断出它是一个乘法器时，他凭借非凡的耐心手动完成了诊断引擎自动执行的工作：给定一个电路及其行为，确定其功能。当马拉贡佐夫观察苹果二代并造出 IMKO-1 时，他是通过结构分析进行技术转移。而当 [约翰·德克勒](https://dekleer.org/) 将综合推理描述为“将电路诊断出来”时，他指出了数学机制在两个方向上是相同的。从一个有缺陷的规格（一切都无法工作，每个门电路都“存在故障”）开始，然后提出问题：什么样的“修复”组合（门电路的布局）能让该电路实现预期的功能？

This is the ∃∀ structure I discussed in [Diagnosing Circuits into Existence](https://llama.gs/blog/index.php/2026/04/06/diagnosing-circuits-into-existence/) — the same quantifier alternation, the same miter-based equivalence checking, the same PSPACE-hard satisfaction problems. Diagnosis, synthesis, and reverse engineering are three faces of the same formal object. The only difference is which variables you fix and which you solve for.这就是我在 [《诊断电路使其存在》](https://llama.gs/blog/index.php/2026/04/06/diagnosing-circuits-into-existence/) 中讨论的∃∀结构——相同的量词交替、相同的基于斜接的等价性检查、相同的PSPACE难满足问题。诊断、综合与逆向工程是同一形式对象的三个不同面向。唯一的区别在于你固定哪些变量，又求解哪些变量。

The ISCAS-85 benchmarks are, incidentally, the circuits that LyDiA diagnoses. When I demonstrate model-based diagnosis on c432, I am diagnosing a 27-channel interrupt controller — I just didn’t know that for the first several years of my PhD. Thank you, Professor Hayes.顺便说一下，ISCAS-85 基准测试就是 LyDiA 用来诊断的电路。当我在 c432 上演示基于模型的诊断时，我诊断的是一个27通道的中断控制器——只是在我读博的头几年，我并不知道这一点。谢谢海耶斯教授。

## The Moral 核心启示

The current approach to technology is to build something forward: throw compute at a model, train it on everything, and see what comes out. Reverse engineering goes the other way: take something that exists, understand its structure, and extract meaning. One approach requires billions of dollars and produces systems that cannot explain themselves. The other requires patience and produces understanding.当下对待技术的思路是向前构建：为模型投入算力，在海量数据上对其进行训练，然后观察能产出什么。逆向工程则反其道而行之：以已存在的事物为对象，探究其结构并挖掘其中的价值。一种方法需要数十亿美元投入，产出的系统却无法自我解释；另一种方法则需要耐心，最终能带来真正的理解。

Bulgaria built a computer industry on reverse engineering. Hayes rebuilt the foundations of benchmark-driven EDA research by reversing fourteen-year-old circuits. I am building a company on the formal connection between taking circuits apart and putting them together.保加利亚通过反向工程建立了计算机产业。海斯通过拆解有十四年历史的电路，为基准测试驱动的电子设计自动化研究奠定了新基础。我则正基于拆解与重组电路之间的形式关联，创办一家公司。

None of this requires a strategic enabler for enterprise-wide transformation. It requires mathematics, a soldering iron, and the willingness to look at something carefully until you understand what it does.这一切都不需要一个推动企业全面转型的战略赋能者。它需要的是数学知识、一把电烙铁，以及愿意仔细审视某样事物直到弄懂其原理的耐心。
