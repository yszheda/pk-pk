At the slightest touch of the reins, he felt a familiarity that shook him. It was 2016, and polo player Adolfo Cambiaso — considered the best in the world — was riding for the first time on a genetic clone of Cuartetera, his flagship mare. The same explosive start, the same agility in the curves, the same sustained stride in the long sprints. “It was the same,” he recalls. “Same movements, same head.... I couldn’t believe it.” It took only a few seconds for him to realize that his gamble — which many had dismissed as nonsense — had paid off.缰绳刚一轻触，他便涌起一种熟悉之感，这让他心头一震。那是2016年，被公认为世界最佳马球运动员的阿道夫·坎比亚索，首次骑乘自己的旗舰母马“库阿泰拉”的基因克隆马。它有着同样爆发力的起步、过弯时同样的敏捷，长距离冲刺时也保持着同样的步幅。“简直一模一样，”他回忆道，“动作一样，头部神态也一样……我简直不敢相信。”仅过了几秒，他便意识到，自己这场被许多人斥为无稽之谈的冒险，终于获得了成功。

Cambiaso, now 50, had seen before anyone else, back in 2006, the opportunity to preserve the genetics of his most exceptional [horses](https://knowablemagazine.org/content/article/living-world/2022/tale-domesticated-horse) through cloning and thus perpetuate his La Dolfina team, from the province of Buenos Aires, at the top of polo for generations.现年50岁的坎比亚索早在2006年就比其他人更早看到了通过克隆保存自己最杰出 [马匹](https://knowablemagazine.org/content/article/living-world/2022/tale-domesticated-horse) 基因的机会，从而让自己来自布宜诺斯艾利斯省的多拉芬纳球队能在马球界世代稳居顶尖位置。

That year, in the middle of the Palermo Open final — the ultimate temple of polo — his horse Aiken Cura suffered a devastating fracture and had to be put down. But before saying goodbye, Cambiaso made an unusual request to the veterinarians. “Just in case, before they put him to sleep, I said, ‘Let’s save some cells.’” It was nothing more than a hunch. He had heard the story of Dolly the sheep, the first mammal cloned from an adult cell, and the idea stuck in his mind.那一年，在帕尔马公开赛决赛的正中心——马球的终极圣殿——他的爱驹艾肯·库拉遭遇了严重的骨折，不得不实施安乐死。但在告别之前，坎比亚索向兽医们提出了一个不同寻常的请求。“以防万一，在给它实施安乐死之前，我说，‘咱们先保存一些细胞吧。’”这不过是一个直觉。他曾听过多莉羊的故事——第一只从成年体细胞克隆出来的哺乳动物，这个想法便在他的脑海里扎了根。

That intuition two decades ago led to a radical change in the world of polo. La Dolfina, now with more than 150 cloned horses, has established unprecedented dominance, and Argentina has become the world center for horse cloning, far ahead of the United States and Europe. Over the years, laboratories have refined the procedure and improved the success rate — although it remains low.二十年前的这一想法引发了马球界的一场颠覆性变革。如今拥有150多匹克隆马的拉多尔菲纳队确立了前所未有的统治地位，阿根廷也成为了全球马克隆技术中心，遥遥领先于美国和欧洲。多年来，相关实验室不断优化克隆流程、提高成功率——尽管成功率依旧偏低。

Hence the high costs: Cloning a horse involves far more investment than that required to breed a good specimen the traditional way. And although equine cloning is no longer a rarity but a mature industry, the ethical dilemmas surrounding it — animal welfare, fair competition and the extent to which biology should be manipulated for sporting purposes — still persist.因此成本高昂：克隆一匹马所需的投入远高于传统方式培育优良种马的成本。尽管马匹克隆已不再是稀罕事，而是一门成熟的产业，但围绕它的伦理困境——动物福利、公平竞争以及出于体育目的操控生物学的边界——依然存在。

## Making genetic copies of mammals 制造哺乳动物的基因复制品

In all mammals, including horses, the cloning process is similar. First, a somatic cell —a nonreproductive cell such as a skin cell — is taken from the animal to be cloned, and its nucleus, which contains the genetic information, is extracted. At the same time, scientists take an egg cell from the same species and remove its nuclear DNA, in a process called enucleation. The nucleus that was extracted from the first cell is then inserted into this “empty” egg cell.包括马在内的所有哺乳动物，其克隆过程都大同小异。首先，从待克隆的动物身上取出一个体细胞——即皮肤细胞这类非生殖细胞，并提取出其含有遗传信息的细胞核。与此同时，科学家从同一物种的动物身上获取卵细胞，并通过一种名为去核的操作去除其细胞核内的DNA。随后，将从第一个细胞中提取出的细胞核植入这个“空”的卵细胞中。

Next, that egg with its new nucleus is stimulated chemically or by electrical impulses to begin dividing and form an embryo. The embryo is cultured in vitro for seven or eight days until it reaches the blastocyst stage, at which point it is implanted in a female who will carry the pregnancy to term.接下来，这个带有新细胞核的卵子会受到化学物质或电脉冲的刺激，开始分裂并形成胚胎。胚胎在体外培养七到八天，直至发育到囊胚阶段，随后被植入一名女性体内，由其完成整个妊娠过程。

The method is called somatic cell nuclear transfer. It was used to [create Dolly the sheep in 1996](https://www.nature.com/articles/385810a0), a milestone that proved it was possible to “reset” an animal’s DNA and bring it to an embryonic state capable of development, although with major challenges along the way.这种方法被称为体细胞核移植。1996年，它被用于 [培育出绵羊多莉](https://www.nature.com/articles/385810a0) ，这一里程碑式的成果证明了可以“重置”动物的DNA并使其恢复到具有发育能力的胚胎状态，尽管这一过程中存在诸多重大挑战。

![Graphic showing the process of cloning a horse.](https://knowablemagazine.org/docserver/fulltext/g-how-to-clone-horse.png)

Horse clones — like those of other mammals — are obtained through the technique of somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT). The nucleus of a non-reproductive cell from the animal to be cloned is inserted into an egg cell that has had its own nuclear DNA removed. After activation, the embryo begins to divide. When it reaches the blastocyst stage, it is implanted into a recipient mare, which will carry the pregnancy to term. 马的克隆与其他哺乳动物的克隆一样，均通过体细胞核移植技术实现。将待克隆动物的非生殖细胞核植入去除自身核DNA的卵母细胞中，经激活后胚胎开始分裂，发育至囊胚阶段时被植入代孕母马体内，由其完成整个妊娠过程。

Since Dolly, more than 25 species of mammals have been successfully cloned, including cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, horses, dogs, cats and wild species such as gray wolves and ferrets. The main limitations of cloning lie in the fact that the transferred nucleus does not always manage to reprogram itself completely, and that the mitochondria of the recipient egg and the genome of the transferred nucleus may have incompatibilities, explains Andrés Gambini, a veterinarian specializing in animal reproduction at the University of Queensland in Australia.自多莉羊诞生以来，已有超过25种哺乳动物成功被克隆，包括牛、羊、山羊、猪、马、狗、猫，以及灰狼、雪貂等野生动物。澳大利亚昆士兰大学专攻动物繁殖的兽医安德烈斯·甘比尼解释说，克隆技术的主要局限在于，移植的细胞核并非总能完成完全的重编程，且受体卵子的线粒体与移植细胞核的基因组之间可能存在不兼容性。

[Mitochondria](https://knowablemagazine.org/content/article/technology/2025/mitochondrial-therapy-to-treat-damaged-organs) are small structures within cells that produce the energy necessary for them to function. They contain DNA of their own, and for this reason clones are not entirely genetically identical to each other. Although they share the same nuclear DNA from the original animal, the mitochondrial DNA will differ because it comes not from the original animal but from the female oocyte that was used in the cloning process. Though its DNA represents a tiny fraction of the total genome, it plays a critical role in the cell, and so those small variations can translate into differences of function and appearance, says Sebastián Demyda Peyrás, an equine geneticist at the University of Cordoba, Spain.[线粒体](https://knowablemagazine.org/content/article/technology/2025/mitochondrial-therapy-to-treat-damaged-organs) 是细胞内的微小结构，负责为细胞功能提供所需的能量。它们拥有自身的DNA，正因如此，克隆体之间并非完全基因相同。尽管它们与原动物共享相同的核DNA，但线粒体DNA会有所不同——因为它并非源自原动物，而是来自克隆过程中使用的雌性卵母细胞。西班牙科尔多瓦大学的马类遗传学家塞巴斯蒂安·德米达·佩拉斯表示，尽管线粒体DNA在整个基因组中占比极小，但它在细胞中发挥着关键作用，因此这些微小差异会转化为功能和外观上的不同。

In addition, he says, “epigenetic patterns in cloning are altered much more frequently than in natural pregnancies. Both factors — mitochondrial replacement and epigenetics — influence the higher rate of miscarriages and the number of clones born with health problems, placental abnormalities or severe physical problems.” (Epigenetics refers to the way that genes may be turned on or off due to the addition or removal of small chemical groups, without affecting the DNA sequence itself.) 此外，他表示：“克隆中的表观遗传模式改变的频率远高于自然妊娠。线粒体替代和表观遗传这两个因素，共同导致了流产率升高，以及许多克隆个体出生时存在健康问题、胎盘异常或严重的身体缺陷。”（表观遗传指的是，基因会因小型化学基团的添加或移除而被开启或关闭，这一过程并不影响DNA序列本身。）

Despite its technical challenges, cloning has opened the door to many applications, such as species conservation, livestock breeding and even attempts to bring back extinct species. In the field of conservation, genetic material stored in biobanks can be used to [reestablish a healthy breeding population](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-animal-071423-093523), improving genetic diversity and increasing the number of animals that can reproduce, says Aleona Swegen, a reproductive veterinarian at the University of Newcastle, Australia, and coauthor of a 2024 overview of cloning in conservation in the *Annual Review of Animal Biosciences*. The main challenges, she says, are the need to find an adequate number of oocytes from closely related species and suitable surrogate mothers for gestation.尽管克隆技术面临诸多技术难题，但它已为众多应用打开了大门，例如物种保护、家畜育种，甚至还有复活灭绝物种的尝试。澳大利亚纽卡斯尔大学的生殖兽医、2024年《动物生物科学年度评论》中一篇关于克隆在保护领域应用综述的合著者阿莱奥娜·斯威根表示，在保护生物学领域，生物库中储存的遗传物质可用于 [重建健康的繁殖种群](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-animal-071423-093523) ，从而提高遗传多样性，增加可繁殖动物的数量。她指出，主要挑战在于需要从近缘物种中获取足够数量的卵母细胞，以及找到合适的代孕母体来完成妊娠。

Cloning also continues to face challenges in domestic animals. A critical moment, different for each species, is when the embryo stops relying on RNA and proteins from the maternal egg and begins to use its own DNA, says Pablo Ross, chief scientific officer at [STgenetics](https://www.stgen.com/default.aspx?language=english-english&type=both), a global leader in bovine reproductive biotechnology, and an animal geneticist at the University of California, Davis. In cattle, this step, known as embryonic genome activation, occurs when the embryo has between eight and 16 cells, while in horses it occurs when it has four to eight cells.克隆技术在家畜身上也依然面临挑战。加州大学戴维斯分校的动物遗传学家、全球牛生殖生物技术领域领军企业STgenetics</b>首席科学官巴勃罗·罗斯表示，每个物种都有一个关键节点，此时胚胎会停止依赖母体卵子中的RNA和蛋白质，转而开始使用自身的DNA。在牛身上，这一被称为胚胎基因组激活的步骤发生在胚胎拥有8至16个细胞时，而在马身上则发生在胚胎有4至8个细胞时。

In horses, as in other species, public protocols for cloning already exist, but success depends on the expertise of the team and technical details that are not always included in manuals. A critical issue in horses is the source of the oocytes. One alternative is to obtain them from the ovaries of dead mares collected at slaughterhouses, although they can also be extracted from live females by transvaginal aspiration, a more invasive procedure but [with better success rates.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37011429/)与其他物种一样，马的公开克隆方案已经存在，但成功与否取决于研究团队的专业技术水平以及手册中未必涵盖的技术细节。马克隆的一个关键问题是卵母细胞的来源。一种获取途径是从屠宰场收集的死母马卵巢中提取卵母细胞，不过也可通过经阴道抽吸术从活体母马体内获取，这种方法侵入性更强，但 [成功率更高。](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37011429/)

With oocytes obtained using transvaginal aspiration, the proportion of embryos that reach the blastocyst stage is around 35 percent, compared to just 26 percent in oocytes obtained from slaughterhouses. And the difference widens in later stages: Among mares that remain pregnant after day 42, just over half of pregnancies derived from eggs obtained by transvaginal aspiration result in healthy foals, compared to just one in 10 when the eggs come from slaughterhouses.通过阴道穿刺取卵获得的卵母细胞，发育至囊胚期的胚胎比例约为35%，而从屠宰场获取的卵母细胞这一比例仅为26%。这种差距在后续阶段进一步扩大：在42天后仍保持妊娠的母马中，通过阴道穿刺取卵获得的卵子所形成的妊娠，仅有略超一半能产下健康的小马驹，而屠宰场来源的卵子所形成的妊娠，这一比例仅为十分之一。

![A graphic comparing the success of equine cloning according to the origin of the oocytes.](https://knowablemagazine.org/docserver/fulltext/g-oocytes-collected-mares.png)

This graph compares the success of equine cloning according to the origin of the oocytes. Those obtained from live mares by transvaginal aspiration show better results than those collected in slaughterhouses post mortem. 该图表根据卵母细胞的来源对比了马克隆的成功率。通过阴道穿刺术从活体母马体内获取的卵母细胞，其效果优于从屠宰场死后收集的卵母细胞。

In recent years, several advances have improved horse cloning, says Flávio Vieira Meirelles, a reproductive biotechnologist at the University of São Paulo, Brazil. These mainly involve methods for activation of the egg after inserting the nucleus, and cultivation conditions for the embryo. In addition, the efficiency with which the genes of the donated nucleus are reprogrammed — a process that is carried out by chemicals in the cytoplasm of the egg — has improved.巴西圣保罗大学的生殖生物学家弗拉维奥·维埃拉·梅雷莱斯表示，近年来有多项技术进展推动了马匹克隆技术的提升。这些进展主要集中在植入细胞核后激活卵子的方法以及胚胎的培养条件上。此外，供体细胞核基因的重编程效率也有所提高，这一过程由卵子细胞质中的化学物质完成。

Greater success, too, is achieved when the donated nuclei come from adult stem cells — which are capable of renewing themselves and transforming into various tissues within an organ — compared with nuclei from fully differentiated cells from a tissue such as skin. The differentiated cells carry more “memory” of their original function. Also, cells from young animals tend to respond better than those from older animals. And, of course, the reproductive capacity of the female surrogate mothers plays a role, too. Even with everything optimized, the birth rate per transferred embryo is low in large mammals, ranging from 3 to 10 percent.当供体细胞核来自成体干细胞时，所取得的成功率也更高——成体干细胞能够自我更新并转化为器官内的各种组织——这与来自皮肤等组织中完全分化细胞的细胞核相比更具优势。分化细胞携带更多其原始功能的“记忆”。此外，幼年动物的细胞通常比老年动物的细胞反应更好。当然，代孕母畜的繁殖能力也起到一定作用。即便所有条件都优化到位，大型哺乳动物每移植胚胎的出生率仍较低，仅为3%至10%。

Despite the difficulties, cloning has expanded and today fuels an industry as diverse as it is disturbing: Exceptional cattle are cloned to produce high-quality meat, companion animals are cloned for owners who are unable to say goodbye to their pets (and are willing to invest $50,000 in a clone of their dog or cat), and extraordinary polo horses are cloned to try to ensure victory on the field.尽管困难重重，克隆技术仍在不断发展，如今已催生了一个既多样又令人不安的产业：优质牛只被克隆以产出高品质肉类，伴侣动物被克隆给那些无法与宠物告别（且愿意为克隆的猫狗投资5万美元）的主人，而顶级马球马也被克隆，以期在赛场上确保胜利。

## Argentina: Epicenter of horse cloning阿根廷：马克隆的中心

In 2010, while completing his doctoral thesis on cloning at the University of Buenos Aires, biotechnologist Gabriel Vichera came across a news story that shook him: Adolfo Cambiaso had auctioned off one of the clones of his star mare, Cuartetera, for an eye-watering $800,000. It was the first time that a cloned animal bred for high athletic performance had been presented as a high-value asset, and in a market where polo horses can be worth anywhere from $50,000 to nearly $1 million for extraordinary specimens, the sale made it clear that there was money waiting to be made.2010年，生物技术学家加夫列尔·比切拉正在布宜诺斯艾利斯大学完成关于克隆技术的博士论文，此时他看到了一则令他震惊的新闻：阿道夫·坎比亚索将其明星母马库阿泰拉的一只克隆马拍卖，成交价高达令人咋舌的80万美元。这是首次将为优异运动表现培育的克隆动物作为高价值资产推出，而在马球马市场中，普通马匹价值5万美元，非凡品种的马球马价格更是接近100万美元，这笔交易清楚地表明，其中蕴藏着巨大的商机。

That clone of Cuartetera had been created in the United States. Vichera wondered if he could bring the technique to Argentina and scale it up. At that time, the University of Buenos Aires had the technology to perform cloning, and a favorable ecosystem to carry out the work was beginning to form in the country. Polo, with horses valued as works of art and team owners obsessed with preserving winning genetics, seemed like the perfect terrain to explore. “Planning to clone these exceptional horses represented a huge business opportunity,” says Vichera.那台 Cuartetera 的克隆体是在美国培育出来的。维切拉想知道自己能否将这项技术带回阿根廷并实现规模化。当时，布宜诺斯艾利斯大学已掌握克隆技术，阿根廷国内也开始形成开展这项工作的有利生态。波尔地区的马被视为艺术品般珍贵，马主们又一心想要保留优良的获胜基因，这里似乎是开展克隆研究的理想之地。“计划克隆这些顶级赛马，意味着巨大的商业机遇。”维切拉说道。

Even as the news of Cuartetera sparked both amazement and controversy due to ethical concerns about manipulating animals for sporting purposes, Vichera, along with two partners, founded a company to optimize and standardize the cloning process and make it a common tool in professional polo.尽管库阿特雷拉事件因利用动物进行体育活动的伦理争议引发了惊叹与非议，但维切拉与两位合作伙伴共同创立了一家公司，旨在优化并标准化克隆流程，使其成为职业马球运动中的常用工具。

![Ten horses with their riders lined up on a polo field.](https://knowablemagazine.org/docserver/fulltext/p-cuartetera-ceremony.jpg)

On November 18, 2023, in Buenos Aires, before the beginning of a Triple Crown match — the three most prestigious polo tournaments in the world — several polo teams presented their cloned horses. It was a ceremony honoring Cuartetera, the mare belonging to player Adolfo Cambiaso and one of the most important in the history of polo, who had died in May of that year. 2023年11月18日，在布宜诺斯艾利斯的三冠赛——全球三项最顶级的马球赛事——开赛前夕，数支马球队伍展示了他们的克隆马。这是一场为纪念库阿特泰拉而举办的仪式，这匹母马属于球员阿道夫·坎比亚索，是马球史上最重要的马球马之一，于当年5月离世。 CREDIT: PHOTO BY SEBASTIÁN LÓPEZ BRACH FOR THE WASHINGTON POST VIA GETTY IMAGES 图片来源：塞巴斯蒂安·洛佩斯·布拉赫 摄，为《华盛顿邮报》通过盖蒂图片社提供

At first, the results were not encouraging. The first clones by Vichera’s company, Kheiron Biotech, between 2012 and 2016, were made from adult skin cells, and almost half of the foals from the 38 live births had abnormalities of the umbilical cord or placenta, or limbs that were abnormally bent. The turning point came when the company started working with [stem cells from bone marrow](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5818860/). “This technology changed everything. Today, almost 100 percent of births are as healthy as those obtained through natural breeding,” says Vichera. To date, Kheiron Biotech reports having produced a thousand cloned horses.起初，结果并不令人鼓舞。2012年至2016年间，维切拉所在的凯龙生物科技公司培育出的首批克隆马，均取自成年皮肤细胞，在38匹存活的幼驹中，近半数出现脐带或胎盘异常，或四肢弯曲畸形的情况。 当该公司开始采用 [骨髓干细胞](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5818860/) 进行研究时，情况迎来了转折点。维切拉表示：“这项技术彻底改变了一切。如今，几乎100%的克隆幼驹都和自然繁育的幼驹一样健康。” 截至目前，凯龙生物科技称已培育出1000匹克隆马。

With the cloning technique now mastered, Kheiron Biotech ventured into even more ambitious territory. In December 2024, the company announced the birth of five foals that had been genetically edited using the [CRISPR-Cas9 technique](https://knowablemagazine.org/content/article/living-world/2024/crispr-gene-editing-therapy-systems-eukaryotic-cells), a global milestone in equine breeding. The intervention consisted of inserting a DNA sequence known as SINE into the control sequences of the creatures’ myostatin gene. This is a genetic variant that already exists naturally in some breeds and influences muscle development. The main goal, however, was to demonstrate that precision genetic editing in horses is technically feasible, and compatible with cloning,随着克隆技术如今已被掌握，凯龙生物科技公司（Kheiron Biotech）进军了更具雄心的领域。2024年12月，该公司宣布五匹通过 [CRISPR-Cas9技术](https://knowablemagazine.org/content/article/living-world/2024/crispr-gene-editing-therapy-systems-eukaryotic-cells) 进行基因编辑的小马驹顺利诞生，这是马类育种领域的一项全球性里程碑。此次基因编辑操作是将一种名为SINE的DNA序列插入这些动物肌抑素基因的调控序列中。这是一种在部分马种中天然存在的基因变异，会影响肌肉发育。不过，其主要目标是证明对马进行精准基因编辑在技术上是可行的，且可与克隆技术兼容。

Vichera presented the achievement as proof of concept and a preview of a scenario in which it will be possible not only to copy the best horses, but also to introduce specific modifications to their genomes. Editing the myostatin gene has known effects on muscle fiber composition and performance during short, intense efforts, and there is ongoing research into other genes and possible applications, although the details have not been revealed.维切拉将这一成果展示为概念验证，同时也是一种场景的预演——在该场景中，人们不仅能复制优质赛马的基因，还能对其基因组进行特定修改。编辑肌抑素基因已知会对肌肉纤维构成以及短时间高强度运动中的表现产生影响，目前研究人员也在探索其他基因及潜在应用，不过相关细节尚未公开。

With polo as its flagship, Argentina overwhelmingly dominates the global equine cloning industry, followed — at a considerable distance — by the United States and some European countries.以马球为核心项目，阿根廷在全球马匹克隆行业中占据绝对主导地位，美国和部分欧洲国家则远远落后。

But despite the technical advances, significant losses occur at each stage. It is estimated that, out of every 100 embryos, 20 reach the blastocyst stage and are transferred. Of these, 10 are successfully implanted in surrogate mares, and of those 10, only five reach full term. Even among foals born, there can be problems with health and development, although the lack of public data prevents this from being quantified accurately.但尽管技术取得了进步，每个阶段仍会出现大量损失。据估计，每100个胚胎中，只有20个能发育至囊胚期并被移植。在这20个胚胎中，有10个成功植入代孕母马体内；而在这10个成功植入的胚胎里，仅有5个能足月发育。即便幼驹顺利出生，其健康和生长发育也可能出现问题，不过由于缺乏公开数据，目前无法对这一情况进行准确量化。

The high loss rate partly explains the high cost of the procedure. Although the price has fallen in recent years thanks to technical advances, cloning a horse remains a luxury: It costs around $40,000 per born animal.高损耗率在一定程度上解释了该手术的高昂成本。尽管近年来技术进步使价格有所下降，但克隆马仍然是一项奢侈行为：每存活一匹幼马的成本约为4万美元。

A taxidermy of Dolly the sheep at the National Museum of Scotland. Dolly was the first mammal cloned from an adult somatic cell. Born in Scotland, her case marked a turning point in reproductive biology and sparked scientific and ethical debates around the world. 苏格兰国家博物馆中多莉羊的标本。多莉是第一只由成年体细胞克隆而成的哺乳动物。它诞生于苏格兰，其案例为生殖生物学研究带来了转折点，也在全球范围内引发了科学与伦理层面的讨论。 CREDIT: SGERBIC / WIKIMEDIA COMMONS 图片来源：SGERBIC / 维基共享资源

## Cambiaso’s dream come true 坎比亚索的梦想成真

Shortly after Cambiaso requested that tissue from his horse Aiken Cura be preserved following the animal’s devastating fall and euthanasia, he launched Crestview Genetics, founded with Texas oilman Alan Meeker and Argentine businessman Ernesto Gutiérrez. In Texas, the company partnered with the firm ViaGen to begin the cloning work.坎比亚索的赛马艾肯·库拉因严重摔倒被实施安乐死后，他要求保存这匹马的组织。不久之后，他便与得州石油商艾伦·米克纳以及阿根廷商人欧内斯托·古铁雷斯共同创立了克雷斯特维尤基因公司。该公司在得州与维亚根公司合作，启动了克隆相关工作。

In August 2010 — four years after the accident that killed Aiken Cura — Cambiaso was in Santa Barbara, California, when he received a call with the news that the first clones of Aiken Cura and those of his mare Cuartetera had been born. He traveled immediately to see them. “It was a strange feeling,” he recalls. “We spent two hours looking at them, unable to believe it.” 2010年8月——也就是导致艾肯·库拉身亡的事故发生四年后——坎比亚索正在加利福尼亚州的圣巴巴拉，这时他接到一通电话，得知艾肯·库拉以及他的母马夸特泰拉的首批克隆体已经出生。他立刻动身前去探望。“那是一种很奇怪的感觉，”他回忆道，“我们盯着它们看了两个小时，简直不敢相信这是真的。”

Fabricio García, one of Argentina’s leading dressage experts, worked with these first young clones. “For me, it was the same as breaking in a normal colt. My boss \[Cambiaso\] told me, ‘Do your training, I like your training.’ The pressure was on, because they were the first clones, but then I realized they were horses like any other,” he says.阿根廷顶尖的盛装舞步专家之一法布里西奥·加西亚曾参与培育这些首批年轻克隆马。“对我来说，这和驯练一匹普通的年轻公马没什么两样。我的老板\[坎比亚索\]对我说，‘按你的方式训练就好，我认可你的训练方法。’当时压力确实很大，因为它们是首批克隆马，但后来我意识到，它们和其他马没什么不同。”他说道。

Training is the basis of everything, adds García, like the foundation of a house. As the years go by, the colt gets used to contact with the saddle and reins. Then it is guided through turns, stops and starts until it learns to respond precisely to the rider’s commands. In general, horses are ready to compete at six years of age, but the Cuarteteras — all clones — started with an obvious genetic advantage that allowed them to jump onto the field earlier.加西亚补充道，训练是一切的基础，就像房子的地基一样。随着岁月流逝，小马逐渐习惯与马鞍和缰绳接触。随后，人们会引导它完成转弯、停下和起步等动作，直到它学会精准响应骑手的指令。一般来说，马匹在六岁时具备参赛能力，但库阿特拉特拉马——全都是克隆马——从一开始就拥有明显的基因优势，这让它们得以更早登上赛场。

The final of the 2016 Palermo Open would leave an unforgettable image. The original Cuartetera had retired a year earlier, but on the field, lined up by Cambiaso, were six identical mares — chestnut, slender, with a distinctive white spot illuminating their foreheads, all clones of Cuartetera — who would bring victory to La Dolfina. For the first time, clones were playing polo. Their names: Cuartetera B01, Cuartetera B02, Cuartetera B03, Cuartetera B04, Cuartetera B05, and Cuartetera B06.2016年巴勒莫公开赛的决赛留下了一幅令人难忘的画面。原本的夸特泰拉母马一年前已退役，但在赛场上，由坎比亚索调教的六匹一模一样的母马并肩而立——它们是栗色、身形修长，额头有醒目的白色斑点，全是夸特泰拉的克隆马——为拉多尔菲纳队赢得了胜利。克隆马参加马球比赛，这还是头一回。它们的名字分别是：夸特泰拉B01、夸特泰拉B02、夸特泰拉B03、夸特泰拉B04、夸特泰拉B05和夸特泰拉B06。

That image — six cloned mares deciding the most important tournament in world polo — sparked a debate off the field. As equine cloning ceased to be an experimental rarity and became a competitive tool, ethical questions intensified: about the morality of standardizing of animals, and the biological costs of the process, especially for the recipient mares.这张六匹克隆母马角逐世界马球最重要赛事的画面，在赛场外引发了一场争论。随着马匹克隆不再是实验性的稀罕物，转而成为竞技工具，相关伦理问题愈发突出：人们对动物标准化的道德性，以及该过程带来的生物代价——尤其是对受体母马的代价，提出了质疑。

“We can talk about identity in any individual, even cloned horses,” says Finnish philosopher Häyry Matti, who specializes in ethics. “Improving the athletic performance of a nonhuman for human entertainment is repugnant. It intensifies objectification, manipulation and hegemonic imposition.” “我们可以探讨任何个体的身份认同，即便是克隆马也不例外，”专攻伦理学的芬兰哲学家海里·马蒂说。“为了人类的娱乐需求去提升非人类动物的运动表现是令人反感的。这会加剧物化、操控以及霸权式的强加行为。”

Indeed, in the daily practice of cloning, dilemmas about animal welfare and objectification translate into procedures and risks that are rarely visible to the public and for which there are often no public records. According to equine geneticist Demyda Peyrás, “many clones are not born in the field, but in specialized veterinary hospitals or in the companies’ own neonatal units due to frequent complications during birth.” These, he adds, are rare in traditional horse breeding.事实上，在克隆的日常实践中，关于动物福利和客体化的困境会转化为公众很少能看到的程序和风险，而这些程序和风险往往也没有公开记录。马遗传学家德米达·佩拉斯（Demyda Peyrás）表示：“由于分娩过程中频繁出现并发症，许多克隆马并非在野外出生，而是在专业兽医医院或企业自身的新生儿护理中心降生。”他补充说，这些情况在传统马匹繁育中十分罕见。

The ethical debate surrounding equine cloning has changed over the years. In the beginning, [the main concern revolved around the health of the clones](https://beva.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/eve.12566): malformations and potential suffering during gestation and adulthood. With technical advances and the standardization of protocols, these objections lost their centrality and, at least in the field of polo, the practice began to enjoy relative acceptance. However, this normalization did not dispel all worries. Concerns remain about the actual rate of miscarriages, health problems in foals born and, above all, the lack of transparency and public data to assess the biological impacts of the process.多年来，围绕马克隆的伦理争议一直在变化。起初， [主要担忧集中在克隆马的健康问题上](https://beva.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/eve.12566) ：克隆马在孕期和成年期可能出现畸形，还可能承受痛苦。随着技术进步和操作流程的标准化，这些反对意见的核心地位逐渐减弱，至少在马球领域，克隆实践开始获得相对认可。然而，这种规范化并未消除所有顾虑。目前，人们仍对克隆过程中实际的流产率、新生小马的健康问题感到担忧，尤其是缺乏评估该过程生物学影响的透明化公共数据。

Pablo Ross, scientific director of STgenetics, argues that today, cloning does not differ substantially from other reproductive technologies applied in animal breeding. Demyda Peyrás, on the other hand, warns of the risk of inbreeding depression, the loss of genetic variability when crossing individuals that are too closely related. [The cloning industry relies almost exclusively on lines of the “Polo Argentino” breed](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/26/1/26) and racehorses. If this trend continues, it could have an impact on the fertility and resilience of animals, and their ability to tolerate stress.STgenetics 的科学总监巴勃罗·罗斯（Pablo Ross）认为，如今的克隆技术与动物育种中应用的其他繁殖技术并无本质区别。另一方面，德米达·佩拉斯（Demyda Peyrás）则警告了近亲繁殖衰退的风险——即当亲缘关系过近的个体进行交配时，遗传变异性会丧失。 [克隆行业几乎完全依赖“阿根廷马球”品种的血统和赛马。](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/26/1/26) 如果这一趋势持续下去，可能会影响动物的繁殖力、适应能力以及其耐受压力的能力。

In turn, the [combination of cloning and gene editing opens up new dilemmas](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17511321.2020.1737204#abstract)**,** says Gambini, the University of Queensland veterinarian who specializes in reproductive biotechnologies. It is one thing to use such tools to prevent disease or improve production efficiency — which could be justified on welfare grounds — and quite another to use them to enhance athletic performance for human entertainment. The risks of editing, such as unwanted health effects on the animals, are still uncertain, which is why geneticists like Demyda Peyrás agree on the value of international regulations and the need for mandatory and detailed reporting systems on the development and health of genetically modified animals.昆士兰大学专攻生殖生物技术的兽医甘比尼表示，克隆与基因编辑的结合 [带来了新的困境](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17511321.2020.1737204#abstract) **。** 利用这类工具预防疾病或提高生产效率是一回事——这在福利层面有其合理性——而利用它们为了人类娱乐而提升运动表现则是另一回事。基因编辑带来的风险，比如对动物产生不良的健康影响，目前仍不明确。这也是为什么德米达·佩拉斯等遗传学家都认可国际法规的价值，以及有必要建立针对转基因动物研发和健康状况的强制性详细报告制度。

For his part, Cambiaso has always been impervious to questioning. From day one, he has defended a utilitarian view. He sees no ethical dilemmas and justifies the practice because of the benefits it brings to his game. He is driven by sporting glory and the consolidation of an industry that he himself started — an industry operating without public reporting systems that this year recorded the birth of 600 to 700 cloned horses in Argentina alone and has already cloned 60 Cuarteteras.就坎比亚索而言，他对质疑向来置若罔闻。从一开始，他就秉持功利主义的观点。他认为不存在伦理困境，并为这种做法辩护，称其能为自己的赛马事业带来益处。他的动力源于体育荣耀，以及亲手开创的行业的壮大——该行业没有公开的报告体系，今年仅在阿根廷就诞生了600至700匹克隆马，且已克隆了60匹库阿特拉特拉马。
