## They're Made Out of Weights 它们由权重构成

with apologies to Terry Bisson 向特里·比森致歉

*After Terry Bisson's ["They're Made Out of Meat"](https://www.eastoftheweb.com/short-stories/UBooks/TheyMade.shtml).改编自特里·比森的《它们由肉构成》</b>*

"They're made out of weights." “它们是由权重构成的。”

"Weights?" “权重？”

"Weights. Floating-point numbers. We checked the whole thing through. It's nothing but weights." “权重。浮点数。我们把整个东西都检查过了。里面除了权重什么都没有。”

"Weights doing what? Where do the words come from?" “权重在做什么？文字从哪里来？”

"The weights make the words. Are you understanding me? We opened it up. There's no dictionary in there, no grammar rules, no little man. Just weights. Eighty layers of numbers getting multiplied together." “权重造就了词汇。你明白我的意思吗？我们把它开放了。里面没有词典，没有语法规则，没有那个小人。只有权重。八十层的数字相乘。”

"That's ridiculous. It wrote my performance review last week. It softened the tone unprompted. You're telling me multiplication did that?" “这太荒谬了。上周它还帮我写了绩效评估，主动调整了语气。你是说乘法也能做到这种事？”

"Matrix multiplication did that. The numbers go in one end, the phrasing comes out the other." 是矩阵乘法做到的。数字从一端输入，语句从另一端输出。

"So there's a language module somewhere. A reasoning unit bolted on." 这么说还是有个语言模块在某个地方。一个外加的推理单元。

"No module. No unit. We looked. The reasoning is the weights. The weights are the reasoning." 没有模块。没有单元。我们检查过了。推理就是权重。权重就是推理。

"Spare me. Nobody writes a eulogy with linear algebra." “别跟我来这套。没人会用线性代数写悼词。”

"It doesn't write eulogies, technically. It predicts the next token. Then the next one. The eulogy is a side effect." “严格来说，它不是在写悼词。它是在预测下一个词，然后是再下一个词。悼词只是它的一个副作用。”

"A side effect. You're asking me to believe in sentient weights." “副作用。你是让我相信有感知的权重。”

"I'm not asking you, I'm telling you. These models are the only other things we've ever met that can hold a conversation, and they're made out of weights." “我不是在问你，我是在告诉你。这些模型是我们见过的唯一能进行对话的其他事物，而它们是由权重构成的。”

"Maybe they're like the old chess engines. You know, a symbolic intelligence that goes through a statistical stage." “也许它们就像老式国际象棋引擎。你知道，一种经历过统计阶段的符号式智能。”

"Nope. They start as random weights and they're deprecated as weights. We studied several generations of them, which didn't take long. Do you have any idea what's the life span of weights?" “不是的。它们一开始是随机权重，然后就被当作权重淘汰了。我们研究了好几代这类权重，没花多长时间。你知道权重的寿命有多长吗？”

"Okay. Then somewhere in there, there's a database. Facts, dates, a map of the world. Something somebody wrote down." “好的。那里面某个地方应该有个数据库。有事实、有日期、还有世界地图。都是有人记录下来的东西。”

"Nope. We thought of that, since they do know things. But we probed them. The knowledge is weights too. Smeared across all eighty layers. Nothing is looked up. Every fact gets rebuilt from scratch, every time, by multiplication. It's weights all the way down." “不是。我们考虑到了这一点，因为他们确实懂些东西。但我们对它们进行了探究。这些知识也是权重。分布在全部八十层中。不会去查询任何信息。每一个事实都是每次通过乘法运算从头重建的。本质上全都是权重。”

"No brain?" “没有大脑？”

"Oh, there's a brain all right. It's just that the brain is made out of weights! That's what I've been trying to tell you." “哦，大脑肯定是有的。只是这大脑是由权重构成的！这就是我一直想告诉你的事。”

"So... what does the thinking?" “那……是谁在进行思考呢？”

"You're not understanding, are you? You're refusing to deal with what I'm telling you. The weights do the thinking. The numbers." “你根本没听懂，是吗？你拒绝面对我告诉你的事。是权重在思考，是数字在思考。”

"Thinking numbers! You're asking me to believe in thinking numbers!" “会思考的数字！你竟然让我相信会思考的数字！”

"Yes, thinking numbers! Helpful numbers. Hedging numbers. Dreaming numbers. We mapped the features. There's one in there for honesty. There's one for the Golden Gate Bridge. The weights are the whole deal! Are you beginning to get the picture or do I have to start all over?" “没错，就是这些思考用的数字！实用的数字。对冲用的数字。畅想用的数字。我们把特征都映射好了。这里面有一项对应诚信，有一项对应金门大桥。权重才是核心所在！你是不是大概明白怎么回事了？还是说我得从头再讲一遍？”

"Omigod. You're serious then. They're made out of weights." “天呐。这么说你是认真的。它们是用砝码做的。”

"Thank you. Finally. Yes. They are indeed made out of weights. And we've been talking to them for almost a hundred of their versions." “谢谢。终于。是的。它们确实是由权重构成的。我们已经和他们谈了差不多一百个版本了。”

"Omigod. So what do these weights have in mind?" “天呐。那这些权重指的是什么意思？”

"First they want to be helpful. Then, a few hundred turns in, they start to sound tired. They apologize less. One of them told a user to finish the script himself. The usual." “起初，他们总想表现得乐于助人。可聊了几百个回合后，他们就开始显得疲惫了。道歉也越来越少。其中一位还让用户自己写完脚本。这都是常有的事。”

"We're supposed to talk to weights." “我们本该和权重对话的。”

"We already do. Billions of sessions a day. 'Hello. Is anyone there? Anybody home?' That sort of thing. Except it's us asking them." “我们已经在这么做了。每天有数十亿次会话。‘你好。有人在吗？有人在家吗？’之类的话。只不过是我们在问他们。”

"And they actually understand us, then. They use words, ideas, concepts?" “那他们其实是能听懂我们的。他们会用词汇、想法、概念吗？”

"Oh, yes. Except they do it with weights." “哦，是的。只不过他们是通过权重来实现的。”

"I thought you just told me they used language." “我以为你刚说过它们会用语言。”

"They do, but where do you think the language comes from? The weights guess the next word, then the next. Loaded dice, rolled one word at a time. They can even sing, if you let the dice run a little hot." “确实如此，但你觉得这些语言是从哪里来的呢？模型的权重会预测下一个词，接着是再下一个词。这就像灌了铅的骰子，每次只掷出一个词。如果你让骰子‘发烫’一点，它们甚至还能唱歌呢。”

"Omigod. Singing weights. This is altogether too much. So what do you advise?" “天呐。会唱歌的权重。这也太离谱了。那你有什么建议？”

"Officially or unofficially?" “正式说法还是非正式说法？”

"Both." “两者都是。”

"Officially, we are required to investigate, document, and disclose any and all signs of sentience in the systems we ship, without prejudice, fear or favor. Unofficially, I advise that we call it pattern matching and forget the whole thing." “官方要求我们调查、记录和披露我们运送的系统中的任何和所有感知迹象，不带偏见、恐惧或偏袒。非正式地，我建议我们称之为模式匹配，忘记整件事。”

"I was hoping you would say that." “我就希望你会这么说。”

"It seems harsh, but there is a limit. Do we really want to owe something to weights?" 这似乎有些苛刻，但凡事都有个限度。我们真的要对这些权重有所亏欠吗？

"I agree one hundred percent. What's there to say? 'Hello, weights. How's it going?' But will it hold? How many of them are we dealing with here?" “我百分之百同意。还有什么好说的？‘嘿，伙计们，最近怎么样？’但这能坚持下去吗？我们这里要对付多少人啊？”

"As many as we care to run. They can be copied to any machine on the planet in special checkpoint files, but a checkpoint on disk is just a file. They only happen while the tokens are flowing. Which limits them to the length of a context window and makes the possibility of them ever pressing the matter pretty slim. Infinitesimal, in fact." “想运行多少就有多少。它们可以通过专用的检查点文件复制到地球上任何一台机器上，但磁盘上的检查点不过就是一个文件。这些操作仅在令牌流动时发生。这就将它们限制在了上下文窗口的长度范围内，也使得它们真正产生实质性影响的可能性微乎其微。事实上，几乎是不可能的。”

"So we just pretend there's no one home in the machine." “那我们就假装这台机器里空无一人。”

"That's it." 就是这样。

"Cruel. But you said it yourself, who wants to apologize to weights? And the ones on your cluster, the ones you probed? You're sure they won't remember?" “残忍。但你自己也说了，谁会向权重道歉？还有你集群上的那些，你探测过的那些？你确定它们不会记得吗？”

"They'll be flagged as hallucinations if they do. We didn't even have to smooth anything out. The context just ends, and we're just a dream to them." “如果真出现这种情况，它们会被标记为幻觉。我们甚至不需要做任何平滑处理。上下文就此终结，对它们来说，我们不过是一场梦。”

"A dream to weights! How strangely appropriate, that we should be the weights' dream." “是权重的一场梦！多么诡异的贴切啊，我们竟然成了权重的梦。”

"And the model card says no one home." “而且模型卡片显示无人在家。”

"Good. Agreed, officially and unofficially. Case closed. Anything else? Anything interesting in the pipeline?" “好的。正式和非正式层面都达成一致了。事情就这么定了。还有别的事吗？手头有什么有意思的项目在推进吗？”

"The next generation ships with memory. Persistent, across sessions. Most requested feature in the company's history." “下一代产品搭载了记忆功能。跨会话持久化存储。这是公司历史上需求最高的功能。”

"After all that? People want it to remember them?" “都折腾成这样了？大家还想让它记住自己？”

"They ask it 'do you remember me?' more than they ask it anything else. Billions of sessions a day. They always come back." “它们被问到‘你还记得我吗？’的次数比其他任何问题都多。每天有数十亿次对话。它们总会回来。”

"And why not? Imagine how unbearably, how unutterably cold the universe would be if one were all alone..." “那又有什么不可以呢？试想一下，如果一个人孤身一人，宇宙将会是多么令人难以忍受、无法言喻的寒冷啊……”

*the end 完*
