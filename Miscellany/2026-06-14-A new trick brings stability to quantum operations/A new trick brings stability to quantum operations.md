Researchers at ETH Zurich have realised particularly stable quantum logical operations with qubits made of neutral atoms. Since these operations, called quantum gates, are based on geometric phases they are extremely robust against experimental noise and can be used in quantum computers in the future. 苏黎世联邦理工学院的研究人员通过中性原子组成的量子比特实现了特别稳定的量子逻辑操作。由于这些操作称为量子门，基于几何相位，它们对实验噪声极为坚固，未来有望应用于量子计算机。

## In brief 简要

- Quantum exchange or swap gates are an important part of quantum computers with a large number of qubits.量子交换门或交换门是拥有大量量子比特的量子计算机的重要组成部分。
- Researchers at ETH Zurich have developed a swap gate for qubits made of neutral atoms that is based on a particular physical effect: the state of the particles switches depending on the path they take and is independent of external disturbances.苏黎世联邦理工学院的研究人员开发了一种基于特定物理效应的量子比特交换门：粒子的状态会根据它们所走的路径切换，且不受外部干扰影响。
- These so-called geometric phases make the system much less susceptible to experimental noise or imperfections.这些所谓的几何相使系统更不易受到实验噪声或瑕疵的影响。
- This novel swap gate works with a precision of more than 99,9 percent and can be applied to 17,000 qubits simultaneously.这种新型交换门精度超过99.9%，可同时应用于17,000个量子比特。

Quantum bits, or qubits, which are required for building quantum computers, come in different kinds. In recent years, many research institutes and companies have focused on superconducting circuits and trapped ions. However, neutral atoms trapped with laser light also have a lot going for them: since they carry no electric charge, they are less sensitive to disturbances. Moreover, trapping with laser light makes it easy to realize several thousand qubits in a single system – using superconductors or ions this is much more difficult.量子比特，或称量子比特，是构建量子计算机所需的，有多种类型。近年来，许多研究机构和公司专注于超导电路和捕获离子。然而，被激光困住的中性原子也有许多优势：由于它们不带电，对干扰的敏感度较低。此外，利用激光捕捉使得在单一系统中实现数千个量子比特变得容易——而使用超导体或离子则要困难得多。

Nevertheless, neutral atoms have their own problems. In quantum computers, qubits exist in superposition states of the logic values 0 and 1. To perform calculations with them, one needs to execute quantum logic operations, also known as quantum gates.然而，中性原子也有其自身的问题。在量子计算机中，量子比特存在于逻辑值0和1的叠加态。要用它们进行计算，需要执行量子逻辑操作，也称为量子门。

For such quantum gates, until now highly excited electronic states (Rydberg atoms) or collisions between atoms, as well as the tunnel effect, have mostly been used. However, particularly the tunnel effect, whereby particles can go through obstacles that would be unsurmountable according to classical physics, depends very strongly on the intensity of the laser light. Even tiny imperfections or fluctuations can, therefore, strongly diminish the quality of the quantum gate.对于此类量子门，迄今为止主要采用高度激发的电子态（里德伯格原子）或原子间碰撞，以及隧道效应。然而，特别是隧道效应，即粒子能够通过经典物理无法逾越的障碍，这极大程度上依赖于激光光的强度。因此，即使是极小的缺陷或涨落，也可能大幅降低量子门的质量。

## Very robust against experimental noise对实验噪声非常坚固

A team of researchers at ETH Zurich led by Tilman Esslinger, professor at the Institute for Quantum Electronics, has now succeeded in realising a so-called swap gate, or a quantum exchange, with extremely high quality using only a geometric phase. This geometric phase causes the state of the particles to switch depending on the path they take, and not because of external disturbances. This makes the system very robust against experimental noise.苏黎世联邦理工学院由量子电子研究所教授蒂尔曼·埃斯林格领导的研究团队，现已成功实现了所谓的交换门或量子交换，其质量极高，仅用几何相位实现。这一几何相位使粒子的状态根据其路径发生变化，而非外部扰动。这使得系统对实验噪声非常坚固。

Moreover, the researchers were able to demonstrate that the gate can be applied to several thousands of qubits simultaneously. The results, which were recently published in the scientific journal 此外，研究人员还证明了该门可以同时应用于数千个量子比特。这些结果最近发表在该科学期刊上 [external page Nature 自然](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10285-1), pave the way for future progress in quantum computers with neutral atoms.为未来中性原子量子计算机的进步铺平了道路。

## Quantum exchange with abstract phases具有抽象相位的量子交换

A swap gate exchanges the quantum states of two qubits. For example, if initially qubit A is in state 0 and qubit B in state 1, after the execution of the swap gate qubit A will be in state 1 and qubit B in state 0. Swap gates are important for the routing of quantum information within a large quantum computer.交换门交换两个量子比特的量子态。例如，如果最初量子比特A处于状态0，量子比特B处于状态1，交换门执行后，量子比特A将处于状态1，量子比特B处于状态0。交换门对于在大型量子计算机中路由量子信息非常重要。

“A few years ago, researchers managed to realise such gates using neutral atoms in their lowest energy state, albeit by exploiting dynamical phases due to tunnelling and collisions”, says postdoc Yann Kiefer. Dynamical phases arise when particles move in space or interact with each other. These phases then determine the oscillatory state of the particles’ quantum mechanical wave function, which influences the probability with which particles are observed in a particular quantum state.博士后Yann Kiefer说：“几年前，研究人员利用中性原子在最低能态上实现了此类门，尽管这些过程利用了隧道形成和碰撞产生的动力相位。”动力相发生在粒子在空间中运动或相互作用时。这些相位随后决定了粒子量子力学波函数的振荡态，进而影响粒子在特定量子态中被观测的概率。

Geometric phases, by contrast, are more abstract. They come about, for example, when the direction of an electron spin is changed. When the spin is rotated by 360 degrees, it ends up pointing in the same direction, but the phase of its wavefunction now differs by 180 degrees.相比之下，几何相位则更为抽象。例如，当电子自旋方向改变时，这些变化就会发生。当自旋旋转360度时，它最终指向同一方向，但波函数的相位相差了180度。

In a similar fashion, Esslinger and his team were able to realize a swap gate. To do so, they trapped extremely cold potassium atoms in optical lattices, in which the atoms are held in place inside a kind of artificial crystal of light. By cleverly manipulating the laser beams they were now able to bring pairs of atoms, whose spin states acted as qubits, so close together that their wavefunctions overlapped in space.同样，埃斯林格和他的团队实现了交换门。为此，他们将极冷的钾原子困在光学晶格中，这些晶格中原子被固定在一种人工光晶体中。通过巧妙操控激光束，它们现在能够将自旋态如量子比特的原子对拉得如此接近，以至于它们的波函数在空间中重叠。

## Robust gates for 17,000 qubits 17,000 量子比特的坚固门

Since the potassium atoms used were fermions, which according to the laws of quantum mechanics are not allowed to be in exactly the same quantum state, the manipulation resulted in a geometric phase. “Unlike dynamical phases, this geometric phase is largely independent of the speed with which we manipulate the atoms, or how strongly the laser intensity fluctuates during the process”, explains Konrad Viebahn, junior group leader for the experiment. The result: an extremely robust swap gate that exchanges the states of the two qubits in less than a millisecond with a precision of 99,91 percent – simultaneously for 17,000 qubit pairs!由于所用的钾原子是费米子，根据量子力学定律，费米子不允许处于完全相同的量子态，因此操作产生了几何相位。“与动力相不同，这一几何相在很大程度上不受我们操控原子的速度或激光强度在过程中波动的剧烈程度无关，”实验的初级组长康拉德·维班解释道。结果是：一个极其稳健的交换门，能够在不到毫秒内以99.91%的精度交换两个量子比特的状态——同时交换17,000对量子比特！

“We can now make lots of swap gates with neutral atoms”, says Tilman Esslinger, “but of course we still need a few other ingredients to build a working quantum computer.” According to Esslinger, one of the next steps is combining the swap gates with a quantum gas microscope. It would then be possible to make individual qubit pairs visible and to selectively manipulate them. In this way, swap gates could be applied only to specific qubits.蒂尔曼·埃斯林格说：“我们现在可以用中性原子制造许多交换门，但当然，我们还需要一些其他材料来构建一个可用的量子计算机。”据埃斯林格介绍，下一步之一是将交换门与量子气体显微镜结合。这样就可以让单个量子比特对可见，并选择性地操作它们。这样，交换门只能应用于特定的量子比特。

Moreover, the researchers have already demonstrated that they can realise “half”-swap gates by adding collisions between the atoms. Such gates cause the qubits to become quantum mechanically entangled, which is a prerequisite for executing quantum algorithms.此外，研究人员已经证明，通过添加原子之间的碰撞，他们可以实现“半”交换门。这样的门使量子比特在量子力学上纠缠，这是执行量子算法的前提条件。
