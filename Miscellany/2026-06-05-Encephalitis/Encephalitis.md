I was recently diagnosed with [anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-NMDA_receptor_encephalitis). It is an autoimmune disorder where your body’s normally helpful antibodies [start acting strangely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-NMDA_receptor_encephalitis#Pathophysiology). This leads to inflammation in the brain. This short blog briefly discusses some of my experience and prognosis.我最近被诊断出患有 [抗NMDA受体脑炎](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-NMDA_receptor_encephalitis) 。这是一种自身免疫性疾病，在这种疾病中，你体内原本有益的抗体 [开始出现异常反应](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-NMDA_receptor_encephalitis#Pathophysiology) 。这会导致脑部出现炎症。这篇简短的博客将简要谈谈我的一些患病经历和预后情况。

**Target audience**: Anyone relying on my work for their own projects.**目标受众** ：所有为自身项目依赖我的工作成果的人。

It all started with flu-like symptoms: heart racing, night sweats, the chills and trouble sleeping. But no congestion or cough. I also felt really off mentally. A deep sort of anxiety, along with panic attacks, that I had never experienced before in my 38 years of life. It was terrifying, especially because I had no idea what was causing it. There were no life events or obvious triggers that precipitated the psychological symptoms, nor was there any obvious biological explanation for the physical symptoms at the time. This was only the beginning.一切始于流感样症状：心跳加速、盗汗、畏寒，还伴有失眠。但没有鼻塞或咳嗽。我的精神状态也极差，一种深沉的焦虑感袭来，还出现了恐慌发作，这是我38年人生中从未经历过的。这一切令人恐惧，尤其是因为我完全不知道病因。当时，没有任何生活事件或明显诱因引发这些心理症状，身体上的症状也没有明显的生物学解释。而这仅仅是个开始。

Over the ensuing weeks my physical symptoms progressed to [chronic jaw pain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temporomandibular_joint_dysfunction), making it incredibly difficult to eat. I also had problems with my balance. As someone who has easily juggled 3 balls and played sports for my entire childhood, I couldn’t catch a ball lobbed to me from a few feet away by my 5 year old son. My psychological symptoms were perhaps even more horrifying to me. I had [suicidal ideation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicidal_ideation) and suffered from [psychosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychosis). Specifically, delusions and auditory hallucinations.在接下来的几周里，我的身体症状发展为 [慢性下颌疼痛](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temporomandibular_joint_dysfunction) ，导致进食变得异常困难。我的平衡也出现了问题。作为一个从小就能轻松抛接三个球、一直坚持运动的人，我连我五岁儿子从几英尺外抛过来的球都接不住。我的心理症状对我来说或许更令人恐惧。我出现了 [自杀意念](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suicidal_ideation) ，还患上了 [精神病](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychosis) ，具体表现为妄想和幻听。

The problems with balance and the overwhelming nature of my psychological symptoms eventually led me to fall and hit my head. This in turn led myself and my wife to decide that I couldn’t be safe at home. And that brought us to my first emergency room visit. They cleared me physically and sent me to an [in-patient psychiatric hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Flew_Over_the_Cuckoo%27s_Nest_\(novel\)), which, at the time, I welcomed because my symptoms had progressed beyond what we could manage at home.平衡感失常以及心理症状的压倒性最终让我摔倒并撞到了头。这反过来让我和妻子决定，我在家无法保证安全。也正因如此，我第一次去了急诊室。医生为我做了身体检查后，将我送往了 [住院精神病院](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Flew_Over_the_Cuckoo%27s_Nest_\(novel\)) ，当时我对此是欢迎的，因为我的症状已经发展到了我们在家无法应对的程度。

It is common for anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis to be misdiagnosed as (in my case) [generalized anxiety disorder](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8693539/) or [schizophrenia](https://www.cshl.edu/putting-out-a-brain-on-fire/). Since I had been cleared *physically*, getting out of the psychiatric hospital quickly to see a neurologist proved difficult. This was the single point, in retrospect, where our health care system let me down. It took a lucky connection with someone who happened to be a doctor to get me out of the psychiatric facility and into the [neurology department at Brigham and Women’s Hospital](https://www.brighamandwomens.org/neurology) in Boston.抗NMDA受体脑炎常被误诊为（就我而言） [广泛性焦虑障碍](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8693539/) 或 [精神分裂症](https://www.cshl.edu/putting-out-a-brain-on-fire/) 。由于我的身体</b>各项指标已无异常，想尽快出院去看神经科医生变得十分困难。回想起来，这是我们的医疗体系唯一一次让我失望的时刻。后来我幸运地结识了一位医生，通过他的帮助，我才得以离开精神病院，进入波士顿 [布莱根妇女医院的神经内科](https://www.brighamandwomens.org/neurology) 就诊。

After that, I was in and out of Brigham and Women’s Hospital for almost a month. I had several MRIs, a lumbar puncture, EEGs and many more tests. As a result of what I now see as a [life saving treatment protocol](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/departments/neurological/depts/multiple-sclerosis/ms-approaches/adult-onset-anti-nmda-receptor-encephalitis), I very quickly received [intravenous immunoglobulin](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/ivig-intravenous-immunoglobulin) (IVIG) and [methylprednisolone](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/corticosteroids-glucocorticoids), even before my diagnosis was known. In particular, MRIs revealed a [lesion in my brain](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/demyelinating-disease). However, confirming a diagnosis of anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis would come later since it is [best done with at least a positive antibody test in your cerebral spinal fluid](https://www.encephalitis.info/types-of-encephalitis/autoimmune-encephalitis/nmdar-antibody-encephalitis/). Results from this specific test typically take a couple weeks to come back.在那之后，我将近一个月的时间里反复进出布莱根妇女医院。我做了多次核磁共振成像检查、一次腰椎穿刺、脑电图检查以及更多其他检测。如今看来，正是这套 [救命的治疗方案](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/departments/neurological/depts/multiple-sclerosis/ms-approaches/adult-onset-anti-nmda-receptor-encephalitis) 发挥了作用，我在确诊结果出来之前，就很快接受了 [静脉注射免疫球蛋白](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/ivig-intravenous-immunoglobulin) （IVIG）和 [甲泼尼龙](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/corticosteroids-glucocorticoids) 治疗。尤其是核磁共振成像检查显示我的 [脑部出现病变](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/demyelinating-disease) 。不过，确诊抗NMDA受体脑炎则需要等到后续，因为该病的诊断 [最好至少以脑脊液抗体检测呈阳性为依据](https://www.encephalitis.info/types-of-encephalitis/autoimmune-encephalitis/nmdar-antibody-encephalitis/) 。这项特定检测的结果通常需要几周时间才能出来。

By the time I received my official diagnosis, the IVIG and steroids had kicked in and I was feeling much better, albeit, not nearly at 100%. I’ve since continued on a course of steroids that I am now already tapering off of. I’m also tapering off of medications I had been prescribed as a result of my psychological symptoms, before encephalitis was known to be the cause. Moreover, I am now officially in the [CIELO clinical trial](https://forpatients.roche.com/en/trials/autoimmune-disorder/autoimmune-encephalitis/a-study-to-evaluate-the-efficacy--safety--pharmacokinet-99237.html) for testing the effectiveness of [satralizumab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satralizumab) in treating anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis.等到我拿到正式诊断结果时，静脉注射免疫球蛋白和类固醇已经开始起效，我感觉好多了，尽管还远未恢复到百分之百的状态。从那以后，我一直按疗程服用类固醇，现在已经开始逐渐减量。我也在逐步停用之前因心理症状而开具的药物，而那时我们还不知道脑炎是致病原因。此外，我目前正式参与了 [CIELO临床试验](https://forpatients.roche.com/en/trials/autoimmune-disorder/autoimmune-encephalitis/a-study-to-evaluate-the-efficacy--safety--pharmacokinet-99237.html) ，该试验旨在测试 [萨特利珠单抗](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satralizumab) 治疗抗NMDA受体脑炎的有效性。

While [autoimmune disorders don’t have a known cure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoimmune_disease#Treatment), the [prognosis for anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6007532/) is very good. My doctors have said that it was caught early (despite the early tangent into a psychiatric hospital), and that this is associated with better long term outcomes. Indeed, I am feeling great now and recovery is exceeding my own expectations.虽然 [自身免疫性疾病尚无已知的治愈方法](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoimmune_disease#Treatment) ，但 [抗NMDA受体脑炎的预后](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6007532/) 非常好。我的医生说，尽管一开始曾被送进精神病院，但这种病还是被及早发现了，而及早发现与更好的长期预后相关。事实上，我现在感觉非常好，康复情况也超出了我自己的预期。

There is some [speculation that anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis could partially explain past accounts of demonic possession](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00415-017-8546-4). Many of the people in my life, close or not, could tell that there was something seriously wrong with me. Without science and modern medicine, I can only imagine what kind of speculation folks might have ventured for the underlying cause.有一些关于抗NMDA受体脑炎可能部分解释过去关于恶魔附身的记载的推测</b>。我生活中的许多人，无论关系亲疏，都能看出我身体出了严重问题。如果没有科学和现代医学，我真不敢想象人们会对根本原因做出怎样的猜测。

My full story of this disease of chaos is quite long and I’m not sure I will ever publish it in full. However, Susannah Cahalan did just that in her book, [*Brain on Fire: My Month of Madness*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain_on_Fire). There is also a [movie adaptation (as of June 2026) available for free on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zsJ3rePEu0Y). My disease didn’t progress as far as Susannah’s, nor did it do so in the same way. For example, I didn’t have any (known) seizures or [catatonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catatonia). The rest of her symptoms, especially the psychosis, were quite similar.我患上这种混乱病症的完整经历篇幅很长，我不确定自己是否会将其完整出版。不过，苏珊娜·卡汉在她的著作 [*《燃烧的大脑：我疯狂的一个月》*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain_on_Fire) 中做到了这一点。此外， [截至2026年6月，有一部改编电影可在YouTube上免费观看](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zsJ3rePEu0Y) 。我的病情发展程度和苏珊娜的并不相同，发病方式也不一样。比如，我没有出现任何（已知的）癫痫发作或 [紧张症](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catatonia) 。她的其他症状，尤其是精神病症状，与我非常相似。

This has been the absolute worst experience of my life, bar none. It is also the explanation behind my higher-than-usual inactivity over the last few months. But I am slowly getting back into the swing of things with a renewed vigor. I’m *excited* for where the industry is headed and I can’t wait to see what things will look like one year from today. I’m so happy that I get to be my “normal” self to experience that, which is a stark juxtaposition from how I felt just two months ago.这绝对是我这辈子最糟糕的经历，没有之一。这也解释了为什么过去几个月我比平时更不活跃。但我正以全新的活力慢慢恢复状态。我对行业的发展方向感到 *兴奋* ，迫不及待想看看一年后的行业会是什么样子。能恢复“正常”的自己去感受这一切，我真的很开心，这与两个月前的心情形成了鲜明对比。

Finally, I want to express some gratitude to two people in particular.最后，我想特别向两个人表达我的感激之情。

First and foremost is my wife, Kaitlyn Brady. She saved my life. She never stopped believing that there was some neurological component and she never stopped fighting for me. I feel so grateful that she is in my corner. More than that, the burden she carried before my diagnosis was known is something that is truly remarkable. She wasn’t just there for me when I needed her. She was there for our son. She was there for the doctors whenever they called, even late into the night. She was there when our basement flooded. And when we all caught influenza. I’ll never know how she juggled everything, but I’ll be in her debt for the rest of my life.首先要感谢我的妻子凯特琳·布雷迪。是她救了我的命。她始终坚信我的病情与神经系统有关，也从未停止为我奔走抗争。能有她做我的后盾，我心怀无比的感激。更重要的是，在我的病情被确诊之前，她独自扛起的那些重担，实在令人动容。她不只是在我需要的时候陪伴我，也悉心照顾我们的儿子；医生们随时来电，哪怕是深夜，她也会第一时间回应；家里地下室发洪水时，是她在；我们全家都患上流感时，还是她在。我永远也想不通她是如何兼顾这一切的，但我这辈子都欠她一份恩情。

Secondly is [Charlie Marsh](https://github.com/charliermarsh). He was patient, understanding and my partner through it all. He didn’t just exceed expectations for how you want your employer to deal with a serious medical condition, but he went above and beyond even that in more ways than one. It’s not often I can say that someone has handled a situation perfectly, but the word fits in this case.其次要感谢的是 [查理·马什](https://github.com/charliermarsh) 。他耐心体贴，自始至终都是我的搭档。他不仅远超雇主在应对严重疾病时应有的表现，还在诸多方面做得更出色。我很少会说某个人把事情处理得完美无缺，但这个词用在他身上再合适不过。

Thank you to my friends, family and doctors as well. Their support during this time was unwavering and I’m not sure what would have happened without them. “Nothing good” is what a nurse said when I posed that question to her.也感谢我的朋友、家人和医生们。他们在这段时间里始终给予我坚定的支持，我不敢想象没有他们我会变成什么样。我曾问过一位护士这个问题，她的回答是“不会有什么好事”。

Happy hacking. 祝编程愉快。
