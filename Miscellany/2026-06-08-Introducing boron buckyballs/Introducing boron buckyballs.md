## Introducing boron buckyballs 介绍硼富勒烯

Theory predicts B <sub>80</sub> cages can’t be made. Experimentalists just proved otherwise 理论预测B80笼状结构无法制成，但实验学家们刚刚证明了事实并非如此

Chemists have observed a [boron buckminsterfullerene](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2026/sc/d6sc02674e) for the first time, providing experimental evidence for an 80-atom cage whose existence has been debated since 2007 (*Chem. Sci.* 2026, DOI: 10.1039/d6sc02674e).化学家首次观测到了 [巴克敏斯特富勒烯硼](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2026/sc/d6sc02674e) ，为一种由80个原子构成的笼状结构提供了实验证据，该结构的存在自2007年以来一直存在争议（《化学科学》，2026年，doi:10.1039/d6sc02674e）。

Buckminsterfullerenes, or buckyballs, are hollow, soccer ball–shaped molecular cages first discovered in carbon. Their discovery launched a new branch of nanoscience. Boron, carbon’s electron-deficient neighbor in the periodic table, has long been considered a candidate for its own fullerene.巴克敏斯特富勒烯，也称巴基球，是一种中空、呈足球状的分子笼，最初在碳中被发现。它们的发现开创了纳米科学的一个新分支。硼作为碳在元素周期表中缺电子的相邻元素，长期以来一直被认为是有望形成自身富勒烯的候选元素。

"Boron is known as the rule breaker in chemistry," says Lai-Sheng Wang of Brown University, who led the experimental work. "For 80 atoms to exhibit this structure—I still find it incredible." 布朗大学的王来生（Lai-Sheng Wang）主导了这项实验研究，他表示：“硼在化学界被称为‘规则破坏者’。80个硼原子竟然呈现出这样的结构，我至今仍觉得难以置信。”

First author Hyun Choi produced the boron clusters by vaporizing a boron target with laser light. Argon was mixed into the helium carrier gas to cool and stabilize the clusters. Proper cooling enables B <sub>80</sub> to settle into a single structure. The process yielded a photoelectron spectrum—an electronic fingerprint of a material—with three sharp peaks matching the fullerene structure. "The moment I saw the spectrum, I knew I was looking at something remarkable," Choi says.第一作者玄哲利用激光轰击硼靶制得了硼团簇。研究人员在氦载气中混入氩气，对团簇进行冷却并使其保持稳定。恰当的冷却让B80能够形成单一结构。该过程得到了光电子能谱——一种物质的电子指纹，其三个尖锐的峰值与富勒烯的结构相吻合。“我看到能谱的那一刻，就知道自己发现了非同寻常的东西。”哲说。

"I’d congratulate the authors with finding the ball at last," says Boris Yakobson a materials scientist at Rice University [who first predicted the boron cage’s stability](https://cen.acs.org/articles/85/i18/Boron-buckyball-predicted-stable.html). “But I’d also wish to see more independent confirmations coming from other labs since it is such an unbelievable structure,” he exclaims!“我要恭喜作者们终于找到了这个‘球’，”莱斯大学的材料科学家鲍里斯·雅各布森说，他 [最早预言了这种硼笼的稳定性](https://cen.acs.org/articles/85/i18/Boron-buckyball-predicted-stable.html) 。“但我也希望能看到其他实验室给出更多独立的验证，毕竟这是一个如此不可思议的结构，”他激动地说道！

The finding puts the experiment in direct conflict with a large body of computational work. Density functional theory (DFT), the field’s workhorse method, ranks the buckyball geometry well below other B <sub>80</sub> structures in terms of predicted stability.这一发现使该实验与大量计算研究结果直接相悖。密度泛函理论（DFT）作为该领域的主流方法，在预测稳定性方面将巴克敏斯特富勒烯结构的排名远低于其他B80结构。

To strengthen their conclusions, the researchers simulated spectra for every competing structure. Only the buckyball matched. “DFT is wrong for this particular system,” Wang says. “This challenges DFT methods.” Not everyone agrees the discrepancy is as significant as it appears. Yakobson argues that the energy gap is far less dramatic when considered on a per-atom basis.为了强化结论，研究人员为所有存在竞争关系的结构都模拟了光谱。只有巴基球与之匹配。“对于这个特定体系，密度泛函理论（DFT）是错误的，”王说，“这对密度泛函理论（DFT）方法构成了挑战。”并非所有人都认为这种差异的重要性如其表面所示。亚克布森认为，从每个原子的角度来看，这种能量差距并没有那么显著。

Wang’s group observed [a boron cage of 40 atoms](https://cen.acs.org/articles/92/i29/Boron-Bonds-Fullerene-Like-Cage.html) in 2014. Although cage-shaped, B <sub>40</sub> does not have the soccer ball symmetry of C <sub>60</sub>, the carbon buckminsterfullerene composed of 60 carbon atoms. “B <sub>80</sub> is exactly equivalent to C <sub>60</sub>,” Wang says.王的团队在2014年观测到了 [一个由40个原子组成的硼笼](https://cen.acs.org/articles/92/i29/Boron-Bonds-Fullerene-Like-Cage.html) 。尽管呈笼状结构，B40却不具备由60个碳原子构成的碳巴克敏斯特富勒烯C60的足球对称结构。“B80与C60完全等价，”王表示。

B <sub>80</sub> is valence-isoelectronic with C <sub>60</sub> —meaning both have 240 valence electrons and nearly identical bonding. With its slightly larger diameter and stronger electron affinity, B <sub>80</sub> may be a better electron acceptor. Wang speculates that bulk B <sub>80</sub> could serve as a semiconductor, a hydrogen storage material, or—if doped—a superconductor. But none of this is possible without bulk synthesis, which has not yet been achieved. Synthesis is complicated by boron’s poor conductivity and two naturally occurring isotopes.B80与C60是价等电子体——这意味着二者均拥有240个价电子，成键方式也几乎相同。凭借稍大的直径和更强的电子亲和性，B80或许是更优质的电子受体。王教授推测，体相B80可作为半导体、储氢材料，或在掺杂后成为超导体。但要实现这些应用，前提是完成体相合成，而目前这一目标尚未达成。硼的导电性差，且存在两种天然同位素，这两大因素让合成过程变得复杂。

Wang’s group aims to test B <sub>80</sub> ’s reactivity with molecules such as water and oxygen. If the boron-boron bonds survive, Wang says, bulk synthesis may be within reach. The team also wants to probe larger boron clusters to determine if the cage structure persists and where it might break down.王的团队旨在测试B80与水和氧气等分子的反应性。王表示，如果硼-硼键能够保持稳定，批量合成或许指日可待。该团队还希望探究更大的硼团簇，以确定笼状结构是否会保持稳定，以及它可能在何处发生分解。

Wang is optimistic about synthesis. When his group proposed [borophene](https://cen.acs.org/materials/2-d-materials/Scientists-synthesize-large-borophene-crystals/99/i41?ref=search_results), a 2D boron analog of graphene, two independent teams synthesized it within 2 years. "Once you show something is possible, people will try it," Wang says.王对合成研究持乐观态度。当他的团队提出 [硼烯](https://cen.acs.org/materials/2-d-materials/Scientists-synthesize-large-borophene-crystals/99/i41?ref=search_results) ——一种石墨烯的二维硼类似物时，有两个独立的团队在两年内就将其合成出来了。“一旦你证明某件事是可行的，人们就会去尝试，”王说。
