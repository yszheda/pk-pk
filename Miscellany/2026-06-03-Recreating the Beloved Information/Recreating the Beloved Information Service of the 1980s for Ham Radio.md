利用电脑的声卡生成并收听数字音频信号，图文电视页面可通过业余无线电进行传输。詹姆斯·普罗沃斯特

Once upon a time in Europe, [television](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/television) remote controls had a magic [teletext](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teletext) button. Years before the [internet](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/internet) stole into homes, pressing that button brought up teletext digital information services with hundreds of constantly updated pages. Living in [Ireland](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/ireland) in the 1980s and ’90s, my family accessed the national teletext service— [Aertel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RT%C3%89_Aertel) —multiple times a day for weather and news bulletins, as well as things like TV program guides and updates on airport flight arrivals.曾几何时，在欧洲， [电视](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/television) 遥控器上都有一个神奇的 [图文电视](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teletext) 按键。早在 [互联网](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/internet) 走进千家万户之前，按下这个按键，就能调出图文电视数字信息服务，上面有数百个持续更新的页面。上世纪八九十年代，我住在爱尔兰</b>，家人每天都会多次访问国家图文电视服务—— [Aertel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RT%C3%89_Aertel) ——查看天气和新闻简报，还有电视节目指南、机场航班到达信息等内容。

It was an elegant system: fast, low bandwidth, unaffected by user load, and delivering readable text even on analog television screens. So when I recently saw it was the [40th anniversary of Aertel](https://bsky.app/profile/40yearsago.bsky.social/post/3mcfgzqm2ns2w) ’s test transmissions, it reactivated a thought that had been rolling around in my head for years. Could I make a ham-radio version of teletext?这是一套精妙的系统：速度快、带宽占用低、不受用户负载影响，即便在模拟电视屏幕上也能呈现清晰可读的文字。因此，当我最近得知 [Aertel 测试播出迎来 40 周年](https://bsky.app/profile/40yearsago.bsky.social/post/3mcfgzqm2ns2w) 时，一个在我脑海中盘旋多年的想法再次被激活。我能否打造一个图文电视的业余无线电版本？

## What is Teletext? 什么是图文电视？

First developed in the [United Kingdom](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/united-kingdom) and rolled out to the public by the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/articles/cvg360rr91zo) under the name [Ceefax](http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/entertainment/3681174.stm), teletext exploited a quirk of analog television signals. These signals transmitted video frames as [lines of luminosity and color](https://spectrum.ieee.org/build-this-8bit-home-computer-with-just-5-chips), plus some additional blank lines that weren’t displayed. Teletext piggybacked a digital signal onto these spares, transmitting a carousel of pages over time. Using their remotes, viewers typed in the three-digit code of the page they wanted. Generally within a few seconds, the carousel would cycle around and display the desired page.图文电视最初在 [英国](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/united-kingdom) 研发成功，随后由 [英国广播公司](https://www.bbc.com/articles/cvg360rr91zo) 以 [Ceefax](http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/entertainment/3681174.stm) 为名向公众推出，它利用了模拟电视信号的一个特性。这些信号将视频帧传输为 [亮度和色彩行](https://spectrum.ieee.org/build-this-8bit-home-computer-with-just-5-chips) ，同时还包含一些未显示的额外空白行。图文电视将数字信号搭载在这些空白行上，随时间传输一系列页面。观众通过遥控器输入想要查看的页面三位数代码。通常在几秒钟内，页面序列就会循环播放并显示出目标页面。

Teletext created unusually legible text in the 8-bit era by enlarging alphanumeric characters and interpolating new pixels by looking for existing pixels touching diagonally, and adding [whitespace](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/whitespace) between characters. Graphic characters were not interpolated, and featured blocky chunks known as sixels for their 2-by-3 arrangement. My modern recreation uses the open-source font Bedstead, which replicates the look of teletext, including the [graphics](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/graphics) characters. James Provost 在8位时代，图文电视通过放大字母数字字符，并通过寻找对角相邻的现有像素来插入新像素，同时在字符之间添加 [空白](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/whitespace) ，从而呈现出异常清晰的文字。图形字符不进行插值处理，其由2×3排列的块状像素构成，这种像素块被称为六像素单元。我用开源字体Bedstead进行了现代复刻，该字体还原了图文电视的视觉效果，包括 [图形](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/graphics) 字符。詹姆斯·普罗沃斯特

Teletext is composed of characters that can be one of eight colors. Control codes in the character stream select colors and can also produce effects like flashing text and double-height characters. The text’s legibility was better than most computers could manage at the time, thanks to the [SAA5050](https://www.cpcwiki.eu/imgs/9/9e/Mullard_SAA5050_datasheet.pdf) character-generator chip at the heart of teletext. Although characters are internally stored on this chip in 6-by-10-pixel cells—fewer pixels than the [typical 8-by-8-pixel cell](https://home-2002.code-cop.org/c64/) used in 1980s home computers—the SAA5050 interpolates additional pixels for alphanumeric characters on the fly, making the effective resolution [10 by 18 pixels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullard_SAA5050). The trade-off is very low-resolution graphics, comprising characters that use a 2-by-3 set of blocky pixels.图文电视由八种颜色之一的字符组成。字符流中的控制代码可选择颜色，还能产生闪烁文字、双倍高度字符等效果。得益于作为图文电视核心的 [SAA5050](https://www.cpcwiki.eu/imgs/9/9e/Mullard_SAA5050_datasheet.pdf) 字符生成芯片，当时的文字清晰度远超大多数计算机所能实现的水平。尽管该芯片内部以6×10像素单元存储字符，其像素数少于20世纪80年代家用电脑常用的 [典型8×8像素单元](https://home-2002.code-cop.org/c64/) ，但SAA5050会实时为字母数字字符插值生成额外像素，使有效分辨率达到 [10×18像素](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mullard_SAA5050) 。与之对应的是极低分辨率的图形，这类图形由采用2×3块状像素组合的字符构成。

Teletext screens use a 40-by-24-character grid. This means that a kilobyte of memory can store a full page of multicolor text, half [the memory required](https://www.c64-wiki.com/wiki/Screen_RAM) for a similar amount of text on, for example, the [Commodore 64](https://spectrum.ieee.org/commodore-64). The [BBC Microcomputer](https://www.computinghistory.org.uk/det/182/acorn-bbc-micro-model-b/) took advantage of this by putting [an SAA5050](https://www.bbcbasic.co.uk/bbcwin/manual/bbcwinh.html) on its [motherboard](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/motherboard), which could be accessed in one of the computer’s graphics modes. Despite the crude graphics, some [educational games](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/educational-games) used this mode, most notably [**Granny’s Garden**](https://www.4mation.co.uk/retro/retrogranny.html), which filled the same cultural niche among British schoolchildren that [**The Oregon Trail**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oregon_Trail_\(1985_video_game\)) did for their U.S. counterparts.图文电视屏幕采用40×24的字符网格。这意味着1千字节的内存可存储一整页多色文本，所需内存仅为类似文本量的一半，例如在 [所需内存](https://www.c64-wiki.com/wiki/Screen_RAM) 方面，就远少于 [康懋达64](https://spectrum.ieee.org/commodore-64) 。 [BBC微型计算机](https://www.computinghistory.org.uk/det/182/acorn-bbc-micro-model-b/) 充分利用了这一特性，在其 [主板](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/motherboard) 上安装了 [SAA5050芯片](https://www.bbcbasic.co.uk/bbcwin/manual/bbcwinh.html) ，该芯片可在计算机的一种图形模式下被调用。尽管图形效果简陋，一些 [教育类游戏](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/educational-games) 仍使用了这一模式，其中最具代表性的便是 [**《奶奶的花园》**](https://www.4mation.co.uk/retro/retrogranny.html) 。这款游戏在英国学童中占据的文化地位，与 [**《俄勒冈之旅》**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Oregon_Trail_\(1985_video_game\)) 在美国同龄学童中的地位如出一辙。

By the 2010s, most teletext services had ceased [broadcasting](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/broadcasting). But teletext is still [remembered fondly by many](https://www.bbc.com/audio/play/m00268v4), and enthusiasts are keeping it alive, [recovering and archiving old content](https://teletextarchaeologist.org/), running [internet-based services with current newsfeeds](https://nmsceefax.co.uk/), and developing systems that make it possible to [create and display teletext](https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/create-your-own-teletext-service/) with modern TVs.到21世纪10年代，大多数图文电视服务已停止 [播出](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/broadcasting) 。但许多人仍 [深切怀念](https://www.bbc.com/audio/play/m00268v4) 图文电视，爱好者们也在让它延续下去—— [回收并归档旧内容](https://teletextarchaeologist.org/) 、 [运行基于互联网的实时新闻源服务](https://nmsceefax.co.uk/) ，还开发出能让现代电视 [制作和显示图文电视](https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/create-your-own-teletext-service/) 的系统。

## Putting Teletext Back on the Air 让图文电视重回大众视野

I wanted to do something a little different. Inspired by how the [BBC Micro](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/bbc-micro) co-opted teletext for its own purposes, I thought it might make a great radio protocol. In particular I thought it could be a digital counterpart to [slow-scan television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow-scan_television) (SSTV).我想做些不一样的尝试。受 [BBC Micro](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/bbc-micro) 将图文电视改造为自有用途的启发，我认为它或许能成为一种出色的无线电协议。我尤其觉得，它可以成为 [慢扫描电视](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow-scan_television) （SSTV）的数字版。

SSTV is an analog method of transmitting pictures, typically including banners with ham-radio call signs and other messages. SSTV is fun, but, true to its name, it’s slow—the most popular protocols take [a little under 2 minutes to send an image](https://sevierraces.org/all-about-slow-scan-tv) —and it can be tricky to get a complete picture with legible text. For that reason, SSTV images are often broadcast multiple times.SSTV 是一种传输图片的模拟方式，通常会包含带有业余无线电呼号及其他信息的横幅。SSTV 很有趣，但名副其实，它的传输速度很慢——最常用的协议发送一张图片需要 [将近两分钟](https://sevierraces.org/all-about-slow-scan-tv) ，而且要得到一张文字清晰的完整图片可能并不容易。因此，SSTV 图片往往会多次广播。

Teletext is still remembered fondly by many.许多人至今仍对图文电视心怀怀念。

I decided to send the teletext using the [AX.25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AX.25) protocol, which encodes ones and zeros as audible tones. For [VHF and UHF transmissions](https://www.arrl.org/frequency-bands) at a rate of 1,200 baud, it would take 11 seconds to send one teletext screen. Over [HF bands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_frequency), AX.25 data is normally sent at 300 baud, which would result in a still-acceptable 44 seconds per screen. When a teletext page is sent repeatedly, any missed or corrupted rows are filled in with new ones. So in a little over 2 minutes, I could send a screen three times over HF, and the receiver would automatically combine the data. I also wanted to build the system in [Python](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/python) for portability, with an editor for creating pages, an AX.25 encoder and decoder, and a monitor for displaying received images.我决定使用 [AX.25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AX.25) 协议传输图文电视，该协议将1和0编码为可听音调。在 [甚高频和超高频频段](https://www.arrl.org/frequency-bands) 以1200波特率传输时，发送一屏图文电视需要11秒。在 [高频频段](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_frequency) 上，AX.25数据通常以300波特率发送，这样每屏44秒的时长仍然可以接受。当图文电视页面被重复发送时，任何丢失或损坏的行都会用新行补齐。因此，在2分多钟的时间里，我可以通过高频频段将一屏内容发送三次，接收端会自动合并这些数据。我还打算用 [Python](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/python) 构建这套系统以保证其可移植性，系统包含用于创建页面的编辑器、AX.25编解码器，以及用于显示接收图像的监控程序。

The reason why I hadn’t done this before was because it requires digesting the details of the [AX.25 standard](https://www.ax25.net/AX25.2.2-Jul%2098-2.pdf) and [teletext’s official spec](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_i_ets/300700_300799/300706/01_60/ets_300706e01p.pdf), and then translating them into a suite of software, which I never seemed to have the time to do. So I tried an experiment within an experiment, and turned to vibe coding.我之前没有这么做的原因是，这需要吃透 [AX.25 标准](https://www.ax25.net/AX25.2.2-Jul%2098-2.pdf) 和 [图文电视官方规范](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_i_ets/300700_300799/300706/01_60/ets_300706e01p.pdf) 的细节，再将其转化为一套软件，而我似乎一直抽不出时间来完成这件事。于是我在一个实验里又做了一个尝试，转而采用了氛围编程。

Despite the popularity of vibe coding with developers, I have reservations. Even if concerns about [AI slop](https://spectrum.ieee.org/responsible-ai), [the environment](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-water-usage), and [memory hoarding](https://spectrum.ieee.org/high-bandwidth-memory-shortage) were not on the table, I would still worry about the [reliance on centralized systems](https://spectrum.ieee.org/top-programming-languages-2025) that vibe coding brings. The whole point of a [DIY](https://spectrum.ieee.org/topic/diy/) project is to, well, do it yourself. A DIY project lets you craft things for your own purposes, not just operate within someone else’s profit margins and policies.尽管氛围编码在开发者中很受欢迎，但我对此持保留态度。即便不考虑对 [AI 劣质内容](https://spectrum.ieee.org/responsible-ai) 、 [环境](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-water-usage) 以及 [内存占用](https://spectrum.ieee.org/high-bandwidth-memory-shortage) 的担忧，我仍然担心氛围编码所带来的对 [中心化系统的依赖](https://spectrum.ieee.org/top-programming-languages-2025) 。 [DIY](https://spectrum.ieee.org/topic/diy/) 项目的核心意义在于，亲力亲为。一个 DIY 项目能让你为自己的需求打造东西，而不仅仅是在别人的利润空间和规则框架内行事。

Still, criticizing a technology from afar isn’t ideal, so I directed [Anthropic’s Claude](https://chat.chatbotapp.ai/) toward the AX.25 and teletext specs and told it what I wanted. After about 250,000 to 300,000 tokens and several nights of back and forth about bugs and features, I had the complete system running without writing a single line of code. Being honest with myself, I doubt this system—which I’m calling Spectel—would ever have come about without vibe coding.不过，从远处批评一项技术并非理想之选，所以我让Anthropic 公司的 Claude</b> 聚焦于 AX.25 和图文电视的技术规范，并向它说明了自己的需求。经过约 25 万到 30 万个标记词的处理，以及连续数晚针对漏洞和功能的反复沟通，我最终让整个系统顺利运行，而自己一行代码都没写。说实话，我怀疑如果没有氛围编码，这个我命名为 Spectel 的系统根本不可能实现。

But I didn’t learn anything new about how teletext works, and only a little bit more about AX.25. Updates are contingent on my paying Anthropic’s fees. So I remain deeply ambivalent about vibe coding. And one final test remains in any case: trying Spectel out on HF bands. Of course, that means I’ll need willing partners out in the [ether](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/ether). So if you’re a ham who’d like to help out, let me know in the comments below!但我没有学到任何关于图文电视工作原理的新知识，对AX.25协议也只多了解了一点点。更新与否取决于我是否愿意支付Anthropic的费用。因此，我对氛围编程仍深感矛盾。无论如何，还有最后一项测试要做：在高频波段上试用Spectel。当然，这意味着我需要在 [空中](https://spectrum.ieee.org/tag/ether) 有愿意合作的伙伴。所以，如果你是业余无线电爱好者，愿意帮忙的话，欢迎在下方评论区留言告诉我！
