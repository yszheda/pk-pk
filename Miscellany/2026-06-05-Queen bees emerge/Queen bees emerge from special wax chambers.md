## Queen bees emerge from special wax chambers 蜂王从特殊的蜡质蜂房里孵化而出

Royal wax, as well as royal jelly, determines the fate of a honeybee larva 蜂王浆以及蜂蜡决定了蜜蜂幼虫的命运

A honeybee larva’s ascent to royalty is shaped by more than gobbling up royal jelly; it is also dictated by her cradle. Scientists have discovered that the queen cells are made of [chemically engineered, “bespoke” wax](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10534-3) that is critical for the survival and development of future queens (*Nature* 2026, DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10534-3).一只蜜蜂幼虫能否成长为蜂后，不仅仅取决于它是否能饱食蜂王浆，还由它所处的“摇篮”决定。科学家们发现，蜂后房是由 [经过化学工程设计的“定制化蜂蜡”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10534-3) 构成的，这种蜂蜡对未来蜂后的生存和发育至关重要（《自然》2026年，数字对象标识符：10.1038/s41586-026-10534-3）。

For decades, the scientific consensus held that the larvae fed an [exclusive diet of royal jelly](https://cen.acs.org/articles/93/i3/Ascension-Queendom.html) grew into fertile, long-lived queens, while their sisters who got standard fare grew into [sterile workers](https://cen.acs.org/articles/93/i34/Worker-BeesChemical-Castration.html). Although nutrition is undeniably important, researchers suspected something else was also at play.几十年来，科学界的共识一直是，仅以 [蜂王浆为唯一食物](https://cen.acs.org/articles/93/i3/Ascension-Queendom.html) 的幼虫会发育为可育且寿命更长的蜂王，而食用普通食物的姐妹幼虫则会成为 [不育的工蜂](https://cen.acs.org/articles/93/i34/Worker-BeesChemical-Castration.html) 。尽管营养因素无疑至关重要，但研究人员怀疑还有其他因素在起作用。

One place to look was the cells in which queen bees are reared. These are large, vertical wax cells, totally different from the classic hexagonal honeycomb of workers.一个值得关注的地方是培育蜂王的蜂房。这些蜂房是大型的垂直式蜡质蜂房，与工蜂典型的六边形蜂巢截然不同。

“Honeybees invest enormous effort in building highly specialized queen cells,” [Boris Baer](https://profiles.ucr.edu/app/home/profile/borisbar), an entomologist at the University of California, Riverside, says in an email. “From an evolutionary perspective, it seemed unlikely that such elaborate structures existed simply as containers for food.” “蜜蜂会投入巨大精力来建造高度特化的蜂王台，”加州大学河滨分校的昆虫学家鲍里斯·贝尔在一封电子邮件中表示，“从进化的角度来看，如此精巧的结构不太可能仅仅作为食物容器而存在。”

Baer and his collaborators investigated the physical and chemical properties of the queen cells and worker cells of western honeybees *(Apis mellifera).* They used scanning electron microscopy to examine the wax’s physical structure and gas chromatography-mass spectrometry to run a full chemical audit.贝尔及其合作者研究了西方蜜蜂（意大利蜜蜂）蜂王巢房和工蜂房的理化性质。他们利用扫描电子显微镜检测了蜂蜡的物理结构，并通过气相色谱-质谱联用技术开展了全面的化学分析。

The researchers found that, compared with worker cell wax, queen cell wax is richer in unsaturated fatty acids, specifically oleic acid, linoleic acid, and α-linolenic acid, and poorer in *n* -alkanes, such as pentacosane, and wax esters. It also has a lower density, hardness, and mechanical strength (both tensile and compressive) than its worker counterparts and exhibits a significantly higher peak melting temperature.研究人员发现，与工蜂房蜡相比，蜂王房蜡的不饱和脂肪酸含量更高，具体为油酸、亚油酸和α-亚麻酸，而正构烷烃（如二十五烷）和蜡酯的含量更低。此外，它的密度、硬度和机械强度（包括拉伸强度和抗压强度）均低于工蜂房蜡，且峰值熔融温度显著更高。

Using a specialized tracking system with thermal imaging and high-definition lenses, the researchers monitored the bees’ construction activities and identified a dedicated crew of queen-cell builder bees who are significantly younger than their worker-cell-building counterparts. These builders physically overheat their bodies to nearly 40 °C to process the queen’s wax and also completely alter the chemical signature of the royal nursery.研究人员借助一套配备热成像仪和高清镜头的专用追踪系统，监测了蜜蜂的筑巢活动，并发现了一支专门负责建造蜂王台的工蜂队伍——这些工蜂的年龄远小于负责建造工蜂房的同伴。这些筑蜂工蜂会将自身体温物理性升高至近40摄氏度，以此处理蜂王分泌的蜂蜡，同时还会彻底改变育王房的化学特征。

To prove the cells do play a role in raising new queens, Baer’s team grafted newly hatched queen larvae into cells capped with standard worker wax. Of the grafted queen larvae, 62.5% died. The grafting experiment was repeated with eastern honeybees (*Apis cerana*), and the results were quite similar.为了证明这些细胞确实在培育新蜂后方面发挥作用，贝尔的团队将刚孵化的蜂后幼虫移植到用标准工蜂蜂蜡封盖的细胞中。在被移植的蜂后幼虫中，62.5%死亡。研究团队对东方蜜蜂（Apis cerana）重复了这项移植实验，得到的结果也十分相似。

“We suspect that developing larvae are responding to a combination of chemical cues and physical properties of the wax, much like developing embryos in other animals respond to signals from their environment,” Baer says.“我们推测，正在发育的幼虫会对蜡质的化学信号和物理特性做出综合反应，这与其他动物的发育胚胎对来自环境的信号做出反应的方式非常相似，”贝尔说。

[Gene E. Robinson](https://sib.illinois.edu/directory/profile/generobi), a honeybee biologist at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign who wasn’t involved in the research, says the work is “compelling” and an “outstanding example” of interdisciplinary research. “The evidence presented in this paper strongly supports the existence of a highly specialized group of worker bees dedicated to queen rearing,” Robinson says in an email to C&EN. The new findings align with his earlier research that found that different worker genotypes show distinct preferences for either queen or worker rearing.伊利诺伊大学厄巴纳-香槟分校的蜜蜂生物学家吉恩·E·罗宾逊（Gene E. Robinson）并未参与这项研究，他表示该研究“极具说服力”，是跨学科研究的“杰出范例”。罗宾逊在写给《化学与工程新闻》（C&EN）的一封邮件中表示：“论文中呈现的证据有力地支持了存在一个高度专业化的工蜂群体，专门负责培育蜂王。”这一新发现与他此前的研究结果一致，后者发现不同的工蜂基因型对培育蜂王或培育工蜂表现出不同的偏好。

“I think it’s quite interesting,” says [James Nieh](https://biology.ucsd.edu/research/faculty/jnieh), an ecologist at the University of California, San Diego, who also wasn’t involved in the research. He wonders if a similar mechanism works in other bees, like those in the genus *Melipona* where “the workers themselves decide whether or not to become a queen.” He speculates that the answer may also lie in the chemical properties of the cells whose occupants become queens.“我觉得这非常有趣，”加州大学圣地亚哥分校的生态学家 [詹姆斯·尼](https://biology.ucsd.edu/research/faculty/jnieh) 表示，他也没有参与这项研究。他想知道类似的机制是否也适用于其他蜜蜂，比如无刺蜂属的蜜蜂，在这类蜜蜂中“工蜂自己决定是否成为蜂后”。他推测，答案可能也藏在那些孕育蜂后的细胞的化学特性中。
