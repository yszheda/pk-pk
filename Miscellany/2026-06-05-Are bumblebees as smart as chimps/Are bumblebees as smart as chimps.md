German psychologist [Wolfgang Köhler](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Wolfgang-Kohler) set up a famous experiment more than 100 years ago that changed how scientists understand animal intelligence and the power of insight — or spontaneous problem-solving. 100多年前，德国心理学家沃尔夫冈·苛勒</b>开展了一项著名实验，这项实验改变了科学家对动物智力以及顿悟——即自发解决问题能力——的认知方式。

Köhler made what he described as a [playground](https://pigeon.psy.tufts.edu/psych26/kohler.htm) for a group of chimpanzees with a banana hanging out of reach and various items — boxes, poles and sticks — lying around. The strewn objects offered opportunities for the animals to explore, and the food presented a challenge for them to unlock. After fruitlessly trying to snatch the banana, the chimps quickly started rearranging the items. The apes eventually stacked the boxes and easily grabbed the reward. 科勒为一群黑猩猩打造了一个他称之为 [“游乐场”](https://pigeon.psy.tufts.edu/psych26/kohler.htm) 的地方，里面挂着一根够不着的香蕉，还散落着各种物品——箱子、杆子和树枝。这些散落的物品为黑猩猩提供了探索的机会，而食物则给它们带来了解开谜题的挑战。在徒劳地试图抢夺香蕉后，黑猩猩们很快开始重新摆放这些物品。这些灵长类动物最终把箱子叠了起来，轻松拿到了奖励。

The experiment demonstrated that chimps were capable of insight. While [most animals](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8002912/) can do basic problem-solving, insight is a step up because it’s an understanding of cause and effect that does not rely on trial and error, copying others, or previous knowledge. Scientists have observed this cognitive ability in only a handful of species: great apes, elephants and some birds. There is an ongoing scientific debate over whether even more species — invertebrates such as octopuses and certain spiders — should also join the ranks of the spontaneous problem solvers. 这项实验证明黑猩猩具备顿悟能力。虽然 [大多数动物](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8002912/) 都能进行基础的问题解决，但顿悟是更高层次的能力，因为它是对因果关系的理解，不依赖于试错、模仿他人或过往知识。科学家仅在少数物种中观察到了这种认知能力：类人猿、大象和部分鸟类。目前科学界仍在争论，是否有更多物种——如章鱼和某些蜘蛛这类无脊椎动物——也应跻身于能自主解决问题的物种行列。

Now, a study published Thursday in [the journal Science](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ady1618?adobe_mc=MCMID=32040115521268807963986547309462626171%25257CMCORGID=242B6472541199F70A4C98A6%25252540AdobeOrg%25257CTS=1780491030) suggests that bumblebees possess insight. In a lab experiment, the insects were able to roll a plastic foam ball underneath an artificial blue flower, climb over the ball and use it to reach the flower, obtaining a sugary reward. “We showed for the first time that bumblebees can solve a completely novel object-manipulation task, spontaneously and without being trained to do so, or without any trial and error,” said lead author Akshaye Bhambore, a doctoral researcher at the University of Oulu in Finland. 近日，一篇于周四发表在 [《科学》杂志](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.ady1618?adobe_mc=MCMID=32040115521268807963986547309462626171%25257CMCORGID=242B6472541199F70A4C98A6%25252540AdobeOrg%25257CTS=1780491030) 上的研究表明，大黄蜂具备洞察力。在一项实验室实验中，这些昆虫能够将一个泡沫塑料球滚到人工蓝色花朵下方，爬过球体并借助它触碰到花朵，从而获得含糖奖励。该研究的第一作者、芬兰奥卢大学的博士研究员阿克沙耶·班博雷表示：“我们首次证明了大黄蜂能够自发地完成一项全新的物体操控任务，无需经过训练，也无需反复试错。”

Bumblebees can use [socially learned behaviors](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07126-4) and [logical reasoning](https://www.stir.ac.uk/news/2024/june-2024-news/wild-bumblebees-capable-of-logical-reasoning-study-finds/) to solve puzzles, previous studies have shown. In the new experiment, however, the researchers exposed the insects to the separate elements of the task but never trained them on the solution itself. 此前的研究表明，大黄蜂可以借助 [社会习得行为](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07126-4) 和 [逻辑推理](https://www.stir.ac.uk/news/2024/june-2024-news/wild-bumblebees-capable-of-logical-reasoning-study-finds/) 来解决谜题。然而在这项新实验中，研究人员让这些昆虫接触到了任务的各个独立组成部分，却从未对它们进行解决方案本身的训练。

This result suggests that a tiny insect brain can support surprisingly flexible behavior, according to James Nieh, a professor in the department of ecology, behavior and evolution at the University of California San Diego, who was not involved with the study. “Bees do not normally move objects around to make platforms, so this is not a natural bumble bee behavior,” he wrote in an email. “But the experiment shows that they can remember a hidden goal location and manipulate an object in relation to that goal.” 美国加州大学圣地亚哥分校生态、行为与进化系教授詹姆斯·尼并未参与该研究，他表示，这一结果表明昆虫微小的大脑竟能支撑起相当灵活的行为。“蜜蜂通常不会移动物体来搭建平台，因此这并非熊蜂的自然行为，”他在邮件中写道，“但实验证明，它们能够记住隐藏的目标位置，并根据该目标操控物体。”

This exciting new study shows that insects can learn and change their behavior in ways scientists are only just starting to understand, Natalie Hempel de Ibarra, an associate professor of neuroethology at the University of Exeter in England, said in an email. Hempel de Ibarra was not part of the research. This flexibility could shape how bees and other pollinators interact with flowers, helping them cope with challenges as environments and landscapes change, she added. 英国埃克塞特大学神经行为学副教授娜塔莉·亨普尔·德·伊巴拉在一封电子邮件中表示，这项令人兴奋的新研究表明，昆虫能够以科学家才刚刚开始理解的方式学习和改变行为。亨普尔·德·伊巴拉并未参与这项研究。她补充说，这种适应性可能会影响蜜蜂和其他传粉者与花朵的互动方式，帮助它们在环境和景观发生变化时应对挑战。

The researchers built a circular arena about 10 centimeters (4 inches) in diameter and 3.2 centimeters (1.3 inches) tall, in which bumblebees could walk but not fly. In the center, the team placed an artificial blue flower containing a sugary solution and let the bees explore it. Nearby, the scientists placed a small foam ball to familiarize the insects with the object and demonstrate that it wasn’t a threat. 研究人员制作了一个直径约10厘米（4英寸）、高3.2厘米（1.3英寸）的圆形竞技场，大黄蜂可以在其中行走但无法飞行。研究团队在竞技场中央放置了一朵装有含糖溶液的人造蓝色花朵，让大黄蜂进行探索。在附近，科学家们放了一个小泡沫球，让昆虫熟悉该物体，并证明它并非威胁。

A second scenario presented a different challenge: The ball now covered the blue flower, and the insects successfully pushed it away to access the reward. In a third and final scenario — the one designed to test insight — the team moved the flower from the floor to the ceiling, just above one of four pits shaped to accommodate the ball. A majority of the bees that had been exposed to the first two scenarios — 75% of them — managed to roll the ball to the correct pit and climb on it to access the flower. 第二种场景带来了不同的挑战：球体此时盖住了蓝色花朵，昆虫们成功将其推开，从而获得了奖励。在第三个也是最后一个场景——这个场景旨在测试昆虫的洞察力——研究团队将花朵从地面移到了天花板上，位置恰好就在四个可容纳球体的坑洞中的一个正上方。经历过前两个场景的大多数蜜蜂（占比75%）成功将球体滚到了正确的坑洞上，并爬上去接触到了花朵。

The researchers also presented the third scenario to two additional groups of bees: one that had only been exposed to the flower but not the ball, and another that was completely new to both the flower and the ball. Bees from these two additional groups were not able to solve the puzzle. 研究人员还向另外两组蜜蜂展示了第三种情景：一组只接触过花朵但未接触过球，另一组对花朵和球都是完全陌生的。这两组额外的蜜蜂都无法解开这个谜题。

“We wanted to know how much previous information they needed in order to solve the task,” said study coauthor Olli Loukola, a behavioral ecologist and senior research fellow at the University of Oulu. “We need to get rid of the neophobia, or fear of new objects, by giving them the ball and showing that it is a safe object. And they also need the motivation, or the association between a reward and the blue of the flower, because if they don’t have that, blue means nothing. But these two things together give them enough information to spontaneously solve the real problem, which is using the ball as a ladder to reach the blue flower.” “我们想知道它们需要多少先前的信息才能完成这项任务，”该研究的合著者、奥卢大学行为生态学家、高级研究员奥利·卢科拉说。“我们得给它们球，让它们知道这是个安全的东西，以此消除它们的新物恐惧症，也就是对新事物的恐惧。而且它们还得有相应的动机，也就是奖励和花朵蓝色之间的关联，因为如果没有这种关联，蓝色就毫无意义。但这两样东西结合起来，就能给它们足够的信息，让它们自发解决真正的问题——把球当作梯子去够到蓝色的花。”

To rule out the possibility that the bees could be solving the problem by moving the ball randomly or simply reacting to the visual stimuli of the blue flower, the researchers repeated the experiment with more stringent conditions. The team created a scenario in which the flower was not visible from the starting position of the ball. The bees that were exposed to the first two experimental scenarios were still able to solve the problem and access the flower. 为了排除蜜蜂是通过随机移动球来解决问题，或是仅对蓝色花朵的视觉刺激做出反应的可能性，研究人员在更严格的条件下重复了实验。该团队设置了一种从球的起始位置看不到花朵的场景。经历过前两种实验场景的蜜蜂仍然能够解决问题并接触到花朵。

Loukola said that the bumblebees exhibited “true goal-directed behavior” by using the ball as a ladder, unlike in the second scenario in which they needed to simply push the ball off the flower. Referring to that simpler task, he added, “The bees didn’t need to understand anything about that task and they could still learn to solve it.” 卢科拉表示，大黄蜂将球当作梯子使用，展现出了“真正的目标导向行为”，这与第二种情境不同——在第二种情境中，它们只需把球从花上推走即可。谈及这项更简单的任务，他补充道：“蜜蜂无需理解该任务的任何相关内容，依然能够学会解决它。”

In the third scenario, however, understanding the objective was a requirement. “They knew that if they could not reach the flower on the ceiling, there was a ball they could move to make themselves bigger, so they needed to kind of understand the physics of the task, and they needed to have a goal in mind,” he explained. 然而在第三种情境中，理解目标是一项必要条件。“他们清楚，如果够不到天花板上的花，还有一个球可以移动来让自己变大，所以他们需要理解这项任务的物理原理，并且心里要有明确的目标，”他解释道。

However, he added, this does not mean that the bumblebees possess humanlike reasoning or consciousness, and the study stops short of calling the rolling of the ball “tool use,” a definition that is usually highly debated when it comes to animal behavior. 不过他补充道，这并不意味着熊蜂拥有类似人类的推理能力或意识，且该研究并未将滚球行为称为“工具使用”——在动物行为领域，这一定义通常存在极大争议。

Nevertheless, Loukola said, the result is particularly significant because the bumblebees are “truly naive,” meaning nothing in their life experience could have prepared them to solve the problem they were presented. “We can be sure that none of the bumblebees have any earlier experience about these tasks, so we know that this is not innate behavior.” 不过卢科拉表示，这一结果具有特别重要的意义，因为大黄蜂是“真正的初生之犊”，这意味着它们的生活经历中没有任何东西能让它们为解决所面临的问题做好准备。“我们可以确定，没有一只大黄蜂对这些任务有任何先前的经验，因此我们知道这并非先天行为。”

The bees’ performance is even more impressive than that of Köhler’s chimps, since in some experiments they couldn’t see the target when they started moving the ball, according to Lars Chittka, a professor of sensory and behavioral ecology at Queen Mary University of London, who was not involved with the study. 伦敦玛丽女王大学感官与行为生态学教授拉斯·奇特卡并未参与这项研究，他表示，蜜蜂的表现比科勒的黑猩猩更令人印象深刻，因为在一些实验中，蜜蜂开始移动球时根本看不到目标。

“In a sense it’s like you and me entering a room, finding something on the ceiling that needs dealing with — perhaps changing the lightbulb of a lamp — seeing that we need a chair or ladder to get high enough, then going to a different room to fetch the chair or ladder, and coming back with the equipment to the correct destination,” he wrote in an email. “从某种意义上说，这就像你我走进一个房间，发现天花板上有东西需要处理——可能是换一盏灯的灯泡——看到我们需要一把椅子或梯子才能够到足够高的地方，然后去另一个房间拿来椅子或梯子，再带着工具回到正确的地点。”他在一封电子邮件中写道。

“All this really requires some understanding of the task at hand, keeping in mind where the target is, and taking appropriate action.” “这一切真正需要的是对当前任务有一定理解，牢记目标所在，并采取适当的行动。”

He added that the results should prompt scientists to rethink how much intelligence can be squeezed into a small nervous system, and that humans as thinking beings are surrounded by all kinds of other thinking beings, “however radically different their modes of thinking might be.” 他补充说，研究结果应促使科学家重新思考一个小型神经系统能承载多少智能，而作为思考生物的人类，身边遍布着各类其他思考生物，“无论它们的思维模式存在多么根本性的差异”。
