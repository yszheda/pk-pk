## The Dark Art of RFIC Design 射频集成电路设计的神秘技艺

So why do these chips all have to be crafted by hand? Why aren’t RFICs designed with an algorithmic synthesis process, much as CPUs and GPUs are?那为什么这些芯片都必须手工制作？为什么射频集成电路（RFICs）不能像中央处理器（CPU）和图形处理器（GPU）那样，通过算法综合流程来设计呢？

The design of RFICs is an exercise in engineering across multiple physical domains. [Maxwell’s equations](https://spectrum.ieee.org/the-long-road-to-maxwells-equations), operating across different spatial and temporal scales, govern how electromagnetic fields interact with active and passive devices that must be carefully codesigned for the chip to function. Alongside these are the laws of thermodynamics, which determine how heat is generated and removed during operation, as well as the mechanics of thermal expansion and contraction that dictate how reliably the chip and its packaging survive temperature changes.射频集成电路（RFIC）的设计是一项涉及多个物理领域的工程实践。 [麦克斯韦方程组](https://spectrum.ieee.org/the-long-road-to-maxwells-equations) 在不同的空间和时间尺度上发挥作用，决定了电磁场如何与有源和无源器件相互作用，这些器件必须经过精心的协同设计，芯片才能正常工作。与此同时，热力学定律也不可或缺，它决定了芯片在工作过程中热量的产生与散发方式，而热膨胀和收缩的力学原理则决定了芯片及其封装在温度变化时的可靠存活能力。

### AI Could Short-Circuit RFIC Design 人工智能或可革新射频集成电路设计

The design of a radio-frequency integrated circuit requires human intuition and multiple, often-repeated optimization steps. The hope is that through an understanding of Maxwell’s Equations, an AI can be taught to short-circuit this process and quickly produce a design.射频集成电路的设计需要人类的直觉以及多次反复的优化步骤。人们希望通过理解麦克斯韦方程组，让人工智能学会简化这一流程，从而快速完成设计。

Simultaneously accounting for all the physical constraints these impose makes the design space almost impossibly large. Every decision involves complex priorities that often compete with one another, preventing the optimization of any of them.同时将这些约束带来的所有物理限制都纳入考量，会让设计空间变得大到几乎无法处理。每一个决策都涉及复杂的优先级权衡，这些优先级往往相互冲突，导致无法对其中任何一个进行优化。

To better understand the issue, let’s walk through the steps involved, after which you’ll better understand why a single new chip design takes years and tens to hundreds of millions of dollars.为了更好地理解这个问题，我们来梳理一下其中涉及的步骤，看完之后你就会明白为什么一款全新的芯片设计需要耗费数年时间，以及数亿甚至上亿美元的成本。

Most of the area of radio-frequency integrated circuits is dominated by complex electromagnetic structures. Human-designed RFICs, like this broadband power amplifier \[1\], start with templates and follow a symmetric, understandable pattern. But freed from the constraints of human-designed templates and the need for humans to even understand the rationale of electromagnetic structures, power amplifier ICs \[2–5\] and low-noise amplifiers \[6\] can take on truly wild-looking yet efficient designs. SENGUPTA LAB 射频集成电路的大部分区域由复杂的电磁结构主导。人工设计的射频集成电路，如这款宽带功率放大器\[1\]，以模板为基础，遵循对称且易于理解的设计模式。但摆脱了人工设计模板的限制，甚至无需人类理解电磁结构的设计原理，功率放大器集成电路\[2-5\]和低噪声放大器\[6\]能够呈现出外观极具奇特却高效的设计。 森古普塔实验室

Let’s say you’re an engineer assigned to design a new 28-gigahertz [power amplifier](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10136184) for a 5G-millimeter-wave handset. (This is the type of RFIC that boosts the 5G signals on your phone and transmits them to the antenna where they can be picked up by a distant base station). Where do you start?假设你是一名工程师，受命为一款5G毫米波手机设计一款新的28吉赫兹功率放大器\</b>。（这是一种射频集成电路，能增强手机上的5G信号，并将其传输至天线，再由天线将信号发送至远处的基站）。你该从何入手？

RFIC design has some features in common with house building. Just as the blueprint for a house dictates the number of bedrooms and bathrooms to be built and the hallways connecting them, the blueprint for an RFIC—called the architecture—establishes the kinds of elements the RFIC needs to fulfill its intended function. Instead of rooms, the architecture includes, for example, the number of stages of amplification your power amplifier needs. Instead of hallways, it shows the paths that signals must take to get through those stages.射频集成电路（RFIC）的设计与房屋建造有一些共同特征。就像房屋的蓝图规定了要建造的卧室和浴室数量以及连接它们的走廊一样，射频集成电路的蓝图——称为架构——确定了射频集成电路为实现其预定功能所需的各类元件。架构中包含的不是房间，例如功率放大器所需的放大级数；它展示的也不是走廊，而是信号必须经过的、穿过这些放大级的路径。

The blueprint for RFICs is actually mostly hallway**;** passive elements, like inductors and transmission lines, take up far more real estate than active elements like transistors.射频集成电路的设计蓝图实际上大部分是走廊**;**电感器和传输线等无源元件占用的面积远大于晶体管等有源元件。

Here’s why. As you have probably experienced yourself, a typical CPU’s transistors overheat when faced with operating frequencies of just a few gigahertz. The frequencies RFICs can operate at are higher by an order of magnitude—28 and 39 GHz for 5G signals, 26.5 to 40 GHz and even higher for satellite communications, and 77 GHz for [automotive radar](https://spectrum.ieee.org/longdistance-car-radar). Under this onslaught, a CPU’s transistors would fail.原因如下。想必你自己也有过这样的体验：一款典型 CPU 的晶体管在仅几吉赫兹的工作频率下就会过热。而射频集成电路（RFIC）的工作频率要高出一个数量级——5G 信号对应的频率为 28 吉赫兹和 39 吉赫兹，卫星通信的频率在 26.5 至 40 吉赫兹甚至更高，汽车雷达的频率则为 77 吉赫兹。在如此高的频率负荷下，CPU 的晶体管会直接失效。

RFIC transistors avoid this fate because these chips cleverly manage the signal’s energy with careful electromagnetic design. This takes the form of byzantine networks of metal elements that dominate the chip’s real estate. Thesestructures are geometrically regular, often symmetrical, and so intricately constructed they sometimes resemble lacelike filigree. But while they may look decorative, they are essential to the chip’s functioning.射频集成电路晶体管能避免这种情况，因为这类芯片通过精心的电磁设计巧妙地控制信号的能量。这体现为复杂的金属元件网络占据了芯片的大量空间。这些结构在几何上规则且通常对称，其构造极为精细，有时看起来如同蕾丝般精致。尽管它们外观颇具装饰性，但却是芯片正常工作的关键所在。

Electrically speaking, these “hallways” work more like the chip’s plumbing**.** Like plumbing, this extensive labyrinth of passives confines electromagnetic energy only to the places it should be traveling around the chip.从电学角度来看，这些“通道”的作用更像是芯片的管道系统 **。** 与管道系统类似，这种由无源元件构成的庞大迷宫将电磁能量限制在芯片内仅需传输的路径上。

The major challenge in RFIC design is putting all these elements together to ensure they work, just as constructing a house from its blueprints demands exact specs for load-bearing beams, pipes, and external walls. On an RFIC, the architecture needs to be realized with physically fabricable transistors and passive components that are connected just so, to permit the signal to travel through the chip and be processed. The way these devices are connected locally is what we call the circuit’s topology.射频集成电路（RFIC）设计的主要挑战是将所有这些元件整合在一起，确保它们正常工作，这就如同依据蓝图建造房屋时，需要对承重梁、管道和外墙制定精确的规格。在射频集成电路上，其架构需要通过物理可制造的晶体管和无源元件来实现，这些元件需按照特定方式连接，以保证信号在芯片中传输并被处理。我们将这些元件的局部连接方式称为电路的拓扑结构。

## The RFIC Design Process 射频集成电路设计流程

To make that power amplifier, then, your first step is to identify a candidate circuit template: The combination of structures that will meet the goals of a particular architecture with a specific circuit topology. Over the years, researchers have eased your burden by developing reusable design templates for specific functions. For example, templates suggest how many amplification stages a circuit needs (because sometimes, combining the output of two smaller amplifiers will result in better bandwidth and efficiency than you would get from a single larger one). And they suggest what the general configuration of the passive structures should be. Today there is an extensive library of such templates.要制作这款功率放大器，你的第一步是确定一个候选电路模板：即能满足特定架构目标与特定电路拓扑结构的组合。多年来，研究人员开发出了针对特定功能的可复用设计模板，减轻了你的设计负担。例如，这些模板会给出电路所需放大级的数量（因为有时将两个小型放大器的输出合并，能比单个大型放大器获得更好的带宽和效率），还会给出无源器件的整体配置方案。如今，这类模板的库已经非常丰富。

However, these can’t simply be used off-the-shelf, because each comes with trade-offs. Some have better gain at the expense of stability; some better bandwidth at the expense of efficiency; still others are more energy efficient at the expense of output power, and so on. There is rarely a clear best choice.然而，这些方案不能直接现成使用，因为每种方案都存在权衡取舍。有些方案增益更优，却以稳定性为代价；有些带宽更优，却以效率为代价；还有些方案能效更高，却以输出功率为代价，诸如此类。几乎不存在明确的最优选择。

To arrive at the “sweet spot” where all these different parameters are balanced into optimal harmony, designers will typically lay out several different versions of the circuit, using intuitions and methods they have picked up in their years of training.为了找到能将所有这些不同参数平衡至最佳协调状态的“最佳点”，设计师通常会根据多年训练中积累的直觉和方法，设计出多种不同版本的电路。

The challenge is that the decision around the architecture, circuit topology, or the electromagnetic passives cannot be done separately. One decision influences the others. So, designing an RF circuit can often feel like trying to fit an oversized carpet into too small a room—press down one corner, and another pops up.挑战在于，围绕架构、电路拓扑或电磁无源元件的决策无法单独进行。一个决策会影响其他决策。因此，设计射频电路往往会让人感觉像是试图把一块过大的地毯塞进太小的房间——按下一个角落，另一个角落就会翘起来。

At microwave and millimeter-wave frequencies, even the smallest misstep is the difference between a chip that works and one that doesn’t, and any number of things can go wrong. For example, when an electromagnetic wave encounters a transistor—or any other component —the path it travels must be properly “matched” to what comes next. If it isn’t, some of the energy reflects backward instead of flowing forward. Imagine trying to connect a high-pressure fire hose directly to a narrow garden hose. Without the right adapter, water will splash backward at the junction. Very little will make it through. In electronics, this is called the impedance-matching problem.在微波和毫米波频段，哪怕是最微小的失误，都会决定芯片是能正常工作还是彻底报废，而且可能出现各种问题。例如，当电磁波遇到晶体管或其他任何元件时，它的传播路径必须与后续部分实现良好的“匹配”。若无法匹配，部分能量会反向反射，而非继续向前传播。这就好比试图将高压消防水带直接连接到细的园艺水带上，若没有合适的接头，水会在连接处反向溅出，几乎无法顺利通过。在电子学中，这被称为阻抗匹配问题。

To prevent those reflections, engineers design special transitions, essentially microscopic adapters, that smooth the handoff between components. On a chip, these adapters can be surprisingly intricate. They don’t just pass the signal along; they can also split it, combine it, or distribute it across multiple paths with carefully controlled timing and strength.为了避免这些反射，工程师设计了特殊的过渡结构，本质上是微型适配器，用于平滑组件之间的信号切换。在芯片上，这些适配器的复杂程度可能超乎想象。它们不仅会传递信号，还能对信号进行拆分、合并，或是在精密控制的时序与强度下，将信号分配到多条路径中。

Once you’ve done the architecture, plumbing, and everything in between comes the moment of truth. Have all the choices you have navigated through the enormous design space resulted in an RFIC that meets its specifications? If the specifications are not met, you will have to go back, either redoing the topology or the entire architecture, and repeat the whole process. So get ready for months of time- and resource-heavy simulation and iteration. Perhaps you now see why, for decades, a core belief has persisted in the RFIC community: “RF design is an art.” It was said that only an experienced designer—with an artisanal understanding of how the pieces make up the whole—could master the subtleties of analog and RF design. Unfortunately, this entrenched notion has long held back algorithmic innovations in the field just when we need them most. Traditional, artisanal RFIC design is hitting its limits as the complexity of these systems inexorably grows.完成架构设计、布线以及中间所有环节后，关键时刻就到了。你在庞大的设计空间中做出的所有选择，最终是否打造出了一款符合规格的射频集成电路？若规格未达标，你就得回头返工，要么重新设计拓扑结构，要么推翻整个架构，然后重复全部流程。所以，你要做好准备，迎接长达数月、耗费大量时间和资源的仿真与迭代工作。或许你现在就能明白，数十年来射频集成电路领域一直秉持着一个核心理念：“射频设计是一门艺术。”据说，只有经验丰富的设计师——凭借着对各组件如何构成整体的匠心理解——才能掌握模拟与射频设计的精妙之处。遗憾的是，这一根深蒂固的观念，却在我们最需要算法创新的时期，一直阻碍着该领域的算法革新。随着这类系统的复杂度不断攀升，传统的匠心式射频集成电路设计正逐渐触及自身的极限。

## AI for RFIC Design 用于射频集成电路设计的人工智能

While RFIC designers continued their battle against their “oversized carpet” problem, a series of interesting developments emerged in allied disciplines. Across a range of other previously intractable problems like [protein folding](https://spectrum.ieee.org/alphafold-proves-that-ai-can-crack-fundamental-scientific-problems) and [climate modeling](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2023/12/ai-weather-forecasting-climate-crisis/), AI has been able to successfully navigate multidimensional complex spaces. This gave us the incentive to look deeper into AI for RF. After all, the combinatorial complexity of protein folding is not that different from the nature of the design space in our domain.在射频集成电路（RFIC）设计师继续与“超大地毯”问题作斗争的同时，相关交叉学科出现了一系列有趣的进展。在 [蛋白质折叠](https://spectrum.ieee.org/alphafold-proves-that-ai-can-crack-fundamental-scientific-problems) 、 [气候建模](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2023/12/ai-weather-forecasting-climate-crisis/) 等一系列此前难以解决的问题上，人工智能已能成功在多维复杂空间中探索路径。这促使我们深入研究将人工智能应用于射频领域的可能性。毕竟，蛋白质折叠的组合复杂性与我们所在领域的设计空间本质并无太大差异。

We were not the first to think of using artificial intelligence to speed up parts of RFIC design. Researchers had previously trained machine learning algorithms on circuit templates in the hope of speeding up the normal optimization processes. While this approach was undoubtedly faster than humans at optimizing templates, it still relied fundamentally on libraries of existing designs invented by humans.我们并非首个想到利用人工智能来加速射频集成电路（RFIC）部分设计流程的人。此前，研究人员已在电路模板上训练机器学习算法，以期加快常规优化流程。尽管这种方法在优化模板方面无疑比人类更快，但它从根本上仍依赖于人类已设计出的现有电路库。

### Training an AI to Design a Chip 训练人工智能设计芯片

A machine learning system learns to do end-to-end RFIC design like other AIs learned to play such games as Go. Essentially, it turns the process into a game, learning from the results of its own efforts.一个机器学习系统学会了端到端的射频集成电路设计，就像其他人工智能学会了下围棋等游戏一样。本质上，它将整个过程变成了一场游戏，从自身操作的结果中学习。

We didn’t want that. We wanted to break free from the restrictions of prefabricated topologies. Because while a designer’s experience and hard-won heuristics are crucial to building a working design, they also place fundamental limits on it. Furthermore, such an approach would necessarily require simulation steps as part of the optimization cycle, and even the fastest simulations use a lot of computing resources. Worse still, in many advanced cases, such as for broadband designs, there are no existing templates.我们并不想这样。我们希望摆脱预制拓扑的限制。因为尽管设计师的经验和来之不易的经验法则对于打造可行的设计至关重要，但它们也会对设计形成根本性的限制。此外，这种方法必然需要将仿真步骤作为优化周期的一部分，而即便是最快的仿真也会消耗大量计算资源。更糟糕的是，在许多高级场景中，比如宽带设计，目前并不存在现成的模板。

But if we didn’t start with templates, where could we start?但如果我们不从模板入手，又能从哪里开始呢？

The goal here was to allow algorithms to determine—entirely from scratch—every parameter for architecture, constituent circuits, and electromagnetic passives. This approach differs fundamentally from conventional optimization, which is limited to determining the parameters—like transistor dimensions and passive component geometries—that optimize structures originally devised by humans.此次研究的目标是让算法从零开始自主确定架构、组成电路以及电磁无源元件的所有参数。这种方法与传统优化有着本质区别，传统优化仅能确定晶体管尺寸、无源元件几何结构等参数，以此优化人类最初设计出的结构。

In our new approach, the architecture begins essentially from nothing and is progressively assembled through successive iterations. The system explores the design space by generating myriad candidate circuit combinations and mapping the resulting performance trade-offs as it navigates this landscape. Because the process is not biased by prior human design choices, it can produce completely novel circuit topologies that look markedly different from those created by human designers.在我们的新方法中，架构本质上从零开始构建，并通过连续迭代逐步组装而成。该系统通过生成无数候选电路组合，并在探索设计空间的过程中绘制出由此产生的性能权衡，来遍历这一设计空间。由于该过程不受人类先前设计选择的偏见影响，它能够生成全新的电路拓扑结构，这些拓扑与人类设计师创造的电路拓扑在外观上有着显著差异。

In some ways, the approach echoes AI systems such as [AlphaGo Zero](https://spectrum.ieee.org/alphago-zero-goes-from-blank-slate-to-grandmaster-in-three-dayswithout-any-help-at-all), which achieved superhuman performance not because it was trained on games played by humans but because it explored the rules by playing against itself. Similarly, our algorithm develops new circuit architectures by exploring and evaluating its own design strategies. In so doing, it learns to understand circuits, electromagnetics, and the close codesign they need to achieve the end-to-end design of RFIC.在某些方面，这一方法与 [AlphaGo Zero](https://spectrum.ieee.org/alphago-zero-goes-from-blank-slate-to-grandmaster-in-three-dayswithout-any-help-at-all) 等人工智能系统有异曲同工之妙。AlphaGo Zero之所以能达到超越人类的水平，并非因为它接受了人类对局的训练，而是因为它通过自我对弈探索规则。同样，我们的算法通过探索和评估自身设计策略来开发新的电路架构。在此过程中，它学会了理解电路、电磁学知识，以及二者协同设计的要点，从而实现射频集成电路端到端的设计。

## Inverse Design for RFICs 射频集成电路的逆向设计

To realize this capability, we proceeded in two stages. First, we developed a [reinforcement-learning](https://spectrum.ieee.org/reinforcement-learning-environments) (RL) framework that determines the optimal system architecture, circuit topology, device parameters, and even the properties of the electromagnetic interfaces that connect different circuit elements. In this stage, the algorithm effectively defines how signals should propagate and interact across the system.为实现这一能力，我们分两个阶段推进。首先，我们开发了一个 [强化学习](https://spectrum.ieee.org/reinforcement-learning-environments) （RL）框架，该框架可确定最优的系统架构、电路拓扑、器件参数，甚至连接不同电路元件的电磁接口特性。在这一阶段，该算法明确了信号在系统中应如何传播和交互。

The algorithm trains very similarly to how a computer learns to play a game. If you let it play enough times, it can learn to play better by observing the relationship between the actions it took and the score it achieves. In a similar way, the RL agent here learns to design effective circuits by playing with a set of combinations, and over time, it can map the space between the circuit performance to its architecture, topology, and parameters. This training takes a few days to a week, but once trained, the agent can design circuits very quickly 该算法的训练过程与计算机学习玩游戏的方式极为相似。如果让它进行足够多次的尝试，它就能通过观察自身采取的行动与所获分数之间的关系，不断提升游戏水平。 与之类似，这里的强化学习智能体通过尝试多种组合来学习设计高效的电路，久而久之，它能建立起电路性能与其架构、拓扑结构及参数之间的关联映射。 这种训练过程需要几天到一周的时间，但一旦训练完成，该智能体就能快速地完成电路设计工作。

The next step was to determine the physical structure of the IC’s electromagnetics—the plumbing—that can create the desired properties of the passive elements, which are characterized by a set of metrics called scattering parameters. These measure if a signal entering a component actually moves forward—or is reflecting backward, being wasted, as in our previous example with the fire hose and the garden hose.下一步是确定集成电路电磁系统的物理结构——即其“管路”——这种结构能够使无源元件具备所需的特性，而无源元件的特性由一组名为散射参数的指标来表征。这些指标可检测输入元件的信号是真正向前传输，还是像我们之前消防水带与花园水带的例子那样，被反射回去而造成损耗。

Deriving the structure from the desired scattering parameters is an example of an approach called inverse design, which appears across many areas of engineering. In structural engineering, for example, one might collaborate with an architect on a physical goal—such as creating large interior spaces with high ceilings—and then determine the arrangement of arches or buttresses that can support it.根据所需散射参数推导结构是一种被称为逆设计的方法示例，该方法广泛应用于工程的众多领域。以结构工程为例，工程师可能会与建筑师协作确定一个物理目标——比如打造挑高的大型室内空间——然后找出能够支撑这一目标的拱券或扶壁的布置方式。

### Generative AI for Electromagnetic Networks 面向电磁网络的生成式人工智能

In an effort to make AI-designed circuits more understandable, engineers took a page from image-generation AIs that allow users to create pictures in the style of different artists. Here, instead of an artist\\u2019s style, the user can dial in the spatial frequency of an electromagnetic structure. Regardless of how pixelated the structure is, it will still reproduce the needed electromagnetic characteristics, or S-parameters.为了让人工智能设计的电路更易于理解，工程师借鉴了图像生成人工智能的思路——这类人工智能允许用户以不同艺术家的风格创作图片。在此次设计中，用户可以调整电磁结构的空间频率，而非艺术家的风格。无论该结构的像素化程度如何，它都能还原出所需的电磁特性，即S参数。 Chris Philpot 克里斯·菲尔波特

But RF integrated crcuits pose a particular challenge for inverse design: The process must account simultaneously for circuit behavior and the electromagnetic responses of the interconnects and passive elements that link them together. But it has to figure that out without doing a lot of artisanal iterating.但射频集成电路给逆向设计带来了特殊挑战：该过程必须同时考虑电路行为以及将它们连接在一起的互连和无源元件的电磁响应。而且还必须在不进行大量手工迭代的情况下完成这一任务。

So we replaced our RF circuit simulator with an AI-based emulator. This AI model can predict the behavior of electromagnetic fields going through any structure—even totally arbitrary two-dimensional shapes—without having to compute the underlying physics from scratch, as simulation tools do. It would predict the solution of Maxwell’s equations and tell you the scattering parameters for any structure you showed it, without actually doing the math. With such an AI in hand, what a time-consuming electromagnetic solver normally takes minutes or hours to accomplish is reduced to milliseconds.于是我们用基于人工智能的仿真器替换了射频电路仿真器。这款人工智能模型能够预测电磁场穿过任意结构——甚至完全不规则的二维形状——的行为，无需像仿真工具那样从头计算基础物理原理。它可以预测麦克斯韦方程组的解，并为你展示的任意结构给出散射参数，无需实际进行数学运算。有了这样的人工智能，原本需要电磁求解器花费数分钟甚至数小时才能完成的任务，耗时被缩短到了毫秒级。

We chose to build our emulator around a [convolutional neural network](https://spectrum.ieee.org/facebook-ai-director-yann-lecun-on-deep-learning) —a machine learning model that has been remarkably successful for image processing. Such networks can extract spatial features from any structure, and it turns out that the image of a structure contains a lot of spatial information that can accurately predict its electromagnetic performance. Then we trained it on a vast number of random pixelated structures whose scattering parameters had been labeled.我们选择围绕 [卷积神经网络](https://spectrum.ieee.org/facebook-ai-director-yann-lecun-on-deep-learning) 构建我们的模拟器，这是一种在图像处理领域取得显著成功的机器学习模型。此类网络能够从任意结构中提取空间特征，而事实证明，一个结构的图像包含着大量空间信息，这些信息可以准确预测其电磁性能。随后，我们基于大量随机的像素化结构对其进行了训练，这些结构的散射参数均已完成标注。

Once we had our inverse-design RL and suitable AI emulator, we essentially had an [end-to-end AI designer](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10904600). So we asked it to design us a power amplifier.一旦我们有了反向强化学习模型和合适的AI模拟器，我们实际上就拥有了一个 [端到端AI设计器](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10904600) 。于是我们让它为我们设计一款功率放大器。

## Unconventional RF Architectures 非传统射频架构

In 2023, [we published this proof of concept](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10136184) —a power amplifier targeting the millimeter-wave band, specifically spanning 30 to 100 GHz, which covers most of the relevant 5G and radar frequencies. The final design achieved the best combination of wide bandwidth, output power, and efficiency then reported for a silicon-based power amplifier—meaning it could amplify a large amount of data across a wide swath of frequencies—while maintaining record efficiency.2023年， [我们发布了这项概念验证研究](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10136184) ——这是一款针对毫米波频段的功率放大器，具体覆盖30至100吉赫兹，涵盖了大部分相关的5G和雷达频段。最终设计实现了当时硅基功率放大器在宽带宽、输出功率和效率方面的最佳组合——这意味着它能在较宽的频率范围内放大大量数据，同时保持了创纪录的效率。

The structure of the IC’s electromagnetic pathways was unlike anything any human would ever consider. Since the AI is not trained on human designs, the layout that emerged looked more like an arbitrary pattern or perhaps a QR code than the regular symmetrical structures we are used to seeing.集成电路的电磁通路结构是人类从未设想过的形态。由于该人工智能未基于人类设计进行训练，最终形成的布局看起来更像是一种随机图案，甚至可能像二维码，而非我们习惯的规则对称结构。

One unexpected insight revealed by this prototype, and our research generally, is that there’s no evidence that the templates we’ve historically relied on are even close to optimal for modern design goals. It’s not that a human designer can never come up with a better design. But with the removal of the templates and the time to synthesize cycle upon cycle of optimized circuits, it is now clear that AI-driven synthesis could break traditional design barriers and push the limits of RFIC capabilities.这个原型以及我们的整体研究揭示了一个出人意料的发现：没有证据表明，我们过去一直依赖的模板在实现现代设计目标方面接近最优状态。这并非意味着人类设计师永远无法设计出更优的方案。但随着模板被摒弃，以及我们得以逐周期合成优化电路，如今已能清晰看到，AI 驱动的综合技术有望打破传统设计壁垒，突破射频集成电路（RFIC）性能的极限。

Our 5G amplifier had only one input port and one output port. Adding more inputs and outputs to a design is not straightforward. Every port electromagnetically couples to every other port, so the scattering parameters quickly add up. Two ports give you four scattering parameters. Four ports, 16 scattering parameters. The math gets ugly fast. Could our model keep up?我们的5G放大器只有一个输入端口和一个输出端口。在设计中增加更多的输入和输出并非易事。每个端口都会与其他所有端口发生电磁耦合，因此散射参数会迅速增加。两个端口对应四个散射参数，四个端口则对应十六个散射参数。这种计算很快就会变得复杂棘手。我们的模型能应对吗？

We next trained our model on larger classes of electromagnetic structures with many input and output ports. In 2024, we published work showing that [multiport integrated circuits](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10600352) are no problem for these AI algorithms either. Where previously multiport electromagnetic simulation required days or weeks of toil, this model evolved new structures in minutes. Since then, a plethora of work in the space by research communities across the globe have demonstrated the power of inverse design in RFIC.接下来，我们在具有多个输入和输出端口的更大类别电磁结构上训练了我们的模型。2024年，我们发表的研究表明， [多端口集成电路](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10600352) 对这些人工智能算法来说也不是问题。过去，多端口电磁仿真需要数天或数周的艰苦工作，而该模型仅需数分钟就能设计出新的结构。从那以后，全球研究机构在该领域开展了大量研究，证明了逆向设计在射频集成电路中的强大能力。

Combining the reinforcement learning framework with the inverse design, we now had the ability to create an RFIC from specifications all the way to a [fabrication-ready layout](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11015614). We’ve so far shown this is true for RFICs ranging from low-noise amplifiers to [subterahertz](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-54178-1) and broadband [power amplifiers](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISSCC49661.2025.10904600)****.**** The hope is that this will work just as well for other circuits.将强化学习框架与逆向设计相结合，我们如今已具备从规格要求一路打造出 [可制造版图](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11015614) 的射频集成电路（RFIC）的能力。到目前为止，我们已验证该方法适用于从低噪声放大器到 [太赫兹以下](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-54178-1) 频段以及宽带 [功率放大器](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISSCC49661.2025.10904600) 的各类射频集成电路 ****。**** 我们希望该方法在其他电路上也能同样适用。

## Making AI Designs Interpretable 让AI设计具备可解释性

Our goal was to make RFIC design better and easier, but we didn’t want to make it beyond human understanding. Chip testing and debugging is a long, arduous process, sometimes even more so than design. Engineers often prefer ICs to have interpretable structures, so that if a problem crops up, they can understand how the chip works well enough to debug it.我们的目标是让射频集成电路（RFIC）的设计变得更完善、更简单，但我们并不想让它超出人类的理解范围。芯片测试与调试是一个漫长而艰巨的过程，有时甚至比设计本身还要棘手。工程师通常更希望集成电路具备可解读的结构，这样一旦出现问题，他们就能充分理解芯片的工作原理，进而开展调试工作。

To create structures that are more interpretable, we turned to [diffusion models](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-art-generator), which you may know from their remarkable ability to generate realistic images from text prompts.为了创建更具可解释性的结构，我们转向了 [扩散模型](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-art-generator) ，你可能已经了解到它们具备从文本提示词生成逼真图像的出色能力。

AI-driven synthesis could break traditional design barriers and push the limits of RFIC capabilities. 人工智能驱动的综合技术有望打破传统设计壁垒，突破射频集成电路（RFIC）性能的极限。

Imagine you go to your favorite image-generation engine and ask it to create a painting of the sky in the style of Picasso, Van Gogh, or Michelangelo. You will get images that capture the essence of their brushstrokes, their use of colors, and their framing. All are pictures of the sky nonetheless, but in different styles.想象你打开最喜欢的图像生成引擎，让它创作一幅以毕加索、梵高或米开朗基罗风格呈现天空的画作。你得到的图像会捕捉到他们笔触的精髓、色彩的运用以及画面构图。尽管都是天空的图像，却呈现出不同的风格。

Electromagnetic design is similar in that multiple structures can have very similar electromagnetic responses. Instead of using text input, we used scattering parameters as our input, and the electromagnetic structure of an RFIC chip as our output. As part of the inputs to the [diffusion model](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11103838), we created a [dial that sets the spatial frequency of the final structure](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11409170). By turning the dial, a designer can direct the model to synthesize structures with low (classical-looking and interpretable), medium (mazelike structures), or high (pixelated or arbitrarily-shaped) spatial frequency.电磁设计的相似之处在于，多种结构可能具有极为相似的电磁响应。我们没有采用文本输入，而是将散射参数作为输入，将射频集成电路（RFIC）芯片的电磁结构作为输出。作为扩散模型输入的一部分，我们设计了一个 [用于设定最终结构空间频率的旋钮](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11103838) 。通过调节该旋钮，设计者可以指导模型生成具有低（外观经典且可解释）、中（迷宫状结构）或高（像素化或任意形状）空间频率的结构。

From prompts to output, the entire process took about 6 minutes. With this diffusion model, algorithms can now both discover novel architectures **and** accelerate the creation of conventional, so-called classical ones.从提示词到输出，整个过程大约耗时6分钟。借助这一扩散模型，算法如今既能发现全新的架构，也能加快传统（即经典）架构的生成速度。

All an RFIC designer needs to do is specify virtually any valid set of scattering parameters. As long as they are physically realizable under Maxwell’s equations, the model pops out a corresponding structure as if it were a vending machine.射频集成电路设计师只需指定几乎任意一组有效的散射参数即可。只要这些参数在麦克斯韦方程组下具有物理可实现性，该模型就能生成对应的结构，就像自动售货机一样。

## The Future of AI-Driven RFIC DesignAI驱动的射频集成电路设计的未来

The results of our investigations have drawn the attention of the RF community. The traditional bottom-up design process is clearly beginning to reverse.我们调查的结果引起了射频社区的关注。传统的自下而上设计流程显然正开始发生逆转。

But there are still questions: How generalizable are these methods? Can they consistently deliver truly high performance? Can we get to a place where AI produces designs that maximize every conceivable trade-off, holistically optimizing every parameter to its most ideal physical state? We want to take this strategy beyond RFIC design and invent other kinds of circuits that are different from anything humans have ever done.但仍有一些问题亟待解决：这些方法的泛化能力如何？它们能否持续实现真正的高性能？我们能否实现这样的目标——AI 生成的设计能最大化所有合理的权衡，从整体上优化每个参数至其最理想的物理状态？我们希望将这一策略拓展到射频集成电路（RFIC）设计之外，研发出与人类以往设计的任何电路都截然不同的新型电路。

These are exciting and ambitious prospects, but we are not there yet. AI can hallucinate a design that creates bad circuits that don’t work. This means verification methods need to remain under human oversight. And, while hallucinations are rare, it would still be good to reduce their occurrence.这些前景令人振奋且充满雄心，但我们尚未实现目标。人工智能可能会“幻觉”出一种设计，从而生成无法正常工作的劣质电路。这意味着验证方法仍需处于人类监督之下。而且，尽管“幻觉”现象较为罕见，但减少其发生仍然是很有必要的。

History suggests that meeting these dreams of the future will take much more data than we’ve been using. Before the creation of the ImageNet repository—a repository of 14 million varied, human-annotated images—image-recognition models didn’t function well in the real world. The datasets they had been trained on were too tiny to be effective. ImageNet’s massive amounts of training data ushered in a revolution that led to AI that can generalize and recognize images in the wild. The rest was history.历史表明，实现这些未来愿景所需的数据量将远超我们目前所使用的。在ImageNet数据集诞生之前——这是一个包含1400万张多样化人工标注图像的数据集——图像识别模型在现实世界中表现不佳。它们所训练的数据集规模太小，无法发挥有效作用。ImageNet海量的训练数据开启了一场革命，催生了能够在真实场景中进行泛化和图像识别的人工智能。此后的发展便成为了历史。

If the goal for RFIC and analog design is a universal foundational model—something that learns the governing laws of electromagnetics and circuit behavior—then we also need data.如果射频集成电路（RFIC）和模拟设计的目标是打造一个通用基础模型——即能够学习电磁学和电路运行规律的模型——那么我们同样需要数据支持。

The good news is that this data is plentiful. Around the world, countless engineers at companies and academic labs simulate nearly identical RF circuits and passive structures every day. The bad news is that it’s all locked away behind nondisclosure agreements.好消息是，这类数据十分丰富。在全球范围内，企业和学术实验室里的无数工程师每天都在模拟几乎完全相同的射频电路和无源结构。但坏消息是，所有这些数据都被保密协议牢牢锁定。

Open ecosystems have propelled other areas, and we think the RFIC community should do the same. There had been some movement toward this. [Natcast](https://spectrum.ieee.org/natcast-layoffs), the operator of the [U.S. CHIPS and Science Act’s R&D program](https://www.nist.gov/chips/research-development-programs), would have bolstered shared infrastructure and innovation for the next generation of wireless, sensing, and defense technologies. Unfortunately, both the organization and the [program](https://www.nist.gov/chips/princeton-university-princeton) it ran specifically for machine learning and RFICs have been closed.开放型生态系统已经推动了其他领域的发展，我们认为射频集成电路（RFIC）领域也应该如此。这一方向已经有了一些进展。 [Natcast](https://spectrum.ieee.org/natcast-layoffs) 作为 [美国《芯片与科学法案》研发项目](https://www.nist.gov/chips/research-development-programs) 的运营方，本可为下一代无线、传感和国防技术强化共享基础设施与创新能力。遗憾的是，该组织以及它专门为机器学习和射频集成电路（RFICs）运营的 [项目](https://www.nist.gov/chips/princeton-university-princeton) 都已关停。

But the momentum Natcast’s effort sparked hasn’t died out. Building on our early work, groups across the community have already demonstrated remarkable advances. AI-driven IC design is part of a much broader technological shift. From biology and materials science to automotive and aerospace engineering, AI is reshaping how complex systems are conceived and optimized. Deeper collaboration between AI researchers and chip designers will unlock the field’s full potential. It’s by no means a foregone conclusion, but if we get this right, this genie won’t stay in its bottle. 但 Natcast 所做努力所激发的势头并未消退。在早期工作的基础上，行业内的各个团队已经取得了显著进展。人工智能驱动的集成电路设计是一场更为广泛的技术变革的一部分。从生物学、材料科学到汽车与航空航天工程，人工智能正在重塑复杂系统的设计与优化方式。人工智能研究人员与芯片设计师之间的深度合作将释放该领域的全部潜力。这绝非板上钉钉的事，但如果我们能做好这件事，这个新事物的影响将无法被束缚。
