## Retro-Tech Parenting 复古科技育儿

![A photograph taken on a wooden tabletop showing a collection of retro-tech items including a CD player, a stack of CDs and a wired telephone](https://havenweb.org/assets/images/retro.jpg)

I am a technologist. I enjoy the things that computers and digital devices can do–they often seem magical and amazing! As a technologist, I have a particular vantage point to see and be *very* uncomfortable with what companies are doing with that technology. There are catchphrases for these patterns: AdTech, Surveillance Capitalism, Rage Bait, Engagement-Optimized Feeds, Harvesting Eyeballs.我是一名技术专家。我喜欢电脑和数字设备所能实现的功能——它们常常显得神奇又令人惊叹！但作为技术专家，我却有一个特别的视角，能看到并对企业利用这项技术所做的一切感到 *非常* 不适。这些行为模式有一些流行说法：广告技术、监控资本主义、愤怒诱饵、互动优化信息流、流量收割。

I am also a parent. As a parent, I am scared about the prospect of letting my kids loose in a digital world so aggressively dominated by these companies and patterns. But at the same time, technology was an enriching part of my childhood and continues to be an enriching part of my life and I want to share that with them. In this post, I don’t want to spend much time discussing the ways that *bad tech patterns are bad*, instead I want to share some of the ways I have held on to the enriching parts of technology to share them with my kids. For many of these choices, it turns out that (surprise!) my favorite solutions involve looking back in time a couple decades.我也是一位家长。作为家长，我对让孩子置身于一个被这些公司和模式强势主导的数字世界这件事感到担忧。但与此同时，科技曾是我童年中丰富成长的一部分，如今也依然是我生活中不可或缺的丰富元素，我想把这份美好分享给孩子们。在这篇文章中，我不想过多探讨 *不良的科技模式为何有害* ，而是想分享一些我如何保留科技中富有价值的部分，并传递给孩子们。事实证明，在很多选择上（出乎意料的是），我最心仪的解决方案都需要回望过去几十年。

### Physical media 实体媒体

I’m starting to fall in love with CDs again. When I was young, music came on CDs. This was a time before MP3s and before Spotify. This was a time when going to summer camp, I would pick which half-dozen CDs I wanted to bring with on the trip. This was a time of wired ear-bud headphones, sitting in school sharing a song with someone by giving them one of the two ear buds and listening side-by-side. This was a time of \[Parental Advisory\] labels on CDs that had objectionable language. This was a time of tiny LCD screens on portable CD players that only displayed the track number, so I sometimes knew my favorite song on a CD by number instead of by name.我又开始重新爱上CD了。小时候，音乐都是以CD形式呈现的。那是MP3和Spotify出现之前的年代。那时候去夏令营，我会挑选五六张CD带上旅途。那时候用的是有线耳塞式耳机，在学校里，我会把一只耳机分给别人，和对方并肩坐着分享一首歌。那时候，含有不当语言的CD上会贴着【家长指导】标签。那时候的便携式CD播放器上只有小小的液晶显示屏，只能显示曲目编号，所以我有时是靠编号而不是歌名来记住CD上我最喜欢的那首歌的。

I bought a mini CD boom box for the house. My oldest loves bringing it around to different rooms, plugging it in, and putting in a CD. I bought her the *K-Pop Demon Hunters* CD for her birthday. The local public library has CDs! CDs are awesome.我给家里买了一个迷你CD便携音响。我的大女儿特别喜欢把它拿到不同的房间，插上电，再放上CD。我在她生日时给她买了 *韩流恶魔猎人* 这张CD。当地的公共图书馆居然也有CD！CD真是太赞了。

Speaking of the public library, they also have DVDs and BluRays. I remember the whole ritual of going to Blockbuster Video with my Dad to pick out a movie for family movie night. Usually he would let my sister and me pick out a few extras that we wanted to watch. That’s how I got familiar with the Three Stooges, even though it was before my time. The magic of having something to hold onto that you can bring home and put in the player next to the TV, and there is your movie!说到公共图书馆，这里也有DVD和蓝光影碟。我还记得小时候和爸爸一起去百视达音像店，为家庭电影夜挑选电影的整个流程。通常他会让我和妹妹选几部我们想看的附加影片。我就是这样认识了《活宝三人组》，尽管那时候我还没出生。那种能把东西带回家，放到电视旁的播放器里，电影就出现在眼前的感觉，真是太奇妙了！

As a parent one of the big wins with physical media is I know exactly what my kids have available to experience. If they aren’t ready for something, it doesn’t come home with us. The kids can be much more independent about watching and listening because there is no adversary inside the device they are using. Kids love that independence.作为家长，实体媒体的一大优势是我能清楚地知道孩子们可以接触到哪些内容。如果他们还没准备好接触某些东西，这些东西就不会被带回家。孩子们在观看和收听内容时可以更加独立，因为他们使用的设备里没有会干扰他们的内容。孩子们很喜欢这种独立性。

### Landline Telephones 固定电话

I hooked up a wired, physical telephone next to the kitchen in our house. I’m using a cheap VoIP provider with an analogue telephone adapter, but there’s a company called Tin Can that makes this really easy (no affiliation). The telephone network has retained backward compatibility as everyone moved to smartphones, so grandparents, neighbors, aunts and uncles, are all now accessible to the kids. Even better, these digitally-managed phones have excellent configuration options. I’ve whitelisted all the friends and family who get to call us, and the phone automatically blocks calls from dinner time to morning. My kids will spontaneously call up their grandparents to ask if they can go over to play, and have memorized my phone number because they like to prank call me when I’m also in the kitchen.我在自家厨房旁边装了一部有线实体电话。我用的是一家便宜的VoIP运营商，搭配了一个模拟电话适配器，不过有一家名为Tin Can的公司能让这件事变得非常简单（我和该公司没有任何关联）。大家都改用智能手机后，电话网络依然保留了向下兼容性，所以孩子们现在能联系到爷爷奶奶、邻居和叔伯姑姨了。更棒的是，这些数字化管理的电话有非常完善的配置选项。我把所有能给我们打电话的亲友都加入了白名单，电话还会自动在晚餐到次日清晨的时段拦截来电。我的孩子们会主动给爷爷奶奶打电话，问能不能过去玩，还记住了我的电话号码，因为他们总喜欢在我也在厨房的时候给我打恶作剧电话。

When I was a kid, the telephone was my bridge to friends and setting up my own playdates. It was “do you want to come over to my house?” instead of “Dad! Can you set up a playdate with so-and-so?” There is still a bit of a network effect until other families get hooked up with their own house phones, but I’m really excited about it. Kids really love that independence.小时候，电话是我联系朋友、自己安排玩伴聚会的桥梁。那时我会问“你想来我家吗？”，而不是喊“爸爸！你能帮我和某某约个玩伴聚会吗？”在其他家庭也装上自家电话之前，这种网络效应还会持续一阵子，不过我对此真的很兴奋。孩子们真的很享受这种独立感。

### The Family Computer 家庭电脑

I remember having friends over and going to friends’ houses and one of our favorite things to do was play computer games. This was the era of Commander Keen, and Prince of Persia. We would sit at the family computer, side-by-side, taking turns, discussing strategy. I definitely have games I want to share with my kids, but the internet is also not something I trust. I bought a used tower PC from Ebay and set it up next to the kitchen. Each kid has their own login and their own games or activities they like to explore. I also set up a pi-hole for our home network and configured the family computer to use the pi-hole for DNS. Just like the phone, I’ve whitelisted every domain that they can visit. For example: they get access to Wikipedia, but not Google. They get access to Minecraft, but we don’t play on public servers. No Youtube or Spotify, but I’ve curated some sites about how to solve a Rubik’s cube, or different ways to tie your shoes.我记得以前会叫朋友来家里玩，也会去朋友家做客，我们最爱做的事之一就是玩电脑游戏。那是《指挥官基恩》和《波斯王子》的时代。我们会并肩坐在家里的电脑前，轮流玩游戏，还会一起讨论策略。我当然有想和孩子们分享的游戏，但我对互联网也并不放心。我从易趣网买了一台二手台式电脑，把它装在了厨房旁边。每个孩子都有自己的登录账号，还有各自喜欢探索的游戏和活动。我还为家庭网络搭建了一个树莓派黑洞（pi-hole），并把家里的电脑设置成通过这个pi-hole进行域名系统（DNS）解析。和手机的设置一样，我把他们可以访问的所有域名都加入了白名单。比如，他们可以访问维基百科，但不能用谷歌；可以玩《我的世界》，但我们不登录公共服务器。他们不能用油管（YouTube）和声破天（Spotify），不过我筛选了一些网站，内容包括如何还原魔方，以及系鞋带的各种方法。

I showed my older kid that she could rip a CD onto the computer, and listen to it there. For now she’s just excited that she has yet another place she can listen to her favorite K-Pop Demon Hunters song, but maybe this is the start of building up her own music collection as I’ve done over the decades. Kids love being able to use a computer on their own!我给大女儿演示了如何把CD翻录到电脑上，然后在电脑上播放。目前她只是很开心，自己又多了一个地方能听最喜欢的K-Pop组合“猎魔人”的歌曲，不过这或许是我几十年来打造自己的音乐收藏后，她开始建立属于自己的音乐库的开端。孩子们就是喜欢能独立使用电脑的感觉！

I opened by calling myself a technologist. I recognize that a lot of the tools I’ve described above aren’t as accessible to less-technical parents, but the core philosophy is definitely still accessible. The dystopian parts of modern technology came to dominate because they are very convenient–but that convenience comes at a cost. Especially when kids are involved, it can be really rewarding to refuse paying that cost and sometimes even look to the past for inspiration.我以自称技术专家的方式开场。我也意识到，上述提到的很多工具对技术水平没那么高的父母来说并非触手可及，但其核心理念绝对是易于理解的。现代科技中那些反乌托邦的元素之所以占据主导地位，是因为它们极为便捷——但这种便捷是有代价的。尤其是涉及到孩子时，拒绝付出这种代价，有时甚至从过去汲取灵感，会带来实实在在的积极意义。
