In a makeshift demonstration kitchen in Concord, California, cooking oil splatters in and around a frying pan, which catches fire on an unattended gas stove. Within moments, a smoke detector wails. But in this demonstration, something less common happens: An AI-driven sensor activates and wall emitters blast infrasound waves toward the source of the fire in an attempt to put it out.在加利福尼亚州康科德的一个临时示范厨房里，食用油溅落在一个油锅内外，锅中无人看管的煤气炉起火。片刻之后，烟雾报警器发出尖锐的警报声。但在这次演示中，发生了一件较少见的事：一个由人工智能驱动的传感器启动，墙壁发射器向火源发射次声波，试图扑灭火灾。

The science of acoustic fire suppression, which has long been [known](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/FOID/Reading%20Room/Science_and_Technology/13-F-1078_REPORT_FLAME_SUPPRESSION_ACOUSTIC_SUPPRESSION.pdf) and [documented in scientific literature](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C5&q=acoustic+fire+suppression&btnG=&oq=acoustic+fire+sup) and the [press](https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-18870258), works by vibrating oxygen molecules away from a fuel source, depriving the fire of a critical component needed for combustion.声学灭火科学长期以来已被 [科学](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/FOID/Reading%20Room/Science_and_Technology/13-F-1078_REPORT_FLAME_SUPPRESSION_ACOUSTIC_SUPPRESSION.pdf) 文献和 [媒体](https://www.bbc.com/news/technology-18870258) [记录](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?hl=en&as_sdt=0%2C5&q=acoustic+fire+suppression&btnG=&oq=acoustic+fire+sup) ，其原理是将氧气分子振动远离燃料源，剥夺火焰燃烧所需的关键成分。

Indeed, after just a few seconds of infrasound, the tiny kitchen blaze goes out.事实上，仅仅几秒的次声波响起，这场微小的厨房火灾就会熄灭。

The demonstration I witnessed took place in the presence of numerous firefighters and officials from Contra Costa County Fire Protection District, the state’s premier wildland firefighting agency ([CAL FIRE](https://www.fire.ca.gov/)), and invited journalists.我目睹的示威活动在众多消防员和康特拉科斯塔县消防保护区官员（该州顶级野外消防机构 [CAL FIRE）](https://www.fire.ca.gov/) 以及受邀记者的见证下进行。

“We were able to not just point-and-shoot like a fire extinguisher; we figured out how to run it through ducting and distribute it like a sprinkler system,” said Geoff Bruder, co-founder and CEO of [Sonic Fire Tech](https://www.sonicfiretech.com/), during the presentation.“我们不仅能像灭火器那样指着就射;我们找到了如何通过管道引导火力，并像喷淋系统一样分配，“ [Sonic Fire Tech](https://www.sonicfiretech.com/) 联合创始人兼首席执行官Geoff Bruder在演示中说。

The company’s goal is to replace sprinklers, which are effective at stopping fires but can also do significant water damage to a property. Sonic Fire Tech appears to be the first company trying to commercialize the science of acoustic fire suppression. Its executives have already been touring Southern California; Wednesday’s event was the first in the northern half of the state.公司的目标是更换喷淋器，这些喷头不仅有效扑灭火灾，也可能对财产造成严重水损害。Sonic Fire Tech似乎是首家尝试商业化声学灭火科学的公司。其高管们已经在南加州巡回演出;周三的活动是该州北部的首场活动。

The company aims to make this infrasound technique mainstream in both commercial (for instance, a data center, where sprinklers would damage electronics) and in-home installations, given that sprinklers are already [required](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bt9DxE7J-b8) in all new California homes built in 2011 and later.公司旨在使这种次声技术成为商业（例如数据中心，喷淋器会损坏电子设备）和家庭安装的主流，因为2011年及以后所有新建的加州住宅都 [已要求](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bt9DxE7J-b8) 喷淋系统。

Sonic Fire Tech also hopes to produce a backpack-based system that could be worn by wildland firefighters headed out into the field.Sonic Fire Tech还希望开发一款基于背包的系统，供野外消防员佩戴。

“We are making meaningful technological improvements on a monthly basis,” Stefan Pollack, a company spokesperson, emailed Ars after the event.“我们每个月都在进行有意义的技术改进，”公司发言人斯特凡·波拉克在活动后通过电子邮件告诉Ars。

But two experts who spoke with Ars raised serious questions about the potential for this technology to supplant traditional sprinklers in a home. They are even more skeptical as to whether the technique can be effective in an uncontrolled wildfire situation, where flames can grow very quickly.但两位与Ars交流的专家提出了关于这项技术取代传统家庭喷淋系统的潜在潜力的严重质疑。他们对该技术在火势可能迅速蔓延的失控野火中是否有效持怀疑态度。

## Sprinkler replacement? 更换洒水器？

Sonic Fire Tech says that its system is as good as, if not better than, traditional sprinklers for many applications.Sonic Fire Tech表示，其系统在许多应用中与传统洒水器相当，甚至更好。

“Sonic Fire Tech is in fact intended to replace interior residential sprinklers,” Pollack told Ars. “The demo showed a critical benefit of SFT over water sprinklers in suppressing a kitchen fire, which represents about half of all residential fires. This is also applicable to commercial kitchen fires and other common grease and chemical fire applications.” “Sonic Fire Tech 实际上旨在替代住宅室内喷淋系统，”Pollack 告诉 Ars。“演示显示，SFT相较水洒在扑灭厨房火灾方面具有关键优势，而厨房火灾约占所有住宅火灾的一半。这同样适用于商业厨房火灾及其他常见的油脂和化学火灾应用。”

The company’s press releases tout infrasound’s advantages over sprinklers. “Traditional residential sprinklers activate several minutes only after heat rises to a threshold, can discharge large volumes of water that damage interiors and electronics, and require plumbing infrastructure that adds cost and complexity,” says [one release](https://www.sonicfiretech.com/news/sonic-fire-tech-included-in-los-angeles-countys-first-ever-approved-3d-printed-concrete-home-permit-lands-nfpa-13d-equivalent-rating-and-wows-san-bernardino-county-fire-in-live-test). “Sonic Home Defense, by contrast, deploys in milliseconds and uses inaudible low-frequency infrasound waves to disrupt the chemistry of combustion before flames can spread, with no water, no chemicals, and no risk of flooding the interior of the home being protected.” 该公司的新闻稿强调次声波相较于喷淋系统的优势。 [一份新闻稿](https://www.sonicfiretech.com/news/sonic-fire-tech-included-in-los-angeles-countys-first-ever-approved-3d-printed-concrete-home-permit-lands-nfpa-13d-equivalent-rating-and-wows-san-bernardino-county-fire-in-live-test) 称：“传统住宅喷淋器只有在热量达到阈值后才会启动几分钟，可能排放大量水，损坏室内和电子设备，并且需要增加成本和复杂性的管道基础设施。”“相比之下，声波家庭防御在毫秒内部署，利用无声的低频次声波干扰燃烧化学，避免火焰蔓延，无需水、化学品，也不会有淹没室内的风险。”

The goals sound great, but they do raise questions among outside observers.这些目标听起来很棒，但确实引发了外界观察者的疑问。

“Sprinklers have a well-established role,” [Nate Wittasek](https://www.linkedin.com/in/natewittasek), a Los Angeles-based fire protection engineer, emailed Ars. “They apply water directly to the fuel, cool the space, slow or stop flashover, and give people time to get out while reducing risk to firefighters. Sound may knock down a small flame, but it does not cool hot surfaces or wet fuel. That raises real questions about re-ignition, smoldering fires, hidden fires, and fires that are partially blocked by contents.” 洛杉矶消防工程师 [Nate Wittasek](https://www.linkedin.com/in/natewittasek) 在给Ars发邮件时说：“洒水器有着明确的作用。”“它们直接向燃料浇水，冷却空间，减缓或阻止闪燃，给人们时间撤离，同时降低消防员的风险。声音可以击倒小火焰，但不会冷却高温表面或湿燃料。这引发了关于重新点燃、闷燃火灾、隐蔽火灾以及部分被内容物阻挡的火灾等真实问题。”

Water sprinklers have been around for a long time. The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), a well-known industry nonprofit, was founded in the late 1800s to develop a uniform standard for sprinklers. The latest iteration of those guidelines, [known as the “13D” standard](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-13d-standard-development/13d), is well documented and widely adopted.水洒水器已经存在很长时间了。美国国家消防协会（NFPA）是一家知名的行业非营利组织，成立于19世纪末，旨在制定喷淋系统的统一标准。这些指南的最新版本被称为 [“13D”标准](https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards/nfpa-13d-standard-development/13d) ，已有充分文献记载并被广泛采用。

A [recent press release](https://www.sonicfiretech.com/news/sonic-fire-tech-included-in-los-angeles-countys-first-ever-approved-3d-printed-concrete-home-permit-lands-nfpa-13d-equivalent-rating-and-wows-san-bernardino-county-fire-in-live-test) from Sonic Fire Tech states that the company has “secured third-party validation of its system as a viable NFPA 13D-equivalent alternative to conventional residential sprinklers.” Sonic Fire Tech [最近的一份新闻稿](https://www.sonicfiretech.com/news/sonic-fire-tech-included-in-los-angeles-countys-first-ever-approved-3d-printed-concrete-home-permit-lands-nfpa-13d-equivalent-rating-and-wows-san-bernardino-county-fire-in-live-test) 称，公司已“获得第三方验证其系统作为传统住宅喷淋系统的可行NFPA 13D等效替代品”。

The company told Ars that it has been evaluated by James Andy Lynch (who was present at the demonstration) and his team at [Fire Solutions Group](https://firesolutionsgroup.com/), a Pennsylvania-based consultancy, to establish Sonic Fire Tech’s bona fides.该公司告诉Ars，这款产品已由宾夕法尼亚州 [咨询公司Fire Solutions Group](https://firesolutionsgroup.com/) 的James Andy Lynch及其团队评估，以确定Sonic Fire Tech的资讯。

Sonic Fire Tech declined to provide Ars with a full copy of Lynch’s report, citing “confidential and patent-pending information,” but it did send Ars the two-page executive summary.Sonic Fire Tech拒绝向Ars提供林奇报告的完整副本，理由是“机密且专利申请中的信息”，但向Ars发送了两页的执行摘要。

This document states that “the Sonic Fire Tech system is capable of delivering extremely rapid fire detection, meaningful suppression or extinguishment, and consistent performance across a variety of installation configurations.” 该文件指出，“声波消防技术系统能够实现极其快速的火灾探测、有意义的扑灭或扑灭，并在多种安装配置中保持稳定的性能。”

But the summary lacks any kind of detailed explanation of which tests were run and under what conditions. It also concludes that “additional testing and optimization are recommended to further expand the range of validated applications,” adding that Sonic Fire Tech’s products have the “potential to complement or, in certain applications, serve as an alternative to traditional suppression systems.” 但摘要中没有详细说明具体进行了哪些测试以及在何种条件下进行。报告还总结道，“建议进行额外的测试和优化，以进一步扩大已验证应用的范围”，并补充说Sonic Fire Tech的产品“有潜力补充传统抑制系统的替代方案，甚至在某些应用中作为替代。”

“Equivalency \[to the 13D standard\] can only be approved by the appropriate authority having jurisdiction and requires technical documentation be submitted demonstrating the equivalency,” Jonathan Hart, NFPA Technical Lead, Fire Protection Technical Resources, emailed Ars.NFPA消防技术资源技术负责人Jonathan Hart在给Ars发邮件时表示：“与13D标准的等效性只能由具有管辖权的相关机构批准，并要求提交技术文件以证明其等效性。”

To date, Sonic Fire Tech has not publicly provided this information.迄今为止，Sonic Fire Tech尚未公开提供这些信息。

Wittasek said that if Sonic Fire Tech is going to claim that its product is as good as or better than the NFPA 13D standard, it should be able to provide a whole range of specifics, such as “who validated it, what test protocols were used, what fire scenarios were included, and how success was defined.” 维塔塞克表示，如果Sonic Fire Tech要声称其产品与NFPA 13D标准一样好甚至更好，就应该能够提供一系列具体信息，比如“谁验证了它，使用了哪些测试协议，包含了哪些火灾场景，以及成功如何定义”。

“I would want to see full-scale testing that includes typical residential fires like furniture and mattress fires, cooking fires, electrical fires, and attic or exterior ember exposures,” he added. “It should also cover different conditions like open and closed doors, varying ceiling heights, crosswinds, obstructed fuel packages, and whether the fire comes back after the system shuts off.” 他补充说：“我希望看到包括典型住宅火灾如家具和床垫火灾、烹饪火灾、电气火灾以及阁楼或外部火星暴露在内的全面测试。”“它还应涵盖不同条件，如开闭门、不同天花板高度、侧风、燃料包阻塞，以及系统关闭后火灾是否复发。”

Similarly, [Michael Gollner](https://me.berkeley.edu/people/michael-gollner/), a professor of mechanical engineering at the University of California, Berkeley, and an expert in fire dynamics, told Ars there’s simply not enough information yet to show that this technology works better than sprinklers.同样，加州大学伯克利分校机械工程教授、火灾动力学专家 [迈克尔·戈尔纳](https://me.berkeley.edu/people/michael-gollner/) 告诉Ars，目前还没有足够的信息表明这项技术比喷淋系统更有效。

He pointed to a [2018 academic paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10694-018-0777-0), which found that “acoustics alone are insufficient to control flames beyond the incipient stage.” 他指出 [2018年的一篇学术论文](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10694-018-0777-0) 指出，“仅靠声学因素不足以控制火焰，超过初期阶段。”

By contrast, “Fire sprinklers are extensively tested and certified by standards developed by the fire safety community over many years,” he emailed Ars. “I think this product needs to demonstrate the same or better performance with the same reliability before it can be considered to replace any existing safety measure. While I am absolutely supportive of out-of-the-box thinking, lives are truly at stake, and new technologies must carefully demonstrate effectiveness and reliability before being entrusted by society.” 相比之下，“消防喷淋器经过了消防安全社区多年来制定的广泛测试和认证，”他通过电子邮件向Ars发邮件。“我认为该产品需要在同等或更优的性能和同样的可靠性上，才能被考虑取代任何现有的安全措施。虽然我完全支持跳出框架思考，但生命确实岌岌可危，新技术必须谨慎展示有效性和可靠性，才能被社会信任。”

## Dozer time 推土机时间

As for the Contra Costa County firefighters who hosted the demonstration, they are curious to see more. Deputy Fire Chief Tracie Dutter told Ars that the agency does not recommend specific products, but it does try to understand the uses that new technology can have.至于举办此次示威的康特拉科斯塔县消防员，他们对更多内容感到好奇。副消防局长特蕾西·达特告诉Ars，该机构并不推荐具体产品，但确实努力了解新技术可能带来的用途。

“Sonic representatives indicated they are exploring opportunities to partner with fire departments to test this technology on a bulldozer,” Dutter said.“Sonic代表表示，他们正在探索与消防部门合作，在推土机上测试这项技术的机会，”达特说。

“The District would be open to testing this system on one of our dozers,” Dutter added, to “better understand its limitations and potential failure points.” “区政府愿意在我们的推土机上测试该系统，”达特补充道，以“更好地理解其局限性和潜在故障点。”

With new tech like this, firefighters also want to understand what “long-term maintenance requirements” it has, whether “routine testing or calibration is required to ensure reliability,” and “how system failures such as a malfunctioning detector or acoustic generator are identified and communicated to an owner.” 有了这样的新技术，消防员还希望了解其“长期维护需求”，是否需要“例行测试或校准以确保可靠性”，以及“如何识别系统故障，如探测器或声学发生器故障，并向业主报告”。
