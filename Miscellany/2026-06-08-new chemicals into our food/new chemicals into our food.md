**How untested chemicals sometimes sneak past the FDA and into our food: Planet Money** **99% of chemicals in our food right now were added without FDA approval. Many were added in secret, through a sneaky loophole built into the 1958 Food Additives Amendment.  
  
It was supposed to require FDA approval for new additives. But food companies and chemical makers found a workaround. And the FDA formally okayed the loophole in the 90s — in the process bringing attention to a loophole *to* the loophole.  
  
The FDA has essentially admitted it doesn’t have the capacity to verify the safety of new food chemicals. So they leave it up to food companies and chemical makers to declare their brand new chemicals are safe. These chemicals are used in everything from chocolate and smoked fish, to tea bags, protein drinks, popcorn, and seeds.  
  
So, how’d the loophole get there, and what does it tell us about the priority the U.S. places on safety versus speed and innovation? And, how much can one lawyer do about it?

99% of chemicals in our food right now were added without FDA approval. Many were added in secret, through a sneaky loophole built into the 1958 Food Additives Amendment.目前我们食物中99%的化学品都是在未获得美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）批准的情况下添加的。许多化学品是通过1958年《食品添加剂修正案》中暗藏的漏洞被偷偷添加进去的。

It was supposed to require FDA approval for new additives. But food companies and chemical makers found a workaround. And the FDA formally okayed the loophole in the 90s — in the process bringing attention to a loophole *to* the loophole.新添加剂本应需要获得美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）的批准。但食品公司和化学品制造商找到了规避方法。而美国食品药品监督管理局在90年代正式认可了这一漏洞——在此过程中，人们也注意到了这一漏洞背后的\*\*另一个漏洞\*\*。

The FDA has essentially admitted it doesn't have the capacity to verify the safety of new food chemicals. So they leave it up to food companies and chemical makers to declare their brand new chemicals are safe. These chemicals are used in everything from chocolate and smoked fish, to tea bags, protein drinks, popcorn, and seeds.美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）实际上已经承认，其没有能力验证新食品化学品的安全性。因此，他们将这一工作交由食品企业和化学品制造商来宣称其全新的化学品是安全的。这些化学品被应用于各类产品中，从巧克力、熏鱼，到茶包、蛋白饮料、爆米花和种子。
